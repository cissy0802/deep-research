# 01 Round 1 原始论断(7 线合并)


# 线 US

## U01 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 设计：1,000 名 21–40 岁、2019 年家庭收入 ≤300% FPL 的低收入者每月 $1,000 无条件发 3 年，对照 2,000 人每月 $50；样本来自伊利诺伊北部（含芝加哥）与得州北部，按 2019 年基线家庭收入约 $29,900 算相当于家庭收入 +40%。
- 原文:Individuals between the ages of 21 and 40 whose total household income did not exceed 300% of the Federal Poverty Level (FPL) in 2019 were eligible to participate, with most of the sample falling below 100% or 200% of the FPL. Participants reported an average household income of about $29,900 in 2019, so the transfers represented a 40% increase in household income.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/w32719.pdf
- 来源:Vivalt, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Miller, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), The Employment Effects of a Guaranteed Income: Experimental Evidence from Two U.S. States, NBER w32719 / QJE 141(4):2675-2734 (2026) · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:局部样本、低收入定向、有期限（3 年，2020-11 至 2023-10，覆盖疫情后期与工资快速上涨期）。对照组每月也拿 $50（不是零）。预注册 AEARCTR-0006750（文中脚注 1，偏离 PAP 处见附录 G）。正式发表于 QJE 2026（NBER 页面 Published Versions 列出）。

## U02 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 劳动供给（最终版）：转移使不含转移的个人收入每年下降约 $1,900，劳动参与率下降 4.2 个百分点（UI 行政数据+调查合并口径），每周工时减少 1–2 小时；伴侣工时减少幅度相当。
- 原文:The transfer caused total individual income excluding the transfers to fall by about $1,900/year relative to the control group and a 4.2 percentage point decrease in labor market participation. Participants reduced their work hours as a result of the transfers by 1-2 hours/week and participants’ partners reduced their work hours by a comparable amount.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/w32719.pdf
- 来源:Vivalt, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Miller, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), The Employment Effects of a Guaranteed Income: Experimental Evidence from Two U.S. States, NBER w32719 / QJE 141(4):2675-2734 (2026) · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:效应量相对对照均值约 5–6%，作者称『moderate』。弹性：参与弹性 ηe=-0.20，无条件工时弹性 ηi=-0.16，条件工时弹性 -0.08。资助：NSF #2149344 + 'private donors'。 【并入】ORUS 就业质量与主观幸福感：尽管详细询问工作福利，未发现就业质量改善且可排除小幅改善；主观幸福感第一年较高、之后回到对照水平。 原文："Despite asking detailed questions about amenities, we find no impact on quality of employment, and our confidence intervals can rule out even small improvements. ... Measures of subjective well-being are higher among treated participants in the first year of the transfers but then revert to control group levels."（https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/w32719.pdf）

## U03 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 不同数据口径给出不同就业效应：调查口径就业 -2.3pp（SE 1.2），UI 行政数据单独 -6.6pp（SE 2.0），合并口径 -4.2pp（SE 1.7）；调查工时 -1.3 小时/周（SE 0.6）。
- 原文:Treated participants appear 2.3 (s.e. 1.2) percentage points less likely to be employed in the survey data (row 2). Similar to how we saw larger impacts on income in the UI data compared to survey data, we estimate a larger effect on labor supply using the UI data: a 6.6 (s.e. 2.0) percentage point decrease in employment using the UI data alone (row 3) and a 4.2 (s.e. 1.7) percentage point decrease in employment using the pooled UI and survey data (row 4).
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/w32719.pdf
- 来源:Vivalt, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Miller, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), The Employment Effects of a Guaranteed Income: Experimental Evidence from Two U.S. States, NBER w32719 / QJE 141(4):2675-2734 (2026) · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:自报 vs 行政数据口径差 3 倍。UI 数据不含合同工/临时工/零工，可能高估退出；调查口径可能低估。行政数据链接同意率 87.5%，匹配率 IL 71%、TX 73%。引用 ORUS 就业效应时必须说明是哪个口径。

## U04 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 就业效应数字随版本上调：2024-07 初版摘要为劳动参与 -2.0pp、工时 -1.3~1.4 小时/周、个人收入 -$1,500/年；2025-01 版为 -3.9pp；2026-08 最终版为 -4.2pp、-$1,900/年。
- 原文:The transfer caused total individual income to fall by about $1,500/year relative to the control group, excluding the transfers. The program resulted in a 2.0 percentage point decrease in labor market participation for participants and a 1.3-1.4 hour per week reduction in labor hours, with participants’ partners reducing their hours worked by a comparable amount.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/revisions/w32719.rev0.pdf
- 来源:Vivalt et al., 2024-07 (初版), NBER w32719 rev0 · 2024-07 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:媒体与二手综述大多仍引 2024 初版的 2.0pp；2025-01 修订版（rev1）摘要为 '3.9 percentage point decrease in labor market participation'。写作时应引最终 QJE 版 4.2pp，并注明变化来自口径（加入/合并 UI 行政数据）。

## U05 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 家庭层面：参与者+伴侣合计每周工时减少 2.1 小时（SE 0.8），全体家庭成员减少 2.4 小时（SE 0.9），约为对照均值的 4.9–5.2%；但伴侣/家庭成员结果非预注册，经 FDR 校正后不显著。
- 原文:we estimate a reduction of 2.1 (s.e. 0.8) in total hours per week worked by the participant and their partner (row 5 of Table IV), and 2.4 (s.e. 0.9) hours per week when we include all household members (row 6). These estimates represent a 4.9-5.2% decrease relative to the control mean
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/w32719.pdf
- 来源:Vivalt, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Miller, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), The Employment Effects of a Guaranteed Income: Experimental Evidence from Two U.S. States, NBER w32719 / QJE 141(4):2675-2734 (2026) · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:原文紧接着承认：'The estimates for the work hours of others in the household were not pre-specified and are subjected to a large penalty in the FDR corrections, but they are significant before those corrections are applied.' 伴侣效应是探索性结果。

## U06 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 时间使用：手机 App 时间日记显示每周工作减少约 1.4 小时，释放的时间主要流向休闲、非通勤交通及其他活动；对求职、培训、照护、自我提升等『生产性』用途可排除哪怕很小的变化。
- 原文:The extra time participants have from reduced work is used largely for leisure, non-commuting transportation, and other activities (Figure V).
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/w32719.pdf
- 来源:Vivalt, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Miller, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), The Employment Effects of a Guaranteed Income: Experimental Evidence from Two U.S. States, NBER w32719 / QJE 141(4):2675-2734 (2026) · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked

## U07 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 自报残疾上升：处理组自报任何残疾的概率高 4.3pp（对照基数 31%），自报限制工作的健康问题/残疾高 4.2pp（对照基数 28%）；作者给出『获得更多医疗而被诊断』与『自我标签以减轻不就业污名』两种解释。
- 原文:We find a significant 4.3 percentage point increase in the likelihood of self-reporting any disability (on a base of 31 percent in the control group) and a 4.2 percentage point increase in the probability of reporting a health problem or disability that limits the work they can do (on a base of 28 percent in the control group)
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w32719/w32719.pdf
- 来源:Vivalt, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Miller, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), The Employment Effects of a Guaranteed Income: Experimental Evidence from Two U.S. States, NBER w32719 / QJE 141(4):2675-2734 (2026) · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:与健康论文『客观生物标志物无变化』并读：自报健康变差而客观指标不变，提示自报口径受就业状态反向影响（self-signaling）。

## U08 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 健康：压力与食物安全大幅但短暂改善；医院/急诊/牙科使用上升、医疗支出约 +$20/月；自报身体健康、血检生物标志物无改善，精神健康第一年后无改善，可排除小效应；家中儿童健康也无效应。
- 原文:The transfer produced large but short-lived improvements in stress and food security and increased hospital, emergency department, and dental care use. However, it did not improve self-reported physical health, clinical biomarkers, or mental health after the first year, and we can rule out small effects on many outcomes. We also find no effects on the health of children in participants’ households.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w32711
- 来源:Miller, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Vivalt, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), Does Income Affect Health? Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial of a Guaranteed Income, NBER w32711 · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:2024-07 初版（https://public.websites.umich.edu/~mille/ORUS_Health.pdf）原话：'the transfer did not improve mental health after the first year and by year 2 we can again reject very small improvements'。即精神健康效应在第二年消失。2026-08 修订版致谢含 'Erzo Luttmer and four anonymous referees'，显示已进入期刊审稿，但 NBER 页未列已发表版本。资助：NIH 1R01HD108716-01A1 + 私人资金。生物标志物在 Census FSRDC 处理。

## U09 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 政治：$1,000/月 × 3 年对政治参与、投入、偏好、政府信任、极化、民主支持均无影响，可排除与收入-投票率观测关联同量级的效应；处理组反而更倾向认为工作对个人/社会重要、甚至应作为领取政府项目的条件。
- 原文:Contrary to resource models of participation, we find no effects on political participation or engagement, and rule out effects equivalent to the observational association between turnout and income. ... There is also some evidence that treated participants saw work as more important for individuals, society, or even as a requirement for accessing government programs
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w33214
- 来源:Broockman, Rhodes, Bartik, Dotson, Miller, Krause, Vivalt, 2024, The Causal Effects of Income on Political Attitudes and Behavior: A Randomized Field Experiment, NBER w33214 · 2024-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:与本期『公民身份/政治议价权』直接相关：私人慈善、免税、有期限的赠款不同于政府发放的权利型转移，作者自己指出结果 'contrast with findings from other economic shocks such as government-sponsored or taxable transfers'。外推到全民、政府、永久 UBI 的政治效应须谨慎。

- 原文:We’d like to fund a study on basic income—i.e., giving people enough money to live on with no strings attached. ... I’m fairly confident that at some point in the future, as technology continues to eliminate traditional jobs and massive new wealth gets created, we're going to see some version of this at a national scale.

## U12 (US)
- 论断:Stockton SEED：131 人处理组每月 $500、200 人对照，2019-02 至 2021-01 共 24 个月；处理组收入波动更低、精神困扰更低、精力与身体功能更好。
- 原文:A mixed-methods randomized controlled trial in Stockton, CA, USA enrolled 131 individuals to the treatment condition and 200 to control to receive a guaranteed income from February 2019 to January 2021. ... The treatment condition reported lower rates of income volatility than control, lower mental distress, better energy and physical functioning
- URL:https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10160253
- 来源:West & Castro, 2023, Impact of Guaranteed Income on Health, Finances, and Agency: Findings from the Stockton Randomized Controlled Trial, Journal of Urban Health 100:227–244 (doi:10.1007/s11524-023-00723-0) · 2023-05 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked

## U18 (US)
- 论断:Compton Pledge（RCT，学术独立评估）：695 户随机获得平均约 $500/月 × 2 年，1,402 户对照（不拿钱）；全职者劳动供给无变化，但基线兼职者劳动参与下降 13pp；不含转移收入每月少 $333；住房安全改善，但心理与财务福祉指数总体无效应。

- Mahdavy 1970 原章节（Cook ed., Studies in the Economic History of the Middle East, OUP）未取得扫描，定义仅经 1971 年德文书评转引。
- Andersen & Ross 2014 CPS 正文与断点估计系数未取得（SAGE 403、CORE 拦截），只有摘要片段；需核对 post-1980 效应量与 Haber-Menaldo 数据重估细节。
- Ross 2012《The Oil Curse》书中核心估计（人均油气收入口径下民主转型概率、p.74『no country with high levels of oil and gas income successfully became a democracy between 1960 and 2010』）只见二手转引，未取得书页；本线用的是作者演讲转写。
- Tilly 1990《Coercion, Capital, and European States》战争—征税—议价论证未取得原文，未收；Herb 2005 Comparative Politics『No Representation without Taxation?』摘要/正文未取得（作者站只有数据 codebook）。
- Lucy Martin 2023《Strategic Taxation》(OUP) 原文与出版社简介未取得（目录站 403），仅有书评转述：统治者会策略性地少征税以避免问责需求。
- Arab Barometer 海湾国家原始交叉表（福利获得 vs 政治参与/民主需求）未直接拉取；Hertog 2020 用的是 GCC 多源调查。Eibl & Hertog 2023（威胁→福利，阿曼 vs 赤道几内亚）原文未读，仅经 Intelligence Curse 转引。
- AI 租金 vs 石油租金类比的不成立处缺少一手实证：(1) 所有权——石油多为国有（1970 年代国有化后），AI 租金目前在私营公司且跨国；(2) 可分配性——Alaska/挪威式基金证明资源租金可直接按公民身份分配；(3) 可迁移性——油田不能搬走，AI 公司与算力可迁移、税基可竞争（Intelligence Curse 自己也提到国家间竞争会压低 AI 税）。目前文献里没有检验这三点的研究，属论证空白。
- 挪威：除 Mehlum 等的增长口径与 Intelligence Curse 转引 Steinar Holden 外，缺挪威石油基金对政治参与/议价权影响的一手实证；博茨瓦纳同样只有增长口径。
