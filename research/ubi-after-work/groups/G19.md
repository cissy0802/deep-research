# G19 税收-问责实验、动员与选举权、阿拉斯加政治、海湾调查

## R14 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Paler 2013 印尼 Blora 县田野实验（n=1,863）：税收处理组相比意外之财组，监督与反现任政治行动更多；但给出支出信息后，意外之财组对被滥用收入的在意程度与税收组相同。
- 原文:the tax treatment increased monitoring and anti-incumbent political action. Yet, when given spending information, citizens in the windfall treatment cared just as much about misused revenue as those in the tax treatment.
- URL:https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/abs/keeping-the-public-purse-an-experiment-in-windfalls-taxes-and-the-incentives-to-restrain-government/1015EC29926C5ADD82C14FFECC582A4D
- 来源:Laura Paler, 2013, Keeping the Public Purse: An Experiment in Windfalls, Taxes, and the Incentives to Restrain Government, APSR 107(4):706-725 · 2013-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:处理是『信息框架』：告诉被试预算来自税还是来自资源——不是真的征税或真发钱；结果变量为调查+明信片行动。含义：意外之财的『麻痹效应』部分是信息问题，可被透明度抵消。数据 DOI 10.7910/DVN/QWXPUA。正文未读。

## R15 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Weigel 2020 刚果 Kananga 市 356 个街区随机征收房产税：纳税率从对照 0.1% 升到 11.6%；政治参与（出席市政会/提交政府评价）提高约 5 个百分点（+31%），参与者平均承担相当于一天家庭收入的成本。
- 原文:It also increased political participation by about 5 percentage points (31%): citizens in taxed neighborhoods were more likely to attend town hall meetings hosted by the government or submit evaluations of its performance.
- URL:https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/104561/
- 来源:Jonathan L. Weigel, 2020, The Participation Dividend of Taxation: How Citizens in Congo Engage More with the State When It Tries to Tax Them, QJE 135(4):1849-1903 · 2020-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:预注册 AEARCTR-0001316；资助 Fulbright、Weiss Family Program Fund；与 Kasaï Central 省政府合作（政府是实施方）。处理是『征税努力』（登记+上门），不是收入来源对比，也不是撤销税收——不能直接外推到『不征税会降低参与』的对称反面。参与形式由政府组织，属低风险参与，不是选举或抗议。

## R16 (RENTIER)
- 论断:de la Cuesta, Martin, Milner & Nielson 2022：乌干达、加纳 lab-in-the-field + 乌干达全国代表性调查实验——问责压力来自对公共资金的『所有权感』；简单干预就能让人对石油和援助收入产生所有权感，问责需求与税收无法区分。
- 原文:simple interventions can significantly increase feelings of revenue ownership over oil and aid windfalls, producing demands for accountability indistinguishable from taxes.
- URL:https://collaborate.princeton.edu/en/publications/owning-it-accountability-and-citizens-ownership-over-oil-aid-and-/
- 来源:Brandon de la Cuesta, Lucy Martin, Helen V. Milner & Daniel L. Nielson, 2022, Owning It: Accountability and Citizens' Ownership over Oil, Aid, and Taxes, Journal of Politics 84(1):304-320 · 2022-01 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:机制从『纳税』改写为『所有权感』——对 AI 租金类比关键：若公民被设定为 AI 红利的共同所有者（如社会财富基金），问责需求未必消失。局限：结果多为态度/实验室惩罚行为，非真实选举。Lucy Martin 2023 专著《Strategic Taxation》(OUP) 同线索，未取得原文。

## R17 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Acemoglu & Robinson 2000（形式模型+历史叙述）：19 世纪西方扩大选举权是精英为防止社会动乱和革命的策略性决定；选举权是对未来再分配的承诺装置。
- 原文:We argue that these political reforms can be viewed as strategic decisions by the political elite to prevent widespread social unrest and revolution.
- URL:https://cpi.stanford.edu/_media/pdf/Reference%20Media/Acemoglu%20and%20Robinson_2000_History%20of%20Inequality.pdf
- 来源:Daron Acemoglu & James A. Robinson, 2000, Why Did the West Extend the Franchise? Democracy, Inequality, and Growth in Historical Perspective, QJE 115(4):1167-1199 · 2000-11 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:论证型（博弈模型 + 英法德瑞典案例叙述），非计量检验。机制依赖『被排斥者有可信的集体行动/革命威胁』，而这种威胁的底座是劳动力与兵源——AI 版论证正是押注这个底座被替代（见 Intelligence Curse 对无人机/监控的讨论）。

## R18 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Przeworski 2009（全球数据集，从代议制起点至今的统计检验）：阶级维度的选举权扩展是被底层『打出来的』，对革命威胁的回应；女性选举权则更多出于政党的选举竞争考虑。
- 原文:The general picture that emerges is that the poorer classes fought their way into the representative institutions and, once admitted, they were organized by different political parties.
- URL:https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/british-journal-of-political-science/article/conquered-or-granted-a-history-of-suffrage-extensions/A4E9E4C21CA70A0603744957F941A78A
- 来源:Adam Przeworski, 2009, Conquered or Granted? A History of Suffrage Extensions, BJPolS 39(2):291-321 · 2009-04 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:计量检验型（以罢工/骚乱等作威胁代理）。支持 A&R 的『威胁→让渡』，但同时给出反例机制：女性选举权不是靠威胁获得的——说明议价权不只来自经济/暴力杠杆，政党竞争本身也能扩权。

## R19 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Scheve & Stasavage 2010：过去一个世纪，大规模战争动员与税收累进性显著上升相关；在无战争情况下，普选权确立或左翼执政都不系统地带来累进性大幅提高（一战参战国 vs 非参战国 DiD）。
- 原文:In the absence of war, neither the establishment of universal suffrage, nor the arrival of political control by parties of the left is systematically associated with large increases in tax progressivity.
- URL:https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/international-organization/article/conscription-of-wealth-mass-warfare-and-the-demand-for-progressive-taxation/14C10D9E21A194E7AC57971043C4EB0C
- 来源:Kenneth Scheve & David Stasavage, 2010, The Conscription of Wealth: Mass Warfare and the Demand for Progressive Taxation, International Organization 64(4):529-561 · 2010-10 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:机制是『公平补偿』（穷人出命、富人出钱），不是革命威胁。对本题：兵役是公民议价筹码的独立来源；若军队自动化，这一筹码与劳动筹码同时消失。样本为富裕民主国家为主，DiD 依赖一战参战的外生性假设。

## R20 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Scheve & Stasavage 2012：1816–2000 遗产税数据集，DiD 下「普选权假说」证据很少，「大规模动员假说」证据很强。
- 原文:Using a difference-in-differences framework for identification, we find little evidence for the suffrage hypothesis but very strong evidence for the mass mobilization hypothesis.
- URL:https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/democracy-war-and-wealth-lessons-from-two-centuries-of-inheritance-taxation/9AE36F339225AA03F2E2A516E16CC2AB
- 来源:Kenneth Scheve & David Stasavage, 2012, Democracy, War, and Wealth: Lessons from Two Centuries of Inheritance Taxation, APSR 106(1) · 2012-02 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:含义双刃：一方面『有投票权≠能拿到再分配』（选票本身杠杆弱）；另一方面说明再分配历史上靠的是『国家需要你的身体』。Tilly 1990《Coercion, Capital, and European States》的战争-征税-谈判论证本次未取得原文（见 open_questions）。

## R21 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Alaska PFD（资源租金→全民无条件分红）反向证据一：Loeffler 2022 DiD（1978–2000 年 11 月大选）发现分红引入使州投票率显著上升，且持续近 20 年；1982 年首次发放后投票率基线模型 +8.3pp（全模型 +18.2pp）。
- 原文:Differences-in-differences estimates covering November general elections from 1978 to 2000 provide compelling evidence that the Alaskan UBI has a significant positive effect on turnout. The results further suggest that the turnout increase was not a one-off effect but persists over a period of almost 20 years.
- URL:https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/11D46D5315AEB1C6D01D88D4DB1A8647/core-reader
- 来源:Hannah Loeffler, 2022, Does a universal basic income affect voter turnout? Evidence from Alaska, Political Science Research and Methods · 2022-12 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:只有一个处理单位（阿拉斯加州），DiD 对照为其他州，+18.2pp 量级偏大应谨慎；1982 年也有其他州级事件（分红本身曾成选举议题）。这直接挑战『租金→去政治化』：当租金以公民身份直接发给个人（而非经国家分配），参与反而上升。

## R22 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Alaska PFD 反向证据二：James, Rivera & Smith 2025 用每年分红额变化（选举前约 1 个月到账）识别——州长选举中现金增加 10%（约 $190）使投票率上升 1.4pp，效应集中于年轻人和穷人，机制是降低政治冷漠。
- 原文:We find that larger transfers cause people to vote, especially in gubernatorial elections in which a 10% increase in cash ($190) causes a 1.4 percentage point increase in turnout. Effects are concentrated among the young and poor.
- URL:https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:ecinqu:v:63:y:2025:i:3:p:805-829
- 来源:Alexander James, Nathalia Rivera & Brock Smith, 2025, (Alaska PFD and voter turnout), Economic Inquiry 63(3):805-829 · 2025-07 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:工作论文版（2022, U. Alaska Anchorage WP 2022-01）写作 $182，正式版 $190——引用时用正式版。与 Loeffler 机制不同（冷漠 vs 资源）。两篇都只测投票率，不测议价权/政策回应性：投票更多 ≠ 国家更需要你。

## R23 (RENTIER)
- 论断:海湾调查实证（Hertog 2020）：GCC 公民在具体人生选择上确有「食利心态」，但大比例声称支持努力、竞争和小政府；政治兴趣「异常高」但伴随高忠诚和对政府高信任——『政治冷漠』预测不成立。
- 原文:When it comes to politics, levels of interest are unusually high, but are coupled with high levels of loyalty and confidence in government.
- URL:https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/103042
- 来源:Steffen Hertog, 2020, The 'rentier mentality', 30 years on: evidence from survey data, British Journal of Middle Eastern Studies · 2020-01 · 【研究】 · 调查 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:用多个 GCC 调查（含此前未发表的沙特公民劳动态度调查）；作者解释为国家自 1980 年代以来用社会工程灌输亲商与爱国观念。威权语境下的『信任/忠诚』自报有社会期望偏差。Arab Barometer 原始交叉表未直接取得。
