# G06 肯尼亚 GiveDirectly 长期 UBI 与一般均衡

## I06 (INTL)
- 论断:肯尼亚 GiveDirectly UBI：295 个村随机分为对照 100 村、长期 UBI 44 村（每名成年人 $0.75/天名义，$1.88 PPP，按月发 12 年）、短期 UBI 80 村（同额 2 年）、一次性 71 村（约 $500 名义/$1,250 PPP）。
- 原文:Long-term UBI: in 44 villages (approximately 5,000 people) each adult over the age of 18 receives US $0.75 nominal per day, or $1.88 PPP, delivered in monthly installments for 12 years.
- URL:https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f192616.pdf
- 来源:Banerjee, Faye, Krueger, Niehaus, Suri, 2023-09, Universal Basic Income: Short-Term Results from a Long-Term Experiment in Kenya, 工作论文（NBER 会议稿/J-PAL WP4795；非 NBER w31490） · 2023-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:村级「全民」（村内所有成年人），但不是全国全民；12 年仍有期限，只是期限长。注意 brief 中 NBER w31490 编号有误：w31490 是 Baron 等的儿童保护论文；该文未查到 NBER WP 号，J-PAL 托管为 WP4795。资助：Gates 基金会、RWJF、匿名捐赠者；Faye、Niehaus 无偿任 GiveDirectly 董事（利益关联）。

## I07 (INTL)
- 论断:肯尼亚两年结果：家庭总劳动供给无净变化——农业雇佣劳动小时显著减少，非农自营小时略多增加；作者称「无证据显示 UBI 助长懒惰」。
- 原文:there is a significant reduction in hours of wage work, all of which comes from work in agriculture, and a slightly larger increase in hours of non-agricultural self-employed work, so there is no net effect on total household labor supply. Overall there is no evidence of UBI promoting “laziness,” but evidence of substantial effects on occupational choice.
- URL:https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f192616.pdf
- 来源:Banerjee, Faye, Krueger, Niehaus, Suri, 2023-09, Universal Basic Income: Short-Term Results from a Long-Term Experiment in Kenya, 工作论文（NBER 会议稿/J-PAL WP4795；非 NBER w31490） · 2023-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:劳动数据为调查自报。两年时点长期臂与短期臂收到的钱一样多，长期臂的「承诺 12 年」效应主要体现在预期上。

## I08 (INTL)
- 论断:肯尼亚：雇佣劳动减少后村内工资与公共工程保留工资上升——受助者的退出抬高了劳动价格（议价权含义）。
- 原文:We also find no effect on equilibrium wage employment, suggesting that wage workers from outside the village took up the slack. This should imply that wages went up, and indeed both reported wages in agriculture and construction (the representative sector used for non-agriculture) as well as reservation wages to work on a public works project increased.
- URL:https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f192616.pdf
- 来源:Banerjee, Faye, Krueger, Niehaus, Suri, 2023-09, Universal Basic Income: Short-Term Results from a Long-Term Experiment in Kenya, 工作论文（NBER 会议稿/J-PAL WP4795；非 NBER w31490） · 2023-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:与「被供养后失去劳动议价权」叙事相反：在此情境下现金提高了保留工资。但这是村级局部均衡、外来工补位，不能外推到全国。

## I09 (INTL)
- 论断:肯尼亚：预注册 10 组汇总结果（AEA 注册 1952）；短期臂效果明显小于一次性与长期臂，福利改善有的有（食物消费、抑郁）有的无（儿童体格、教育）。
- 原文:Household well-being improved on some common measures (e.g. food consumption, depression) but not others (e.g. children’s anthropometrics and schooling).
- URL:https://conference.nber.org/conf_papers/f192616.pdf
- 来源:Banerjee, Faye, Krueger, Niehaus, Suri, 2023-09, Universal Basic Income: Short-Term Results from a Long-Term Experiment in Kenya, 工作论文（NBER 会议稿/J-PAL WP4795；非 NBER w31490） · 2023-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:预注册：https://www.socialscienceregistry.org/trials/1952。截至 2026-11 未找到正式发表版或 5 年以上结果的一手论文（只有二手报道提及长期臂通过 ROSCA 把小额转为整笔），见 open_questions。

## I10 (INTL)
- 论断:一般均衡：约 $1,000 一次性转移给 653 个随机村中 10,500+ 户，冲击超当地 GDP 15%；正式发表版估计局部转移乘数 2.5，非受助户与企业有正溢出、物价几乎不涨。
- 原文:We find large impacts on consumption and assets for recipients. Importantly, we document large positive spillovers on non-recipient households and firms, and minimal price inflation. We estimate a local transfer multiplier of 2.5.
- URL:https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10445626
- 来源:Egger, Haushofer, Miguel, Niehaus, Walker, 2022, General Equilibrium Effects of Cash Transfers: Experimental Evidence from Kenya, Econometrica 90(6):2603–43 · 2022-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:一次性、定向贫困户（茅草屋顶标准）、低收入国家，非 UBI。乘数口径因版本而异：NBER 2021 修订版摘要为 2.4（支出法 2.5、收入法 2.3），Econometrica 正文支出法 2.6、摘要 2.5——引用时须标版本。AEARCTR-0000505；Niehaus 为 GiveDirectly 联合创始人。

## I11 (INTL)
- 论断:一般均衡：平均物价涨幅 0.1%，转移最集中时价格效应也<1%；乘数在 90% 置信下拒绝 ≤1。
- 原文:Average price inflation is 0.1%, and even during periods with the largest transfers, estimated price effects are less than 1% and precisely estimated across all categories of goods.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w26600/w26600.pdf
- 来源:Egger et al., 2019/2021 revised, NBER w26600 · 2021-10 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:作者自述存在生产端「slack」才可能有大乘数——在满负荷经济或全民永久项目中不能假定同样结果。乘数 90% 单侧检验，非 95%。

## V14 (VOICES)
- 论断:【倡导方】GiveDirectly 肯尼亚 UBI 研究官网自述为「$30M 项目」、约 2.3 万人/195 村、长期臂 $22.50/月×12 年,并称转移「让村里每个人脱离(货币)贫困」;其政策倡导页把「一次性大额现金」列为优先,没有关于 UBI/保障收入的政策立场;官网不列资助方。
- 原文:As part of this $30M project, we have distributed millions of dollars to ~23,000 individuals living across 195 villages. ... These transfers lift everyone in a village out of (monetary) poverty. | Position large, one-time cash transfers as a core tool to reduce extreme poverty.
- URL:https://www.givedirectly.org/ubi/ ; https://www.givedirectly.org/policy-and-advocacy
- 来源:GiveDirectly, UBI 研究页与 Policy & Advocacy 页 · 2026-11 · 【厂商】 · 表态 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:同页 FAQ 写的是 2 万人/197 村,与正文不一致。「lift everyone out of poverty」是机械算术(把月转移加到收入上),不是实测福利效应。资助方:官网/财务页不点名;二手资料只确认 Omidyar Network 2017 年 $493,000;GiveWell/Open Philanthropy 对 GiveDirectly 的一般捐助未核到 UBI 专项。注意倡导重点是「一次性大额」而非「终身月付」——GiveDirectly 自己的政策优先与「UBI」标签并不重合。
