# G03 美国市级试点、AEI 汇总与 1970s 负所得税实验

## U18 (US)
- 论断:Compton Pledge（RCT，学术独立评估）：695 户随机获得平均约 $500/月 × 2 年，1,402 户对照（不拿钱）；全职者劳动供给无变化，但基线兼职者劳动参与下降 13pp；不含转移收入每月少 $333；住房安全改善，但心理与财务福祉指数总体无效应。
- 原文:Receiving guaranteed income had no impact on the labor supply of full-time workers, but part-time workers (at baseline) had lower labor market participation by 13 percentage points. Income (excluding the transfer) was reduced by $333 per month on average relative to control households, and expenditures were reduced by $302 per month. ... We find a significant improvement in housing security, but no overall effects on indices of psychological and financial well-being.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w33209
- 来源:Balakrishnan, Chan, Constantino, Haushofer, Morduch, 2024, Household Responses to Guaranteed Income: Experimental Evidence from Compton, California, NBER w33209 · 2024-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:2021–2023 疫情期；18 个月后调查 1,074 人，IEL 称响应率 51%（无差异性流失）。自报数据为主。项目由 Jain Family Institute 参与设计；学术顾问含 Evelyn Forget、Greg Duncan。

## U19 (US)
- 论断:Denver Basic Income Project（面向无家可归者，RCT 三臂：A $1,000/月、B $6,500 一次性+$500/月、C $50/月作对照）：官方年报宣称『所有组』住房显著改善——即包括只拿 $50 的对照组，说明报告的是组内前后变化而非组间处理效应。
- 原文:All payment groups showed significant improvements in housing outcomes, including a remarkable increase in home rent and ownership, and decrease in nights spent unsheltered.
- URL:https://static1.squarespace.com/static/64f507a995b636019ef8853a/t/6671a2285cf8513e1383ed9d/1718723132992/FINAL_DBIP+Year+One+Executive+Summary.pdf
- 来源:Denver Basic Income Project, 2024, Year One Research Report Executive Summary (research led by University of Denver Center for Housing and Homelessness Research) · 2024-06 · 【官方】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:最典型的『对照组也拿钱+组内前后比』陷阱：组间差异微小（媒体转述 10 个月时稳定住房 A/B 48% vs C 46%，secondhand）。报告称研究由丹佛大学『independently』主导，但执行摘要由项目方发布、措辞为倡导式（'incredibly promising'）。自报为主，调查响应率随时间下降（媒体称 10 个月约 60%，secondhand）。

## U20 (US)
- 论断:UChicago Inclusive Economy Lab（受 Cook County 委托评估 Cook County Promise：$500/月 × 2 年、3,250 人、ARPA 出资 $4,200 万）在汇总中只把 ORUS、BFY、Compton 列为『最严格』研究，并直言倡导者常只挑积极结果；Cook County 本身的因果评估结果尚未在其页面发布。
- 原文:While advocates often highlight the more positive or exciting findings, it is critical to understand the full set of results and learn from pilots that find no impact or had unexpected results in order to better inform the field and policy.
- URL:https://urbanlabs.uchicago.edu/attachments/40baa6d7e49551dc92e9a4204af9cb10dbc566f9/store/596a5de8256dd10415e8c3125c8cd78d670908a4ed0672f3d0c824a3c293/Policymakers%27+Report.pdf
- 来源:University of Chicago Inclusive Economy Lab, 2025, Policymakers' Report (Cook County Promise Guaranteed Income Pilot) · 2025-10 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:筛选标准：RCT、预注册、样本足够、无差异性流失、2020 后发表。Cook County Promise 是抽签 RCT（23 万+申请抢 3,250 名额），但评估方受县政府委托、评估资金来自 Kellogg、Rockefeller、J-PAL 等，结果尚未出。日期 2025-10 依二手检索结果，PDF 内无明确日期。

## U21 (US)
- 论断:1968–82 四个负所得税实验（NJ、Rural、Gary、SIME/DIME）汇总：丈夫（主要收入者）报告工时减少约 7%，妻子与单亲女户主减少约 17%；减少主要来自就业间隔变长、失业期变长与退出劳动力，而非在职者缩短周工时。
- 原文:Husbands reduced their reported work effort by approximately 7 percent, while wives and female heads reduced reported hours by 17 percent.
- URL:https://www.bostonfed.org/economic/conf/conf30/conf30b.pdf
- 来源:Gary Burtless, 1986, The Work Response to a Guaranteed Income: A Survey of Experimental Evidence, in Munnell (ed.), Lessons from the Income Maintenance Experiments, FRB Boston Conference Series No. 30 · 1986-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这是『reported』工时，样本加权（夫妻：NJ 0.20、Rural 0.07、Gary 0.17、SIME/DIME 0.56）。各实验保障水平为贫困线 50%–135%，边际税率 30%–70%，与今天的 UBI 无退坡设计不同。资助：联邦 HEW/OEO。

## U22 (US)
- 论断:NIT 自报偏差与期限效应双向：Gary 与 SIME/DIME 中，用 UI 行政数据核对后，妻子（次要收入者）的收入减少『几乎消失』；但 SIME/DIME 五年组的反应约为三年组两倍（丈夫工时 -13%，妻子 -27%，单亲母亲 -32%），说明有期限实验会低估永久方案效应。
- 原文:The earnings reduction of wives, for example, virtually disappears when the response estimate is based on presumably accurate data from the unemployment insurance system.
- URL:https://www.bostonfed.org/economic/conf/conf30/conf30b.pdf
- 来源:Gary Burtless, 1986, The Work Response to a Guaranteed Income: A Survey of Experimental Evidence, in Munnell (ed.), Lessons from the Income Maintenance Experiments, FRB Boston Conference Series No. 30 · 1986-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:同文：'The bias in the Gary experiment was large enough so that the entire earnings response and much of the apparent employment effect of the experiment disappeared'；以及 'the sluggishness of the labor supply response and the attenuated response to a shorter duration plan cause the long-term impact of a permanent negative income tax to be substantially understated'。注意 Burtless 也指出临时实验是『a sale on leisure』可能反向夸大。与 ORUS『行政数据效应更大』方向相反，口径陷阱不能一概而论。

## U23 (US)
- 论断:SIME/DIME 离婚效应被重估：Hannan、Tuma、Groeneveld 1977 年起报告 NIT 增加婚姻解体；Cain & Wissoker 重新分析（区分纯 NIT 与培训处理、用全时段、处理流失与复合）后结论是对婚姻解体率无影响。
- 原文:The conclusion of this paper is that the plans (specifically, the negative income tax plans in the experiment) had no effect on the rate of marital dissolutions among the "treatment" couples relative to the control couples. The control couples were eligible for the existing program, Aid to Families with Dependent Children.
- URL:https://www.irp.wisc.edu/publications/dps/pdfs/dp85788.pdf
- 来源:Glen G. Cain & Douglas A. Wissoker, 1988, A Reanalysis of Marital Stability in the Seattle-Denver Income Maintenance Experiment, IRP Discussion Paper 857-88 (published AJS 95(5), 1990) · 1988-01 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:引语取自 IRP 工作论文版（PDF 字距错乱，已人工复原），AJS 1990 正式版付费墙未读；同期 AJS 刊出 Hannan & Tuma 回应（doi:10.1086/229429）。对照组不是『零』而是现行 AFDC。原离婚发现曾在 1970s 末国会福利改革辩论中被广泛引用（secondhand）。

## M22 (META)
- 论断:Corinth & Mayhew(AEI)2026:2017–2025 美国至少 122 个保障收入试点;30 个有就业结果的 RCT 平均就业 +0.8 个百分点,但治疗组≥500 人的 4 个大试点(占治疗组人数 55%)平均 −3.2 个百分点,收入弹性 −0.18。
- 原文:Among the 30 GBI pilots that are randomized controlled trials with published employment outcomes, employment increased by a mean of 0.8 percentage points. Among the four such pilots with a treatment group of at least 500 participants, which together comprise 55 percent of all treatment-group participants, the mean effect on employment is −3.2 percentage points, or a mean income elasticity of −0.18, which is consistent with estimates from the academic literature.
- URL:https://cosm.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026_07-Corinth-and-Mayhew.GBI_.report-1.pdf
- 来源:Corinth & Mayhew, 2026-09, What Can We Learn from Guaranteed Basic Income Pilots in the United States? Evidence on Employment Effects, AEI Center on Opportunity and Social Mobility(2026-02 工作论文版文字相同) · 2026-09 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:是简单平均/计数式汇总,不是逆方差加权 meta,无发表偏倚检验;单位为百分点(pp)不是 SD。AEI 为保守倾向智库;作者明确提醒试点不能外推到永久、全民、全国性 GBI。小试点平均为正、大试点为负——典型的小样本噪声/选择性报告信号。

## M23 (META)
- 论断:同报告:122 个试点中只有 52 个(43%)发布个体结果报告,其中 35 个是 RCT;几乎全部依赖问卷;有数据的 26 个试点平均流失率 37%。
- 原文:Among the 26 pilots for which we can determine attrition rates, the mean is 37 percent. This substantial attrition could bias treatment effects if it is nonrandom across the treatment and control groups.
- URL:https://cosm.aei.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026_07-Corinth-and-Mayhew.GBI_.report-1.pdf
- 来源:Corinth & Mayhew, 2026-09, AEI · 2026-09 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:另引:'Of the 122 pilots in our universe, 52 (43 percent) resulted in a published report tracking individual outcomes.' 资助方结构:Economic Security Project、Mayors for a Guaranteed Income 等倡导组织与 ARPA 联邦资金资助大量试点(原文 Introduction);报告未统计预注册比例。洛杉矶 BIG:LEAP 流失率 55%。
