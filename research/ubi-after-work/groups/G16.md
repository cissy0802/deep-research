# G16 失业伤害的量级与收入/非收入分解

## J11 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Paul 2005 横截面 meta(即 Paul & Moser 2009 JVB 的底稿):k=323 个样本、n=458,820,失业者心理健康问题效应量 d=0.54(95% CI 0.50–0.57);剔除离群值后 d=0.51;失业者中处于心理问题风险者占 34%,就业者 16%。
- 原文:With an average weighted effect size of d = 0.54 (k = 323, n = 458,820) the overall effect was of medium size with a narrow confidence interval from 0.50 to 0.57, clearly excluding effects of small size (see table 3).
- URL:https://forschungsnetzwerk.ams.at/dam/jcr:3bd79c1c-9fd4-4d6f-9cbf-eeda870d0be8/The_negative_mental_health_effect_of_unemployment_UoN_2005.pdf
- 来源:Karsten I. Paul, 2005, dissertation (basis of Paul & Moser 2009, JVB 74:264-282) · 2005-11 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:口径陷阱:JVB 2009 正式版(237 项横截面 + 87 项纵向研究)付费墙未取得,正式版数字以 d=0.51、34% vs 16% 广为引用,与本论文『剔除离群后 d=0.51』『34% vs 16%』一致,但样本数可能有出入;引用时应注明来自 2005 博士论文。测量为精神健康量表(GHQ、CES-D 等),不是生活满意度。

## J12 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:纵向 meta:就业者失业后痛苦显著上升(d=0.19),失业者再就业后痛苦显著下降(d=-0.35)——支持『失业导致痛苦』的社会因果方向;工厂关闭类准实验研究 d=0.38,与其他研究无显著差异。
- 原文:For employed persons who lost their jobs, a significant increase in distress was found (d = 0.19). For unemployed persons who found new jobs, a significant decrease in distress was found (d = -0.35).
- URL:https://forschungsnetzwerk.ams.at/dam/jcr:3bd79c1c-9fd4-4d6f-9cbf-eeda870d0be8/The_negative_mental_health_effect_of_unemployment_UoN_2005.pdf
- 来源:Karsten I. Paul, 2005, dissertation · 2005-11 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:工厂关闭:『Studies examining persons affected by a factory closure or a mass-layoff had slightly smaller effect sizes (d = 0.38)… This difference was not significant』。注意纵向 d 远小于横截面 d(0.19 vs 0.54),部分差距可能来自选择效应或测量时点。

## J13 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:失业保护水平的调节作用不稳:高失业保护国家效应量较小(d=0.46 vs 0.58,p=0.003),但控制研究设计后差异缩小且不再显著(0.41 vs 0.49,p=0.15)——慷慨的救济能缓冲一部分,但远不能消除失业的精神健康伤害。
- 原文:In countries with a high level of unemployment protection, the effect sizes were significantly weaker than in countries with a medium or low level of unemployment protection (unemployment protection high (d = 0.46 vs. d = 0,58, Qb = 8.61; p = 0.0033). After the exclusion of possibly biasing studies, the difference between the average effect sizes was slightly reduced and not significant any longer (d = 0.41 vs. d = 0.49, Qb = 2.03; p = 0.1539).
- URL:https://forschungsnetzwerk.ams.at/dam/jcr:3bd79c1c-9fd4-4d6f-9cbf-eeda870d0be8/The_negative_mental_health_effect_of_unemployment_UoN_2005.pdf
- 来源:Karsten I. Paul, 2005, dissertation · 2005-11 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:国家层面的生态比较(12 国高保护 vs 其他),且与收入不平等高度共线(作者指出 12 个高保护国中 10 个是低不平等国家)。即便在最慷慨的保护下 d≈0.41——仍是中等效应。

## J14 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:McKee-Ryan 等 2005 meta(104 项研究、437 个效应量):失业者心理与身体健康均差于就业者;失业持续时间与样本类型有调节作用,但当期失业率和失业救济金额都没有调节作用;失业者中『工作角色中心度』与心理健康关系强于人力资本和人口学变量。
- 原文:Unemployment duration and sample type (school leaver vs. mature unemployed) moderated the relationship between mental health and unemployment, but the current unemployment rate and the amount of unemployment benefits did not. Within unemployed samples, work-role centrality, coping resources (personal, social, financial, and time structure), cognitive appraisals, and coping strategies displayed stronger relationships with mental health than did human capital or demographic variables.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15641890/
- 来源:McKee-Ryan, Song, Wanberg & Kinicki, 2005, Psychological and physical well-being during unemployment: a meta-analytic study, Journal of Applied Psychology 90(1):53-76 · 2005-01 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:与 Paul 2005 在『救济是否调节』上冲突:McKee-Ryan 不显著、Paul 显著但不稳;Paul 归因于统计功效差异。两份 meta 都说明『钱的多少』至少不是失业伤害的主开关。工作中心度越高、失业越痛——意味着伤害与个人把工作当身份的程度相关,而这是可被文化改变的。

## J15 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Winkelmann & Winkelmann(德国 SOEP,1984–89,20–64 岁男性,个体固定效应 logit):失业的非金钱成本远大于收入损失;按收入系数 0.13 推算,要抵消失业的满意度下降,收入需增加约 7 倍(对数单位);若失业使收入减半,金钱成本 0.13×0.5 远小于非金钱成本 0.96。
- 原文:Based on the fixed effects logit income estimate of 0·13, the compensating variation is roughly 7. This number suggests that income would need to be increased tremendously in order to trigger an increase in satisfaction large enough to just offset the adverse effect of unemployment. Moreover, it also suggests that the pecuniary costs of unemployment are relatively small. If we assume an unem- ployment-induced income reduction of 50%, the estimated pecuniary costs of unemployment of A0·13 ∗ 0·5 fall substantially short of the non-pecuniary cost of A0·96.
- URL:https://www.uzh.ch/cmsssl/econ/dam/jcr:59d43fc7-11ad-40b2-b1b9-bd6c4c50046a/economica.pdf
- 来源:Liliana Winkelmann & Rainer Winkelmann, 1998, Why Are the Unemployed So Unhappy? Evidence from Panel Data, Economica 65(257):1-15 · 1998-02 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:N=20,944 观测;因变量是二分化的生活满意度(≤7 为不满意)。『A0·13』为 PDF 中负号的 OCR 乱码(即 −0.13/−0.96)。口径陷阱:补偿变差依赖于收入系数,而收入系数在固定效应下被测量误差严重衰减(收入在个体内变化小),所以『需要 7 倍收入』很可能高估了非金钱成本;同时只看男性。论文同时报告:非劳动力(OLF)效应为负但显著小于失业,且失业满意度与失业持续时长无关(无适应)。

## J16 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:同文:失业效应几乎是健康不佳效应的三倍;非劳动力(OLF)的负效应显著小于失业——即『不工作』本身的代价小于『想工作却没工作』。
- 原文:The effect of unemployment is large—almost three times larger than the effect of bad health. The out of labour force (OLF) coefficient is negative and significant as well; however, it is smaller, and the hypothesis that unemploy- ment and OLF have the same effect on satisfaction can be rejected at any conventional significance level.
- URL:https://www.uzh.ch/cmsssl/econ/dam/jcr:59d43fc7-11ad-40b2-b1b9-bd6c4c50046a/economica.pdf
- 来源:Winkelmann & Winkelmann, 1998, Economica 65:1-15 · 1998-02 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:非自愿失业 vs 自愿不工作区分的早期证据。OLF 样本含提前退休、病休等,异质性大。

## J17 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Lucas 等(德国 15 年追踪,>24,000 人):失业后生活满意度向基线回升但平均不完全恢复,即使再就业后也是如此;曾经失业的人再次失业时反应并不更轻。
- 原文:However, on average, individuals did not completely return to their former levels of satisfaction, even after they became reemployed. Furthermore, contrary to expectations from adaptation theories, people who had experienced unemployment in the past did not react any less negatively to a new bout of unemployment than did people who had not been previously unemployed.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14717825/
- 来源:Lucas, Clark, Georgellis & Diener, 2004, Unemployment alters the set point for life satisfaction, Psychological Science 15(1):8-13 · 2004-01 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:SOEP;单题 0–10 生活满意度。『疤痕效应』:再就业后仍未完全恢复——说明伤害不只是当期收入或当期时间结构。

## J18 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Clark、Diener、Georgellis、Lucas(SOEP 20 波,前后滞后模型):对结婚、离婚、丧偶、生子、被裁员都不能拒绝完全适应假设,但对失业几乎看不到适应(正式版限定为男性)。
- 原文:We cannot reject the hypothesis of complete adaptation to marriage, divorce, widowhood, birth of child and layoff. However, there is little evidence of adaptation to unemployment for men.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0297.2008.02150.x
- 来源:Clark, Diener, Georgellis & Lucas, 2008, Lags and Leads in Life Satisfaction: A Test of the Baseline Hypothesis, Economic Journal 118(529):F222-F243 · 2008-06 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:EJ 正式版摘要(OpenAlex);CEP DP 836(2007)工作论文版措辞是『little evidence of adaptation to unemployment』未限定男性——正式版收窄到男性,引用时注意。区分『被裁』(事件,可适应)与『处于失业状态』(持续,不适应)。

## J19 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Kassenboehmer & Haisken-DeNew(SOEP 1991–2006):用公司关闭识别外生失业入口以排除反向因果;女性因公司关闭进入失业对生活满意度有额外强负效应,所需补偿收入『巨大』,表明外生失业存在巨大非金钱成本。工作论文版估计西德女性需补偿 4.7 个对数点收入。
- 原文:The large compensating variation in terms of income indicates enormous non‐pecuniary negative effects for women of exogenous entry unemployment due to company closures.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1468-0297.2008.02246.x
- 来源:Kassenboehmer & Haisken-DeNew, 2009, You're Fired! The Causal Negative Effect of Entry Unemployment on Life Satisfaction, Economic Journal 119(536):448-462 · 2009-02 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:工作论文 Ruhr Economic Papers No.63(2008):『women in West Germany would have to be compensated 4.7 log points [(0.245+1.017)/0.271] of income』;同文结论:『For men, with few exceptions, the reason for entry into (voluntary or involuntary) unemployment has no additional effect』——对男性而言,自愿还是被迫离职并不改变失业的伤害,意味着伤害来自『失业状态』而不是『被抛弃的事件』。4.7 对数点≈百倍收入,同样受收入系数衰减偏误影响,不宜字面解读。

## J29 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Pohlan(德国调查+行政数据联接,倾向得分加权 + DiD):失业显著损害社会融入感、生活满意度、经济资源与精神健康,并阻碍通常由就业满足的心理社会需要(社会地位、自我效能);影响持久,随失业时长加深,再就业后仍持续。
- 原文:Moreover, this paper shows that becoming unemployed hinders the fulfillment of psychosocial needs that are typically associated with employment, such as social status and higher self-efficacy. The effects of job loss are long-lasting, grow more profound the longer the duration of unemployment and persist following reemployment.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jebo.2019.06.006
- 来源:Laura Pohlan, 2019, Unemployment and social exclusion, Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization 164:273-299 · 2019-08 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:OpenAlex 摘要;正文未读,系数未核。数据为 IAB PASS 面板(德国低收入家庭超采样),与 Hartz IV 收入保障下的失业者相关——即在有最低收入保障的情形下潜在功能损失仍存在。
