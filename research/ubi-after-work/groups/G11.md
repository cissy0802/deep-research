# G11 现金与主观福祉/精神健康 meta 及幸福测量口径

## M06 (META)
- 论断:McGuire–Kaiser–Bach-Mortensen 2022 meta:LMIC 45 项研究、116,999 人,平均随访约 2 年,现金转移对主观幸福感 d=0.13(95%CI 0.09–0.18)、对精神健康 d=0.07(0.05–0.09);转移额(绝对与相对)是最强预测因子。
- 原文:After an average follow-up time of two years, we find that CTs have a small but statistically significant positive effect on both SWB (Cohen's d = 0.13, 95% confidence interval (CI) 0.09, 0.18) and MH (d = 0.07, 95% CI 0.05, 0.09) among recipients. CT value, both relative to previous income and in absolute terms, is a strong predictor of the effect size.
- URL:https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-021-01252-z
- 来源:McGuire, Kaiser, Bach-Mortensen, 2022, A systematic review and meta-analysis of the impact of cash transfers on subjective well-being and mental health in LMICs, Nature Human Behaviour 6:359–370 · 2022-01 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:单位 Cohen's d(SD),全部为自报量表;仅 LMIC。第一作者单位 Happier Lives Institute(HLI 以「幸福感成本效益」为主张,曾据此倡导 StrongMinds 等,存在立场关联);论文声明 'The authors received no specific funding for this work' 与 'no competing interests'。正文付费墙,衰减/发表偏倚细节见 M07–M09(来自 2020 年工作论文版)。

## M07 (META)
- 论断:同一 meta 的 2020 工作论文版(38 项研究):效应每年衰减约 0.01–0.014 SD;按此速度,一笔使家庭收入翻倍的转移要十余年才衰减完。
- 原文:Using the coefficient from Model 2, each year the effect is estimated to decline by 0.014 SDs. With that estimate, a CT which doubles household income would take over a decade to decay.
- URL:https://gwern.net/doc/www/www.happierlivesinstitute.org/ec0850b0b0c0ad5eea87469f20d4adac3d015dd3.pdf
- 来源:McGuire, Bach-Mortensen, Kaiser, 2020-12, The impact of cash transfers on SWB and MH in LMICs (Working paper, Happier Lives Institute) · 2020-12 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这是预印本版本(gwern 镜像 HLI 原 PDF),与 2022 正式版研究数不同(38 vs 45)、合并效应不同(0.10 composite vs 分开报告);正式版的衰减系数未能在付费墙后核对。线性外推到「十年」是模型假设,原文数据大部分随访<5 年,作者自称需要 5+ 年研究。

## M08 (META)
- 论断:衰减主要来自一次性(lump-sum)转移;持续发放的(stream)转移在最后随访时多数仍在发放,趋势平;7 项多次随访研究中 6 项效应下降(第一次 vs 第二次随访 p=0.007)。
- 原文:Lump CTs appear to decay over time while stream CTs (which are nearly all ongoing at the time of the last follow-up) show a flat trend.
- URL:https://gwern.net/doc/www/www.happierlivesinstitute.org/ec0850b0b0c0ad5eea87469f20d4adac3d015dd3.pdf
- 来源:McGuire et al., 2020-12, HLI working paper · 2020-12 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:关键口径陷阱:「不衰减」的 stream 转移其实还在发钱,测的是「发钱期间」而非「停钱之后」;lump-sum 衰减可能主要由 Blattman 等乌干达研究驱动(原文:'this may largely be driven by the study of Blattman et al')。原文亦称 lump vs stream 交互项不显著。

## M09 (META)
- 论断:该 meta 的发表偏倚检验:漏斗图基本对称,Egger 检验 p=0.769;p-curve 不支持普遍 p-hacking;38 项中仅 9 项预注册。
- 原文:We use Egger’s regression test to check this quantitatively by regressing the standard error on the effect size. The test does not reject the null of funnel plot symmetry (p=0.769), supporting our reading of the plot.
- URL:https://gwern.net/doc/www/www.happierlivesinstitute.org/ec0850b0b0c0ad5eea87469f20d4adac3d015dd3.pdf
- 来源:McGuire et al., 2020-12, HLI working paper · 2020-12 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:预注册比例出自编码表 'Pre-registered ... Yes=9, No=29'(约 24%)。Egger 检验在 k≈38 且异质性大时检验力有限;正式版 2022 的偏倚检验结果未核对。

## M10 (META)
- 论断:Ridley–Rao–Schilbach–Patel 2020 Science 综述:贫困与抑郁焦虑存在双向因果——精神疾病降低就业与收入,负面经济冲击导致精神疾病,现金转移等反贫困项目改善精神健康。
- 原文:Research shows that mental illness reduces employment and therefore income, and that psychological interventions generate economic gains. Similarly, negative economic shocks cause mental illness, and antipoverty programs such as cash transfers improve mental health.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aay0214
- 来源:Ridley, Rao, Schilbach, Patel, 2020, Poverty, depression, and anxiety: Causal evidence and mechanisms, Science 370(6522) · 2020-12 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:综述型;证据几乎全部来自 LMIC。2024-03 Science 发布了该文 Erratum(doi 10.1126/science.adp1916),内容未能读取(付费墙),见 open_questions。

## M11 (META)
- 论断:Ridley 等(NBER WP 版)对 RCT 的小型 meta:无条件现金转移对精神健康平均 0.067 SD,多元反贫困项目 0.138 SD,总体 0.094 SD。
- 原文:Multi−faceted anti−poverty programs effect (average: 0.138 SD) Cash transfers effect (average: 0.067 SD) Overall effect (average: 0.094 SD)
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w27157.pdf
- 来源:Ridley et al., 2020, NBER Working Paper 27157 (Figure 4) · 2020-05 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:取自工作论文版图注,Science 正式版图 4 数值未核对,且 2024 有勘误;与 McGuire 的 MH d=0.07 高度一致,两者纳入研究大量重叠,不能当作两条独立证据。

## M20 (META)
- 论断:Cochrane 2022(Pega 等)LMIC 无条件现金转移:34 项研究,1–2 年时「两周至三个月内生病」概率可能下降(RR 0.79,95%CI 0.67–0.92,6 项整群 RCT,中等确定性);对抑郁水平与死亡率证据不确定/无研究;对劳动参与的影响不确定。
- 原文:Despite several studies providing relevant evidence, the effects of UCTs on the likelihood of being moderately stunted and on the level of depression remain uncertain. We found no study on the effect of UCTs on mortality risk.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.cd011135.pub3
- 来源:Pega F, et al., 2022, Unconditional cash transfers for reducing poverty and vulnerabilities: effect on use of health services and health outcomes in LMICs, Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews CD011135.pub3 · 2022-03 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:GRADE 口径与经济学 meta 不同:Cochrane 判定抑郁效应「不确定」,而 McGuire/Ridley 报告显著小正效应——差别来自纳入标准与偏倚评级('Most studies carried an overall high risk of bias');劳动参与亦判 'uncertain'。资助:'Most studies were funded by national governments or international organisations, or both'。效应单位是 RR(风险比),不能与 SD 直接比较。作者名单仅确认第一作者 Pega。

## M21 (META)
- 论断:Epidemiologic Reviews 2025 美加保障收入与健康 scoping review:25 项研究、16 个项目(检索截至 2022-04),总体对健康有显著正效应,尤其对最脆弱人群;但缺长期效应与非预期后果数据。
- 原文:Guaranteed-income initiatives generally had significant positive impacts on health outcomes, especially among the most vulnerable recipients. Data were absent on neighborhood-level health outcomes, chronic and infectious diseases, potential unintended consequences, and long-term impacts of GI on health.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1093/epirev/mxaf003
- 来源:(作者未取得), 2025, Guaranteed income and health in the United States and Canada: a scoping review, Epidemiologic Reviews · 2025-03 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:检索截止 2022-04-23,早于 OpenResearch(2024,健康效应一年后回归基线)等大样本 RCT,结论偏乐观的时间窗陷阱;scoping review 无效应量合并。

## M24 (META)
- 论断:OECD 2013 指南把主观幸福感分为对生活的评价、对生活的体验(情感),以及「eudaimonic」心理福祉(意义/目的)三类,并给出原型问卷模块。
- 原文:They provide guidance on collecting information on people's evaluations and experiences of life, as well as on collecting “eudaimonic” measures of psychological well-being.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264191655-en
- 来源:OECD, 2013, OECD Guidelines on Measuring Subjective Well-being (Europe PMC/PubMed 24600748 书目摘要) · 2013-03 · 【官方】 · 官方数据 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:引语为官方书目摘要,不是正文定义句;OECD 与 NCBI Bookshelf 正文均被 403/验证码拦截。现金转移文献多测生活满意度或抑郁量表,很少测 eudaimonia(意义)——「不工作的人有没有意义感」恰是证据最薄的维度。

## M25 (META)
- 论断:Kahneman & Deaton 2010:基于 45 万份 Gallup 回答,生活评价随 log 收入稳定上升;情绪福祉也上升,但年收入约 7.5 万美元以上不再提升。
- 原文:When plotted against log income, life evaluation rises steadily. Emotional well-being also rises with log income, but there is no further progress beyond an annual income of ~$75,000.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1011492107
- 来源:Kahneman & Deaton, 2010, High income improves evaluation of life but not emotional well-being, PNAS 107(38) · 2010-09 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:横截面相关,非因果;情绪用「昨天是否有…」二分题。资助含 NIA/NICHD。

## M26 (META)
- 论断:Killingsworth 2021:173 万条经验采样、33,391 名在职美国成人,体验与评价福祉都随 log 收入线性上升,7.5 万美元以上无平台。
- 原文:There was no evidence for an experienced well-being plateau above $75,000/y, contrary to some influential past research.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2016976118
- 来源:Killingsworth, 2021, Experienced well-being rises with income, even above $75,000 per year, PNAS 118(4) · 2021-01 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:样本只有在职者(employed)——恰好不覆盖「不工作的人」;连续量表经验采样。

## M27 (META)
- 论断:Killingsworth–Kahneman–Mellers 2023 对抗性合作:平台只出现在最不快乐人群;较快乐人群幸福感随收入持续上升;Kahneman-Deaton 的二分题测的其实是「不快乐」,有天花板效应。
- 原文:A reanalysis of Killingsworth's experienced sampling data confirmed the flattening pattern only for the least happy people. Happiness increases steadily with log(income) among happier people, and even accelerates in the happiest group.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2208661120
- 来源:Killingsworth, Kahneman, Mellers, 2023, Income and emotional well-being: A conflict resolved, PNAS 120(10) · 2023-03 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:对本期的含义:收入对「减少痛苦」与「增加快乐」可能是两条曲线;现金转移 meta 多用抑郁量表(测痛苦)与生活满意度(测评价),二者效应差(LMIC:SWB 0.13 vs MH 0.07)与此一致但非直接检验。
