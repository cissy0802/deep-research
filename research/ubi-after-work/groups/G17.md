# G17 情绪 vs 满意度、救济能补多少、社会规范

## J20 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Knabe 等(DRM,2008 年柏林与马格德堡,714 人:366 名全职雇员、348 名领取 ALG II 的长期失业者):失业者生活满意度 4.58 vs 就业者 7.11(差 2.53);但按时长加权的净情感几乎相同(4.24 vs 4.23),U 指数 0.17 vs 0.15、片段满意度 7.04 vs 7.23 差异均不显著。
- 原文:The employed reported an average value of 7.11, the unemployed stated an average value of only 4.58 (Table 4). The difference of 2.53 points is statistically significant at any reasonable level. … An employed person’s average net affect is 4.23. … The unemployed report a score of 4.24. Measured by the duration-weighted net affect, the unemployed do not feel unhappy, but are in fact as happy as the employed.
- URL:https://www.ifo.de/DocDL/cesifo1_wp2604.pdf
- 来源:Knabe, Rätzel, Schöb & Weimann, 2009/2010, Dissatisfied with Life but Having a Good Day: Time-use and Well-being of the Unemployed, CESifo WP 2604 / Economic Journal 120(547):867-889 · 2010-09 · 【研究】 · 调查 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:引文取自 CESifo 工作论文版(2009-04),EJ 正式版摘要措辞一致。口径陷阱:失业者在职业介绍所招募、应答率约 15%、给 10 欧元报酬,雇员从电话簿抽样——两组抽样方式不同,存在选择偏误;横截面。资助方:Fritz Thyssen Foundation(Research project No. 10.07.1.086)。『…』处省略中间句子。

## J21 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Knabe 等的机制:失业者在相同活动中更悲伤,但他们把就业者用于工作的时间用在更愉快的活动(社交等),两者相抵;作者推测失业者未调整期望(故生活满意度低)但经历了享乐适应。
- 原文:Although the unemployed feel sadder when engaged in similar activities, they can compensate this by using the time the employed are at work in more enjoyable ways. Our finding that unemployment affects life satisfaction and experienced utility differently may be explained by the fact that people do not adjust their aspirations when becoming unemployed but face hedonic adaptation to changing life circumstances, triggered by the opportunity to use the time in a way that yields higher levels of satisfaction than working.
- URL:https://www.ifo.de/DocDL/cesifo1_wp2604.pdf
- 来源:Knabe et al., 2009, CESifo WP 2604 · 2009-04 · 【研究】 · 调查 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:对 AI 情景最重要的口径分叉:『日子过得怎样』(情绪)与『对人生评价如何』(生活满意度)给出相反答案。生活满意度带有社会比较与规范评价成分,情绪不带——若规范改变,生活满意度缺口可能缩小。

## J22 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Krueger & Mueller(PATS 美国时间使用+情绪调查):失业者一天中更少疲惫、更悲伤、更不快乐;看电视和找工作时的悲伤尤其强烈;2012 年纵向数据显示再就业时悲伤骤降。
- 原文:We find, for example, that the unemployed report feeling less tired, more sad, and less happy than the employed over the course of the day. Watching television and searching for a job are associated with notably intense feelings of sadness for the unemployed.
- URL:https://docs.iza.org/dp3490.pdf
- 来源:Krueger & Mueller, 2008, The Lot of the Unemployed: A Time Use Perspective, IZA DP 3490; and 2012, Time Use, Emotional Well-Being, and Unemployment: Evidence from Longitudinal Data, AER P&P 102(3):594-599 · 2008-05 · 【研究】 · 调查 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:2012 AER P&P 摘要(OpenAlex):『(3) the unemployed report higher levels of sadness during specific episodes of the day than the employed; and (4) sadness decreases abruptly at the time of re-employment.』与 Knabe 2010 部分冲突:Knabe 说时长加权后情绪持平,K&M 说一天整体更悲伤。差异可能来自样本(美国 vs 德国长期失业者)、测量(单项情绪 vs 净情感)及 Knabe 的抽样偏误。

## J23 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Di Tella、MacCulloch、Oswald(欧美约 25 万人,1970s–1990s):更慷慨的失业救济与更高的国民幸福相关,且对失业者和就业者的提升幅度相近(系数 1.25 与 1.44)——救济并未缩小就业者与失业者之间的幸福差距。
- 原文:Moreover, the two coefficients on the benefits variable, 1.25 and 1.44, are similar. Hence an increase in the generosity of unemployment benefits helps the well-being of the unemployed and employed by a similar amount (perhaps because the employed know they may in the future lose their jobs, and the jobless know they may find a job). … The reason is that the benefits variable enters the Gap equation – where the ‘gap’ can be thought of as the difference in well-being between those with jobs and those looking for a job – with a coefficient that is insignificantly different from zero.
- URL:https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/314/1/WRAP_Oswald_finaldmo30jul.pdf
- 来源:Di Tella, MacCulloch & Oswald, 2003, The Macroeconomics of Happiness, Review of Economics and Statistics 85(4):809-827 · 2003-11 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:国家-年份层面 OECD 替代率,重复横截面(Eurobarometer + GSS)。关键:『钱让每个人都好过一点,但不填平失业的缺口』——与 Wulfgramm 2014 的结论方向不一致(见下)。

## J24 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Wulfgramm(ESS,21 个欧洲国家,多层与面板估计):失业在所有国家都显著降低生活满意度,但失业救济慷慨度的调节作用『出人意料地大』——救济微薄时失业的负效应几乎翻倍;作者认为既有资源机制也有非金钱机制。积极劳动力市场政策的调节作用不稳健。
- 原文:While unemployment has strong negative life satisfaction effects in all 21 European countries under study, the generosity of passive labour market policy moderates this effect to a surprisingly large extent: the adverse effect of unemployment is almost doubled if unemployment benefits are meagre. This moderating effect can be explained both by a resource as well as a non-pecuniary mechanism. The positive moderating effect of active labour market policy is less robust across model specifications.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1177/0958928714525817
- 来源:Melike Wulfgramm, 2014, Life satisfaction effects of unemployment in Europe: The moderating influence of labour market policy, Journal of European Social Policy 24(3):258-272 · 2014-07 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:OpenAlex 摘要;正文付费墙未读,系数未核。口径:『翻倍』是从慷慨到微薄的对比,反过来说即最慷慨的救济也只把伤害减半左右,远未归零。与 McKee-Ryan(不显著)、Di Tella(缺口不变)冲突;差异可能来自 ESS 跨国横截面 vs 面板、救济指标定义。

## J25 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Clark(BHPS 7 波,GHQ-12):失业者的幸福感与参照群体(地区、伴侣、家庭)失业率强正相关——周围人失业越多,失业的伤害越小;男性尤其明显,且对个体固定效应稳健。
- 原文:The unemployed's well‐being is shown to be strongly positively correlated with reference group unemployment (at the regional, partner, or household level). This result, far stronger for men, is robust to controls for unobserved individual heterogeneity.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1086/345560
- 来源:Andrew E. Clark, 2003, Unemployment as a Social Norm: Psychological Evidence from Panel Data, Journal of Labor Economics 21(2):289-322 · 2003-04 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:正文付费墙未读;摘要经 OpenAlex。GHQ-12 是精神健康量表不是生活满意度。注意:社会规范效应缩小的是失业者与就业者的『差距』,并非证明伤害会消失;且被 Oesch & Lipps 2013 在德国/瑞士面板中未能复现。

## J26 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Oesch & Lipps(SOEP 1984–2010、瑞士家庭面板 2000–2010,固定效应):没有证据表明周围高失业率能缓冲失业者幸福感下降;地区失业率高时失业同样痛;伤害既不随时间消退,反复失业也不会让它更轻。
- 原文:Our fixed-effects estimates show no evidence for a mitigating effect of high surrounding unemployment on the subjective well-being of the unemployed. Becoming unemployed hurts as much when regional unemployment is high as when it is low. Likewise, the strongly harmful impact of being unemployed on well-being neither wears off over time, nor do repeated episodes of unemployment make it any better.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1093/esr/jcs071
- 来源:Oesch & Lipps, 2013, Does Unemployment Hurt Less if There is More of it Around? A Panel Analysis of Life Satisfaction in Germany and Switzerland, European Sociological Review 29(5):955-967 · 2012-08 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:直接反驳『大家都失业就不痛』的外推。但地区失业率最高也就十几个百分点,远非『全民不工作』,所以两边都无法外推到 AI 全民情景;且『失业』在这些样本里仍然是被定义为偏离规范的状态。

## J27 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Stutzer & Lalive(瑞士):用 1997 年全国失业救济公投中支持削减救济的投票比例度量『工作规范』强度;规范越强,失业者越快找到工作,且失业者生活满意度下降越大。
- 原文:The stronger the norm, the more quickly unemployed people find a new job. This behavior can be explained by utility differences, probably due to social pressure. Unemployed people are significantly less happy than employed people and their reduction in life satisfaction is the larger, the stronger the norm is.
- URL:https://wwz.unibas.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/wwz/00_Professuren/Stutzer_Politische_Oekonomie/Publications/Stutzer_Lalive_SWN_JEEA.pdf
- 来源:Stutzer & Lalive, 2004, The Role of Social Work Norms in Job Searching and Subjective Well-Being, Journal of the European Economic Association 2(4):696-719 · 2004-06 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:接收版 PDF(2003-12)。公投以 50.8% 通过反对削减(即 49.2% 支持削减),投票率 41%。规范是地区差异而非可操控变量,属利用投票的准实验/工具变量设计。数据由瑞士经济事务国务秘书处提供。与 Clark 2003 方向一致:污名/规范是失业伤害中可变的一块。
