# G02 ORUS 健康/政治/育儿;Stockton SEED;Baby's First Years

## U08 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 健康：压力与食物安全大幅但短暂改善；医院/急诊/牙科使用上升、医疗支出约 +$20/月；自报身体健康、血检生物标志物无改善，精神健康第一年后无改善，可排除小效应；家中儿童健康也无效应。
- 原文:The transfer produced large but short-lived improvements in stress and food security and increased hospital, emergency department, and dental care use. However, it did not improve self-reported physical health, clinical biomarkers, or mental health after the first year, and we can rule out small effects on many outcomes. We also find no effects on the health of children in participants’ households.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w32711
- 来源:Miller, Rhodes, Bartik, Broockman, Krause, Vivalt, 2024 (rev. 2026-08), Does Income Affect Health? Evidence from a Randomized Controlled Trial of a Guaranteed Income, NBER w32711 · 2026-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:2024-07 初版（https://public.websites.umich.edu/~mille/ORUS_Health.pdf）原话：'the transfer did not improve mental health after the first year and by year 2 we can again reject very small improvements'。即精神健康效应在第二年消失。2026-08 修订版致谢含 'Erzo Luttmer and four anonymous referees'，显示已进入期刊审稿，但 NBER 页未列已发表版本。资助：NIH 1R01HD108716-01A1 + 私人资金。生物标志物在 Census FSRDC 处理。

## U09 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 政治：$1,000/月 × 3 年对政治参与、投入、偏好、政府信任、极化、民主支持均无影响，可排除与收入-投票率观测关联同量级的效应；处理组反而更倾向认为工作对个人/社会重要、甚至应作为领取政府项目的条件。
- 原文:Contrary to resource models of participation, we find no effects on political participation or engagement, and rule out effects equivalent to the observational association between turnout and income. ... There is also some evidence that treated participants saw work as more important for individuals, society, or even as a requirement for accessing government programs
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w33214
- 来源:Broockman, Rhodes, Bartik, Dotson, Miller, Krause, Vivalt, 2024, The Causal Effects of Income on Political Attitudes and Behavior: A Randomized Field Experiment, NBER w33214 · 2024-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:与本期『公民身份/政治议价权』直接相关：私人慈善、免税、有期限的赠款不同于政府发放的权利型转移，作者自己指出结果 'contrast with findings from other economic shocks such as government-sponsored or taxable transfers'。外推到全民、政府、永久 UBI 的政治效应须谨慎。

## U10 (US)
- 论断:ORUS 育儿与儿童：处理组父母在子女上花费更多、自报育儿行为更好（如更密切监督），但同时自报孩子发育困难与压力更多；学校行政记录中大多数教育结果无显著效应；父母压力与精神困扰的改善在第二年消失。
- 原文:The transfer did not have a meaningful effect on most educational outcomes measured in school administrative records ... While the transfer reduced parents’ stress and mental distress in the first year of the program, these effects were short-lived and dissipated by the second year of the transfer
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w34040
- 来源:Krause, Rhodes, Miller, Bartik, Broockman, Vivalt, 2025 (rev. 2026-09), The Impact of Unconditional Cash Transfers on Parenting and Children, NBER w34040 · 2026-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:又一处自报 vs 行政数据张力：父母自报育儿改善，而学校行政数据无效应；作者自己指出『possibly due to this closer monitoring』父母报告孩子问题更多。

## U12 (US)
- 论断:Stockton SEED：131 人处理组每月 $500、200 人对照，2019-02 至 2021-01 共 24 个月；处理组收入波动更低、精神困扰更低、精力与身体功能更好。
- 原文:A mixed-methods randomized controlled trial in Stockton, CA, USA enrolled 131 individuals to the treatment condition and 200 to control to receive a guaranteed income from February 2019 to January 2021. ... The treatment condition reported lower rates of income volatility than control, lower mental distress, better energy and physical functioning
- URL:https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10160253
- 来源:West & Castro, 2023, Impact of Guaranteed Income on Health, Finances, and Agency: Findings from the Stockton Randomized Controlled Trial, Journal of Urban Health 100:227–244 (doi:10.1007/s11524-023-00723-0) · 2023-05 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:局部（收入 ≤ 斯托克顿家庭中位数 $46,033 的普查区）、有期限、n 极小。全部结果为自报（SF-36、K10、SMS 自报月收入）。研究资助：RWJF Evidence for Action + Economic Security Project（UBI 倡导组织）；发放资金来自私人捐赠。前市长 Tubbs 为项目发起人。有预分析计划（文内引 [26]）。 【并入】SEED 统计效力严重受限：作者承认样本量受成本限制，终期保留率仅约对照 35%、处理 55%（因指标而异），另有 14 人『政治性目的组』被剔除。 原文："By endline, retention was approximately 35% in control and 55% in treatment dependent upon outcome measure."（https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10160253）

## U13 (US)
- 论断:SEED 就业：常被引用的『全职就业率翻倍』来自第一年初步报告；同行评审终稿中疫情前一年从失业转就业的趋势不显著（ANCOVA p=0.183），疫情期间与停发后也无显著效应。
- 原文:Preliminary reporting noted a substantial increase in full and part-time employment among the treatment group during year one [30]. Trend data in the pre-pandemic year of the experiment show substantial shifts from unemployment to employment (full time, part time, or as a caregiver) from baseline to Wave 4, but were not statistically significant. Further observation during the pandemic, as well as after withdrawal of the intervention did not show significant effects on employment
- URL:https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC10160253
- 来源:West & Castro, 2023, Impact of Guaranteed Income on Health, Finances, and Agency: Findings from the Stockton Randomized Controlled Trial, Journal of Urban Health 100:227–244 (doi:10.1007/s11524-023-00723-0) · 2023-05 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:就业变量把『居家照护者』编码为就业=1，口径宽。Table 8 各时段 F 检验 p=0.183 / 0.612 / 0.909。疫情从第 13 个月起干扰全部第二年数据。

## U14 (US)
- 论断:Baby's First Years：1,000 名收入低于贫困线的母亲产后被随机分到每月 $333 或 $20；4 年后（n=891）四项预注册主要结果（语言、执行功能、社会情绪问题、高频脑电）与三项次要结果均无显著效应。
- 原文:After the first four years of the intervention (n=891), we find no statistically significant impacts of the cash transfers on four preregistered primary outcomes (language, executive function, social-emotional problems, and high-frequency brain activity) nor on three secondary outcomes (visual processing/spatial perception, pre-literacy, maternal reports of developmental diagnoses).
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w33844
- 来源:Noble, Magnuson, Duncan, Gennetian, Yoshikawa, Fox, Halpern-Meekin, Troller-Renfree et al., 2025, The Effect of a Monthly Unconditional Cash Transfer on Children's Development at Four Years of Age: A Randomized Controlled Trial in the U.S., NBER w33844 · 2025-05 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:局部（纽约、新奥尔良、明尼阿波利斯-圣保罗、奥马哈 12 家医院）、低收入定向、有期限（延长至 76 个月）。对照也拿 $20。预注册 NCT03593356。资助方三十余家（NICHD R01HD087384、HHS OPRE、Gates、CZI、Arnold Ventures、Robin Hood 等）。约等于家庭收入 +18%，转移强度弱于 ORUS。计划 6 岁、8 岁随访。

## U15 (US)
- 论断:BFY 1 岁脑电（2022）曾报告高现金组婴儿高频 EEG 功率更高，但显著性随设定变化；4 岁随访（2026）预注册主要与次要脑电结果均为零，只有探索性 alpha 功率差异。
- 原文:We find no impact on our primary preregistered outcome (an aggregated index of mid-to-high-frequency brain activity) or our secondary preregistered outcome frontal gamma power.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2026.101673
- 来源:BFY team, 2026, The impact of a monthly unconditional cash transfer on child brain activity: A 4-year follow-up, Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience · 2026-01 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:对照 2022 PNAS（Troller-Renfree et al., doi:10.1073/pnas.2115649119, n=435 子样本）：'Indeed, infants in the high-cash gift group showed more power in high-frequency bands' 但 'the significance of estimates varied with the analytic specification'。早期阳性在随访中未复现，是典型『早期信号衰减』。

## U16 (US)
- 论断:BFY 健康（JAMA Pediatrics 2025）：4 年累计约 $15,000 的转移未改善母亲抑郁、焦虑、BMI，也未改善儿童 BMI 百分位与母亲报告的儿童健康。
- 原文:No statistically detectable group differences were found in maternal depressive symptoms (effect size [ES], 0.04; 95% CI, -0.08 to 0.17; P = .51), anxiety (ES, 0.12; 95% CI, -0.02 to 0.25; P = .09), or BMI (ES, -0.06; 95% CI, -0.21 to 0.09; P = .42).
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapediatrics.2025.1612
- 来源:BFY team, 2025, Cash Transfers and Their Effect on Maternal and Young Children's Health: A Randomized Clinical Trial, JAMA Pediatrics · 2025 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:作者给出三种解释之一：'an 18% increase in income is insufficient to overcome the structural vulnerabilities'。与 ORUS 精神健康第一年后消失方向一致。

## U17 (US)
- 论断:BFY 母亲劳动供给：前四年无统计可检测的参与率或家庭收入差异；疫情高峰期（2020–21）转移似乎减少了母亲工时。
- 原文:We find no overall statistically detectable differences in whether mothers participated in the paid workforce or on total household earnings. Receipt of the cash transfer appears to have reduced hours of maternal work during the height of the pandemic in 2020-21.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpubeco.2024.105159
- 来源:Sauval, Duncan, Gennetian, Magnuson, Fox, Noble, Yoshikawa, 2024, Unconditional Cash Transfers and Maternal Employment: Evidence from the Baby's First Years Study, Journal of Public Economics · 2024 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:UChicago IEL 综述补充：样本量下 'these null effects are not precise enough to rule out small effects, but they can rule out moderate or large effects'。
