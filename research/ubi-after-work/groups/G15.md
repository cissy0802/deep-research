# G15 马林塔尔、潜在剥夺理论及其检验、SDT

## J01 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:马林塔尔研究的起因是 1930 年当地纺织厂关闭,1,200 名工人和 90 名职员同时失业——这是一个全村几乎同时失业的「整体失业社区」,而非个体失业样本。
- 原文:1.200 Arbeiterinnen und Arbeiter sowie 90 Angestellte verloren im Jahr 1930 ihre Arbeit.
- URL:https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/museum_marienthal/shop/2012_05_03_folder_marienthal.pdf
- 来源:Reinhard Müller / Museum Marienthal (AGSÖ, Universität Graz), 2012, Museum Marienthal Folder · 2012-05 · 【官方】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:来源是 Jahoda 遗稿所在的格拉茨大学 AGSÖ 档案馆策展材料,不是 1933 原书;原书受版权保护,网上未见合法全文。brief 要求的「478 户」「失业救济仍在发放」原文数字未能回到一手,见 open_questions。关键外推意义:马林塔尔恰恰是『周围人全都失业』的情形,但仍出现严重的『疲惫社区』——与 Clark 2003 的社会规范缓冲效应形成张力。

- URL:https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/museum_marienthal/shop/2012_05_03_folder_marienthal.pdf
- 来源:Reinhard Müller / Museum Marienthal (AGSÖ, Universität Graz), 2012, Museum Marienthal Folder · 2012-05 · 【官方】 · 观察 · paraphrase
- 备注:引文逐字取自档案馆对原书结论的转述,不是 Jahoda 等 1933 原文,故标 paraphrase。『去政治化』一点对本期『公民议价权』线有直接意义:没有工作的人群并没有更具政治动员力。研究设计为 1931-32 年田野民族志 + 时间使用表,非对照研究。

## J03 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:马林塔尔方法上首创时间使用表:失业者逐小时填写活动;一名男子在上午 10–11 点一栏写下『……等着等着就到中午了』——时间结构瓦解的标志性证据。
- 原文:Diese Bögen wurden von den Forscherinnen an die Arbeitslosen ausgegeben, damit sie darin für jede Stunde des Tages ihre jeweiligen Aktivitäten notieren. Einer der Männer trug für die Stunde von 10.00–11.00 Uhr den Satz »… einstweilen wird es Mittag« ein.
- URL:https://agso.uni-graz.at/archive/museum_marienthal/shop/2012_05_03_folder_marienthal.pdf
- 来源:Reinhard Müller / Museum Marienthal (AGSÖ, Universität Graz), 2012, Museum Marienthal Folder · 2012-05 · 【官方】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:逐字为档案馆文本,其中引用的原书条目(»… einstweilen wird es Mittag«)为原书转引。Paul 等 2023 对 Jahoda et al. 1933/1975 p.83 的概述:突然多出的时间成为『tragic gift』,导致『chaotic and squandered days』。

## J04 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:马林塔尔的伤害不能干净地归因于『非收入功能』:当时失业者获得的经济支持远低于当代西方水平,Paul 认为该研究可能反映的是存在一条『绝对贫困线』(饥饿、缺衣),越过即必然导致绝望与冷漠。
- 原文:This study, conducted at a time when unemployed people received much less financial support than is usual today in Western countries, suggests that there might exist a level of absolute poverty, characterized by starvation and a lack of clothing, that does inevitably lead to despair, apathy and other signs of severe distress (the “broken attitude” in the terminology of Jahoda et al., 1933/1975, p. 96).
- URL:https://forschungsnetzwerk.ams.at/dam/jcr:3bd79c1c-9fd4-4d6f-9cbf-eeda870d0be8/The_negative_mental_health_effect_of_unemployment_UoN_2005.pdf
- 来源:Karsten I. Paul, 2005, The negative mental health effect of unemployment: Meta-analyses of cross-sectional and longitudinal data, Dissertation FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg · 2005-11 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:口径陷阱:常被当作『有钱也没用,缺的是工作』证据的马林塔尔,其实是『救济在发但很微薄且会断』的情形——收入与潜在功能在此高度混淆。救济仍在发放这一事实说明它不是零收入,但也绝非 UBI 级别的充足保障。

## J05 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Jahoda 潜在剥夺模型:就业除了显性功能(挣钱)还有五种潜在功能——时间结构、社会接触、集体目标、地位/身份、规律活动;她认为在现代社会只有就业能充分提供这些功能。
- 原文:She specified five latent functions of employment: “time structure, an enlarged social network, participation in collective efforts, definition of social identity, and required regular activity” (Jahoda, 1997, p. 318). Jahoda (1983) stated that these latent functions correspond to “deep seated needs” (p. 298).
- URL:https://forschungsnetzwerk.ams.at/dam/jcr:3bd79c1c-9fd4-4d6f-9cbf-eeda870d0be8/The_negative_mental_health_effect_of_unemployment_UoN_2005.pdf
- 来源:Karsten I. Paul, 2005, dissertation (quoting Jahoda 1997, 1983) · 2005-11 · 【研究】 · 论证 · secondhand
- 备注:Jahoda 1981 American Psychologist 36(2):184-191 与 1982 Employment and Unemployment (Cambridge UP) 原文均为付费墙/纸本,未取得;此处五功能措辞是 Paul 转引 Jahoda 1997 词典条目,非 1981/1982 原文。引文已对照 Paul 2005 PDF 逐字核对(跨行断字已合并)。Paul 等 2023 用的另一套措辞『time structure, social contact, collective purpose, status, and activity』是后人简称,不是 Jahoda 原话——引用时勿混用。

## J06 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Fryer 的能动性限制理论批评 Jahoda 把人看成被动、受外部驱动的存在;认为人是主动追求自我决定的能动者,失业之所以伤人,是因为它伴随贫困、对未来的不确定和社会权力低下,从而限制和挫败能动性。
- 原文:In contrast to this, Fryer (1986, 1997a) assumes humans to be “agents actively striving for purposeful self-determination, attempting to make sense of, initiate, influence, and cope with events in line with personal values, goals, and expectations of the future” (1997a, p. 12). However, unemployment severely restricts and frustrates agency as well as undermines planning and purposeful action because it is usually associated with poverty, future insecurity, and low social power.
- URL:https://forschungsnetzwerk.ams.at/dam/jcr:3bd79c1c-9fd4-4d6f-9cbf-eeda870d0be8/The_negative_mental_health_effect_of_unemployment_UoN_2005.pdf
- 来源:Karsten I. Paul, 2005, dissertation (summarising Fryer 1986 Social Behaviour 1:3-23; Fryer 1997) · 2005-11 · 【研究】 · 论证 · secondhand
- 备注:Fryer 1986 原文未找到在线全文,经 Paul 转述/转引。对 UBI 的含义:若 Fryer 对,伤害主要来自贫困+不确定+无权,则永久、无条件、充足的收入理论上可补大部分;若 Jahoda 对,则补不上。两者在多数现实失业数据中无法分离——这是本线最大的识别问题。

## J07 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Paul & Batinic 对德国代表性样本(N=998)的检验:就业者在时间结构、社会接触、集体目标、活动四项上高于失业者和非劳动力(学生、家庭主妇、退休者);地位/身份一项就业者与非劳动力无差异,但失业者低于所有其他组。
- 原文:For the fifth of Jahoda's dimensions, identity/status, no significant differences between employed persons and persons who are out of the labor force could be identified. However, unemployed persons reported less status than all other groups did. Thus, Jahoda's model was clearly endorsed for four of the five latent functions of employment and partly endorsed for the fifth function.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1002/job.622
- 来源:Paul & Batinic, 2010, The need for work: Jahoda's latent functions of employment in a representative sample of the German population, Journal of Organizational Behavior 31:45-64 · 2010-01 · 【研究】 · 调查 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:摘要文本经 OpenAlex 摘要索引重建(与出版社摘要同序)。横截面;LAMB 量表自评。『地位』只在失业者身上缺失、非劳动力不缺失——提示地位损失是失业的污名效应,而非『不工作』本身;这正是外推到『大家都不工作』时最关键的一项。

## J08 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Selenko 等四波追踪(T1 N=1,026):交叉滞后 SEM 显示潜在功能剥夺会导致 6 个月后心理健康下降;就业/失业/非劳动力之间的痛苦差异有一部分可由潜在功能获取差异解释。
- 原文:Multiple mediation analyses show the expected differences in distress between the employed, unemployed, and OLF persons and indicate that part of this difference can be explained by differential access to the latent benefits. Furthermore, cross‐lagged structural equation modelling confirms that a deprivation of latent benefits leads to a decrease in psychological health 6 months later.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1348/096317910X519360
- 来源:Selenko, Batinic & Paul, 2011, Does latent deprivation lead to psychological distress? Investigating Jahoda's model in a four-wave study, Journal of Occupational and Organizational Psychology 84:723-740 · 2011-12 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:OpenAlex 摘要。『part of』——中介只解释部分差异。观察性面板,交叉滞后不等于因果识别。

## J09 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Paul 等 2023 meta:潜在与显性功能合计解释心理健康 19% 的方差;单项中财务状况 β=0.20 最大,其次集体目标 0.16、时间结构 0.15、地位 0.13、社会接触 0.05、活动 0.03——五项潜在功能合计权重远超收入,但收入是最大的单一预测因子。
- 原文:Results showed that, together, the latent and manifest functions explained 19% of the variance in mental health (see Table 5). All six manifest and latent functions emerged as significant predictors of mental health in this analysis. The effects were of small-to-medium size, with financial situation β = 0.20, p < 0.001, collective purpose β = 0.16, p < 0.001, time structure β = 0.15, p < 0.001, status β = 0.13, p < 0.001, social contact β = 0.05, p < 0.001, and activity β = 0.03, p = 0.014.
- URL:https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1017358/full
- 来源:Paul, Scholl, Moser, Zechmann & Batinic, 2023, Employment status, psychological needs, and mental health: Meta-analytic findings concerning the latent deprivation model, Frontiers in Psychology 14:1017358 · 2023-03 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:基于元分析相关矩阵的回归(MASEM 类),全部为横截面自评,不是因果分解。『集体目标』(被他人需要)单项仅次于钱——对 AI 场景最有指向性。CC BY 开放获取。

## J10 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:同一 meta 发现退休者几乎与失业者一样缺乏潜在功能(但不缺显性收入功能),学生接近就业者——即『不工作』本身确实减少时间结构等功能,但退休者通常并不像失业者那样痛苦(见 J17),提示潜在功能缺失≠伤害。
- 原文:Specific analyses for three OLF-subgroups found retired people to be almost as deprived of the latent functions (but not the manifest function) as unemployed people, while students were more similar to employed people but still experienced some manifest and latent deprivation. For homemakers, the effect sizes pointed in the expected direction, but they were not significant.
- URL:https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2023.1017358/full
- 来源:Paul et al., 2023, Frontiers in Psychology 14:1017358 · 2023-03 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这是对 Jahoda 理论的关键反面线索:退休者缺乏潜在功能却不如失业者痛苦,说明决定伤害的可能是规范/身份(该不该工作)而不仅是功能缺失。作者自己也提出『低地位可能是失业特有的污名』这一替代解释。

## J31 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:自我决定理论(Ryan & Deci):胜任、自主、关系三种先天心理需要满足时带来自我动机与心理健康,受挫时动机与幸福下降——该理论预测工作的价值取决于它是否满足这三种需要,而非工作本身;工作只是诸多可满足需要的领域之一。
- 原文:The findings have led to the postulate of three innate psychological needs--competence, autonomy, and relatedness--which when satisfied yield enhanced self-motivation and mental health and when thwarted lead to diminished motivation and well-being. Also considered is the significance of these psychological needs and processes within domains such as health care, education, work, sport, religion, and psychotherapy.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.55.1.68
- 来源:Ryan & Deci, 2000, Self-determination theory and the facilitation of intrinsic motivation, social development, and well-being, American Psychologist 55(1):68-78 · 2000-01 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:PubMed/OpenAlex 摘要。SDT 与 Jahoda 的分歧:Jahoda 认为现代社会中只有就业能提供潜在功能;SDT 认为需要可以在多个领域满足,且受控(外在强制)的活动反而损害幸福——这为『不工作也能活得好』提供理论空间,但该推断属外推,非 SDT 作者对失业的直接主张。
