# G22 对手方立法、民调与制度设计

## V16 (VOICES)
- 论断:【对手方/立法】爱达荷 2024 与南达科他 2024 立法禁止地方政府搞「保障收入项目」,定义都是「定期、无条件、可任意使用的现金」,并明确豁免要求求职/工作/参训的项目——禁的是「无条件」,不是「给钱」。
- 原文:"guaranteed income program" means a plan funded or administered by the government under which an individual is provided with regular, unconditional cash payments to be used for any purpose by the individual. "Guaranteed income program" does not include a program under which an individual is required to seek reemployment, perform work, or attend training as a condition of any payments.
- URL:https://legislature.idaho.gov/wp-content/uploads/statutesrules/idstat/Title56/T56CH20.pdf ; https://sdlegislature.gov/api/Statutes/8-5-15.html?all=true
- 来源:Idaho Code Title 56 Ch. 20(2024 ch. 224,2025 重编号);South Dakota Codified Law 8-5-15(SL 2024 ch 27) · 2024-03 · 【官方】 · 官方数据 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:爱达荷 56-2002:「a county, city, agency, board, or any other political subdivision of this state shall not adopt or enforce any law ... for the purpose of making payments to individuals under a guaranteed income program ... null and void」。南达科他 8-5-15 现行文本(我抓到的版本)只写 township,并由州检察长起诉——早期报道称 SB 115 覆盖 county/township/municipality,可能分散在多条法条,未逐条核。这些州当时多无本地试点:属预防性立法。

A guaranteed income program operating prior to or on the effective date of this Act may remain in effect until January 1, 2025, or until expiration of the program, whichever is earlier.
- URL:https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/publications/LGE/90/HF2319.pdf
- 来源:Iowa General Assembly, 2024, House File 2319 (Enrolled) · 2024-04 · 【官方】 · 官方数据 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:定义:「regular periodic cash payments that are unearned and that may be used for any purpose, but does not include a program under which an individual is required to perform work or attend training」。直接针对 Polk 县 UpLift 试点(二手:试点改由私人资金续跑)。立法只切断「地方公共资金」,不禁止私人慈善——所以私人资助的 RCT(如 OpenResearch)不受影响,但这让「政府能否做」这一政策问题无法在这些州被检验。

## V21 (VOICES)
- 论断:【论证/制度设计】Hoynes & Rothstein 2019:向每位 18+ 美国居民每年 $12,000 的「标准 UBI」约 $3 万亿/年,约为 2017 年联邦总支出的 75%;若保留社保、Medicare、Medicaid,其余全部转移项目只够覆盖约五分之一成本;排除 65 岁以上可降约五分之一。
- 原文:A universal payment of $12,000 per year to each adult U.S. resident over age 18 would cost roughly $3 trillion per year. This is about 75 percent of current total federal expenditures, including all on- and off-budget items, in 2017. ... If Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid are preserved, the remaining programs in Table 3 together would cover only about one-fifth of the cost of the canonical UBI.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w25538
- 来源:Hilary Hoynes & Jesse Rothstein, 2019, Universal Basic Income in the US and Advanced Countries, NBER WP 25538 / Annual Review of Economics 11 · 2019-02 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:逐字取自 NBER 工作论文版(注明为 Annual Review of Economics UBI 专题约稿),期刊版页码/措辞可能微调。$3T 是毛成本,未扣回收税收;作者强调「taxes ... are likely of first-order importance」。用标准弹性(收入弹性 -0.05 到 -0.10)推算 $12,000 UBI 使工时下降 1.6%–3.3%。摘要直说:「the ongoing UBI pilot studies will do little to resolve the major outstanding questions」——对本期所有试点证据的外推性是关键警告。作者为学术经济学家,无已知厂商利益。

## V22 (VOICES)
- 论断:【论证/制度设计】Hoynes & Rothstein:相对现有转移体系,UBI 会把更大份额转向无子女、非老年、非残障与中等收入家庭;若「替代」现有项目则对最低收入家庭帮助大减,若「叠加」并由累进税融资则是很大的向下再分配。
- 原文:A UBI would direct much larger shares of transfers to childless, non-elderly, non-disabled households than existing programs, and much more to middle-income rather than poor households. A UBI large enough to increase transfers to low-income families would be enormously expensive.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w25538
- 来源:Hilary Hoynes & Jesse Rothstein, 2019, Universal Basic Income in the US and Advanced Countries, NBER WP 25538 · 2019-02 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:正文:「If a UBI of the scale often contemplated were introduced on top of the existing transfer system, it would represent a very large downward redistribution of income. In contrast, a UBI that replaced, rather than supplemented, current programs would be less targeted, providing much less assistance to the lowest income families.」这是评价 Yang(二选一)、Musk(联邦支票)、Altman(资本税股息)的同一把尺:关键不是发不发钱,而是「替代还是叠加、谁出税」。老年家庭平均社保 $17,400 + 医保精算值 $12,900,替代后老人转移收入降超三分之一。

## V24 (VOICES)

- 原文:(转述)The total cost of £42bn – representing 2.3% of UK GDP – could be fully funded through changes to the Personal Allowance, making the proposal fiscally neutral
- URL:https://www.ucl.ac.uk/bartlett/igp/news/2017/oct/igps-social-prosperity-network-publishes-uks-first-report-universal-basic-services
- 来源:UCL Institute for Global Prosperity, 2017, Social Prosperity for the Future: A Proposal for Universal Basic Services · 2017-10 · 【研究】 · 论证 · secondhand
- 备注:UCL 页面与报告 PDF 均 403,数字来自 UCL Bartlett 摘要的搜索片段与 P2P Foundation 转述,未逐字核对。批评(RSA Painter,二手):「universal」名不副实,最大受益是约 150 万获免费住房的家庭,人人都拿到的只有约 £5 的宽带/手机。该方案为论证型,无实测;与现金 UBI 的取舍是「选择自由 vs 成本与针对性」。
