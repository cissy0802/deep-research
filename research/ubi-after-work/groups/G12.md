# G12 彩票中奖者的劳动供给

## W01 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:瑞典彩票(PLS/Kombi/Triss 三类,注册数据):中 100 万 SEK(约 $14 万)使中奖者前 10 年税前劳动收入累计减少约 8 万 SEK(≈半年平均收入);即每 10 万 SEK 中奖,10 年累计少挣 8,033 SEK;下降立即发生、大致恒定、持续 10 年以上,且按年龄/教育/性别差异小。
- 原文:In our main specification, a windfall gain of 1 million Swedish krona (about $140,000) reduces the pre-tax labor earnings of the winner over the first 10 years by roughly 80,000 SEK, or approximately one half of the average annual income.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w21762
- 来源:Cesarini, Lindqvist, Notowidigdo, Östling, 2017, The Effect of Wealth on Individual and Household Labor Supply: Evidence from Swedish Lotteries, American Economic Review 107(12)(引自 NBER WP 21762, 2015 版正文) · 2017-12 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:自然实验:条件于彩票类型/持票数后奖金随机。一次性财富冲击(Triss-Monthly 为月付),非 UBI 式全民永久转移;局部样本(购彩者)。数字取自 WP 版,AER 正式版系数可能小幅修订。资助:瑞典研究理事会、Handelsbanken 研究基金、NSF 等;无预注册(劳动供给论文)。'持续10年以上'中后期衰减主要因中奖者到达退休年龄。外部效度:作者与按年龄性别加权的代表性样本比较,称 'at least in terms of observable characteristics, we find no large differences between the players we study and a representative sample of the Swedish population';中奖时均龄 48.6 岁;不可观测偏好(风险、闲暇偏好)无法排除。

## W02 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:AER 正式版摘要:收入下降「温和」;校准生命周期模型给出终生边际收入倾向(MPE)随年龄从 20 岁的 -0.17 降到 60 岁的 -0.04(WP 版报告的是平均 -0.11);劳动供给弹性处于既往估计低端。
- 原文:Winning a lottery prize modestly reduces earnings, with the reduction being immediate, persistent, and quite similar by age, education, and sex. A calibrated dynamic model implies lifetime marginal propensities to earn out of unearned income from -0.17 at age 20 to -0.04 at age 60, and labor supply elasticities in the lower range of previously reported estimates.
- URL:https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.20151589
- 来源:Cesarini, Lindqvist, Notowidigdo, Östling, 2017, The Effect of Wealth on Individual and Household Labor Supply: Evidence from Swedish Lotteries, American Economic Review 107(12) · 2017-12 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:MPE 是'终生'口径(结构模型外推到前10年之外),不可与 Golosov 的'当期每$1非劳动收入'MPE(-0.5)直接比:前者分母是一次性财富总额,后者分母是年化的非劳动收入流。'modestly' 是作者措辞,外推到 UBI 需谨慎。

## W03 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:调整边际:中 100 万 SEK 使参与率(年劳动收入>25,000 SEK)下降 2.07 个百分点;外延边际(退出)在中奖后即刻约占总响应的 50%,6 年后约 1/3;工时下降相当于全职的 3.11%(约每周 1.3 小时)——多数人减量而非辞职。
- 原文:Winning 1M SEK reduces the probability of participation, defined as having labor earnings in excess of 25,000 SEK, by 2.07 percentage points.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w21762
- 来源:Cesarini, Lindqvist, Notowidigdo, Östling, 2017, The Effect of Wealth on Individual and Household Labor Supply: Evidence from Swedish Lotteries, American Economic Review 107(12)(NBER WP 21762 Table 4 讨论) · 2017-12 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:'外延边际占比'依赖'退出者收入等于样本均值'的假设(作者自述)。工时数据只来自薪资调查子样本。对'不工作的人'这一议题:绝大多数中奖者并未退出劳动力。

## W04 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:配偶效应:已婚中奖者每中 100 SEK 少挣 0.98 SEK,未中奖配偶少挣 0.46 SEK,家庭合计 1.44;只看中奖者会低估家庭劳动供给响应约 24%;中奖者反应强于配偶(p=0.059),与单一家庭模型(收入共享)不符。
- 原文:Table 7 shows the labor supply of married winners drops by 0.98 SEK for each 100 SEK won, compared to 0.46 for their spouses. The labor supply of the household is hence reduced by 1.44 SEK per 100 SEK.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w21762
- 来源:Cesarini, Lindqvist, Notowidigdo, Östling, 2017, The Effect of Wealth on Individual and Household Labor Supply: Evidence from Swedish Lotteries, American Economic Review 107(12)(NBER WP 21762 Table 7) · 2017-12 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:Golosov 等指出家庭内谁中奖并非随机(中奖者更可能是男性、在业、主要收入者),Cesarini 自己的 Table A.6 也有此证据,故'违背单一模型'的推论有识别保留。

## W05 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:美国彩票中奖者(IRS 全量税务记录 × 第三方申报的州彩票奖金,1999–2016,事件研究用'晚中奖者'作对照):当期每多 $1 非劳动收入,税前劳动收入减少约 50 美分、劳动税减少 10 美分、消费增加 60 美分;高收入分位减得更多。
- 原文:On average, an extra dollar of unearned income in a given period reduces pre-tax labor earnings by about 50 cents, decreases total labor taxes by 10 cents, and increases consumption by 60 cents.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w29000
- 来源:Golosov, Graber, Mogstad, Novgorodsky, 2024, How Americans Respond to Idiosyncratic and Exogenous Changes in Household Wealth and Unearned Income, Quarterly Journal of Economics 139(2)(引自 NBER WP 29000 修订版) · 2024-05 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:口径:'unearned income'是把一次性奖金按年金化折成每年可分配的非劳动收入后计算的 MPE;按财富口径则是每 $100 财富年收入少 $2.3。作者为芝大+IRS SOI 合作,无预注册。样本是中奖者(自选购彩),奖金 ≥$20k 级别。与 Cesarini '温和' 结论看似冲突,主要来自分母口径不同;作者在引言直接反驳:'they conclude that income effects are modest. This conclusion, in turn, led a number of studies to build models specifically around the assumption that income effects are essentially negligible'。其他可能差异:税制社保、奖金规模(每$效应随奖金增大而减小)。

## W06 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:每多 $10 万财富,中奖者就业概率下降 3.7 个百分点;最低收入四分位下降 5.2pp,最高四分位 2.3pp——低收入者更可能完全停工,但以美元计高收入者减得更多。
- 原文:Finally, the probability that the winner is employed decreases by 3.7 percentage points per 100,000 dollars of additional wealth.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w29000
- 来源:Golosov, Graber, Mogstad, Novgorodsky, 2024, How Americans Respond to Idiosyncratic and Exogenous Changes in Household Wealth and Unearned Income, Quarterly Journal of Economics 139(2)(引自 NBER WP 29000 修订版)(Table 3.2) · 2024-05 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:Table 3.2:Winner Employment 全样本 -0.0368(SE 0.0008),Q1 -0.0517(0.0021),Q4 -0.0231(0.0010),均按每 $100,000 缩放。低收入者基期参与率低造成'天花板'问题,作者在附录做了子样本检验。

## W07 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:临近退休(62–64 岁)中奖者:每 $10 万财富使连续 ≥1/2/5 年退出劳动力的概率上升约 5 个百分点(相对反事实均值 +11%–14%),而提前领取社保(OASI)只升约 1.1pp(作者称不显著,表中 SE 0.0041)。
- 原文:In contrast, the propensity to leave the labor force for at least one, two or five consecutive years increases by around 5 percentage points per $100,000 of additional wealth on average.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w29000
- 来源:Golosov, Graber, Mogstad, Novgorodsky, 2024, How Americans Respond to Idiosyncratic and Exogenous Changes in Household Wealth and Unearned Income, Quarterly Journal of Economics 139(2)(引自 NBER WP 29000 修订版)(Table 6.1) · 2024-05 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:Table 6.1 一年退出 0.0489(SE 0.0053),反事实均值 0.43。正文称 OASI 效应 'statistically insignificant',但表中 0.0114/0.0041 看似显著——正文与表存在出入,待核对 QJE 终版。这是'财富→提前退休'的直接证据,把彩票线与退休线连起来。

## W08 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:把估计映射到含非线性税、内外延边际的生命周期模型后,作者给出 UBI 下界:即便不计为融资而加税带来的抑制,每 $1 UBI 至少使总劳动收入减少 52 美分;若马歇尔收入弹性为 0.3,UBI 几乎一比一挤出收入;$12,000/年的 UBI 会显著降低平均家庭收入(约 $5 万)。
- 原文:For example, even if one abstracts from any disincentive effects from higher taxes that are needed to finance this transfer program, each dollar of UBI will reduce total earnings by at least 52 cents. With the relatively modest disincentive effects of a Marshallian earnings elasticity of 0.3, a UBI crowds out earnings essentially one for one.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w29000
- 来源:Golosov, Graber, Mogstad, Novgorodsky, 2024, How Americans Respond to Idiosyncratic and Exogenous Changes in Household Wealth and Unearned Income, Quarterly Journal of Economics 139(2)(引自 NBER WP 29000 修订版) · 2024-05 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这是模型外推(部分均衡、个体冲击),不是 UBI 实验结果。个体中奖不改变同伴、工资、社会规范与需求——全民 UBI 有一般均衡与规范效应,方向不定。'劳动收入下降'本身不等于福利下降(闲暇/照料价值未计)。

## W09 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:马萨诸塞彩票(1980 年代中期购彩者,奖金分 20 年按年支付):非劳动收入降低劳动收入,'消费闲暇的边际倾向'约 11%,55–65 岁效应更大;拿到约一半奖金时储蓄约 16%。
- 原文:We find that unearned income reduces labor earnings, with a marginal propensity to consume leisure of approximately 11 percent, with larger effects for individuals between 55 and 65 years old. After receiving about half their prize, individuals saved about 16 percent.
- URL:https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aer.91.4.778
- 来源:Imbens, Rubin, Sacerdote, 2001, Estimating the Effect of Unearned Income on Labor Earnings, Savings, and Consumption: Evidence from a Survey of Lottery Players, American Economic Review 91(4):778-794 · 2001-09 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:奖金为 20 年年金,形式上最接近'定期无条件收入流',但有期限。资助 NSF+Sloan,彩票委员会配合。WP 版:年 $15,000 的'小奖'对工时无显著影响,$80,000 vs $15,000 才降工时(弹性约 -0.2);有基期零收入者在小奖后参与率反而上升的迹象。邮寄调查总应答率约 46%(中奖者 42%、未中者 49%),样本数百人;Cesarini 等脚注指其估计 'highly non-linear' 且对少数 >$200 万中奖者与非随机无应答敏感(Hirano & Imbens 2004)。
