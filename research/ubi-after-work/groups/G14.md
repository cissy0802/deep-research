# G14 退休对健康/认知/精神健康的因果证据;失业者退休

## W17 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Rohwedder & Willis(HRS/ELSA/SHARE 跨国比较,用各国养老金/税收/残障政策作工具变量):提前退休对 60 岁出头者的认知能力有显著、量化重要且因果的负效应('mental retirement')。
- 原文:We find that early retirement has a significant negative impact on the cognitive ability of people in their early 60s that is both quantitatively important and causal. Identification is achieved using national pension policies as instruments for endogenous retirement.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20975927/
- 来源:Rohwedder & Willis, 2010, Mental Retirement, Journal of Economic Perspectives 24(1):119-138(PubMed 20975927 / PMC2958696 摘要) · 2010-01 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:识别是国家层面的政策差异(约 13 国截面),作者自称'informal'论证;易受国家层面混杂(教育、文化、测量)影响。Bonsang 等 2012 JHE 用美国社保领取年龄作 IV 也得到负效应;Coe 等 2012 得到相反结果(W18)。效应幅度原文未抓到(PMC 有 reCAPTCHA)。

## W18 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:反方:Coe 等用 HRS 中雇主提供的提前退休窗口作工具变量,OLS 显示退休时长与认知负相关,但 IV 估计表明并非因果;白领无清晰关系,蓝领若有则为正。
- 原文:Specifically, we find no clear relationship between retirement duration and later-life cognition for white-collar workers and, if anything, a positive relationship for blue-collar workers.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21818822/
- 来源:Coe, von Gaudecker, Lindeboom, Maurer, 2012, The effect of retirement on cognitive functioning, Health Economics 21 · 2012-08 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:仅老年男性。说明认知效应方向取决于识别策略与职业类型:'工作维持心智'对重复性体力劳动者可能不成立。

## W19 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Insler(HRS,以首期自报'62/65 岁后仍工作的概率'作工具变量):退休对健康有显著有益效应,部分通过更健康的习惯(戒烟、运动)实现。
- 原文:Results indicate that the retirement effect on health is beneficial and significant. Investigation into behavioral data, such as smoking and exercise, suggests that retirement may affect health through such channels.
- URL:https://ideas.repec.org/a/uwp/jhriss/v49y2014i1p195-233.html
- 来源:Insler, 2014, The Health Consequences of Retirement, Journal of Human Resources 49(1):195-233 · 2014-01 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:工具变量是个人主观预期,排他性约束存疑(预期本身可能反映健康轨迹)。健康口径是一般健康指数,不是幸福或认知。

## W20 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Fé & Hollingsworth(BHPS,英国):短期用断点设计(对弱工具稳健),长期用强假设参数模型;发现退休对健康影响很小,但退休可能开启'久坐、社交贫乏'的生活,构成长期间接风险通道。
- 原文:We apply our framework to the British Household Panel Survey and find that retirement has little effect on health. However, our estimates suggest that retirement opens the gate to a sedentary life with an impoverished social component and this is a channel through which retirement could indirectly affect health in the long run.
- URL:https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/55561/
- 来源:Fé & Hollingsworth, 2016, Short- and long-run estimates of the local effects of retirement on health, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series A 179(4):1051-1067 · 2016-10 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:标题即强调'local':断点只识别在法定领取年龄附近被诱导退休的人(complier)的短期效应;长期效应要靠作者自称的'strong assumptions'。

## W21 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Kolodziej & García-Gómez(SHARE,利用各国各时期退休资格年龄差异):退休对精神健康有保护作用,且分布不均——在抑郁风险临床阈值附近的人获益更大,女性与蓝领更大。
- 原文:We find unequally distributed protective effects of retirement on mental health. These gains are larger among those just below and above the clinically defined threshold of being at risk of depression. The preserving effects are larger for women and blue collar workers.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30822608/
- 来源:Kolodziej & García-Gómez, 2019, Saved by retirement: Beyond the mean effect on mental health, Social Science & Medicine · 2019-02 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:brief 中的'Kolodziej & García-Gómez 2019'核实为 Soc Sci Med 而非 Econ & Human Biology。精神健康口径为 EURO-D 抑郁量表(从摘要'risk of depression'推断,未读正文)。与 Heller-Sahlgren 用相近数据和识别却反号。

## W22 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Heller-Sahlgren(SHARE 十国,以法定养老金年龄阈值作 IV + 个体固定效应,借鉴断点思路):退休对精神健康无短期效应,但有'大的'长期负效应,对男女及不同教育/职业背景同样成立。
- 原文:The results display no short-term effects of retirement on mental health, but a large negative longer-term impact.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28505541/
- 来源:Heller-Sahlgren, 2017, Retirement blues, Journal of Health Economics 54 · 2017-07 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:作者结论带政策倾向('reforms inducing people to postpone retirement ... could also pay a mental health dividend')。与 Kolodziej(保护)及 van der Heide(有益)冲突:差异可能来自时间窗(短 vs 长)、样本国家与波次、精神健康量表、是否以首次跨过阈值定义。'长期'的具体年数正文未抓到。

## W23 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:van der Heide 等系统综述(22 项纵向研究,11 项高质量):有'强证据'表明退休有益精神健康,对一般健康与身体健康证据矛盾;很少有研究区分自愿/非自愿/法定退休及蓝白领。
- 原文:Strong evidence was found for retirement having a beneficial effect on mental health, and contradictory evidence was found for retirement having an effect on perceived general health and physical health. Few studies examined the differences between blue- and white-collar workers and between voluntary, involuntary and regulatory retirement with regards to the effect of retirement on health outcomes.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24330730/
- 来源:van der Heide, van Rijn, Robroek, Burdorf, Proper, 2013, Is retirement good for your health? A systematic review of longitudinal studies, BMC Public Health 13:1180 · 2013-12 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:纳入的是纵向观察研究(best evidence synthesis),多数不处理'健康差→退休'的反向因果,不等于因果证据。

## W24 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Filomena & Picchio 元分析(2000–2021 年 85 篇、308 个估计,含发表偏倚检验):退休对健康的平均效应'非常小且勉强显著';异质性来自健康测量与退休制度,强制或非自愿退休与(小的)负效应相关。
- 原文:Using meta-regression analysis and after checking for the presence of publication bias, we found that the average effect of retirement on health outcomes is very small and barely significant, under the assumption of a common true effect. ... In particular, mandatory or involuntary retirement is associated with a negative impact of retirement on health, although it is small in magnitude.
- URL:https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:bla:jecsur:v:37:y:2023:i:4:p:1120-1155
- 来源:Filomena & Picchio, 2023, Retirement and health outcomes in a meta-analytical framework, Journal of Economic Surveys 37(4):1120-1155 · 2023-09 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:元分析把身体健康、精神健康、自评健康、医疗利用、死亡率合并,口径混合;正文表格显示精神健康类 105 个估计中正向 33、负向 12、零 60。引语中省略号为本线所加,两句均为摘要原文。

## J28 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Hetschko、Knabe、Schöb(SOEP 1984–2009):就业者退休时生活满意度不变(约 7.2),长期失业者退休时满意度显著上升(6.1→6.5,696 人),尽管退休前后可支配收入几乎不变——作者解释为社会规范切换带来的身份效用上升。
- 原文:Using data from the German Socio-Economic Panel from 1984-2009, we follow persons from their working life into their retirement years and find that, on average, employed people maintain their life satisfaction upon retirement, while long-term unemployed people report a substantial increase in their life satisfaction when they retire.
- URL:https://www.diw.de/documents/publikationen/73/diw_01.c.379163.de/diw_sp0399.pdf
- 来源:Hetschko, Knabe & Schöb, 2011/2014, Changing Identity: Retiring from Unemployment, SOEPpapers 399 / Economic Journal 124(575):149-166 · 2014-03 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这是本线对 AI 情景最直接的证据:日常作息、收入、时间结构都几乎不变,仅『社会类别』从『该工作的人』变成『退休者』,满意度就回升。数字(6.1→6.5,n=696;就业者 n=2,115)取自工作论文表 1 描述统计,EJ 正式版未核。只针对老年、德国。也提示:若 AI 让『不工作』被社会重新分类为正当状态(类似退休),失业伤害中的规范部分可能消失,但仍不及就业者水平(6.5 vs 7.2)。
