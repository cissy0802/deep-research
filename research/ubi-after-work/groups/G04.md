# G04 Dauphin Mincome 与切罗基赌场分红

## U24 (US)
- 论断:Dauphin Mincome（加拿大，1974–79，非美国）：Forget 2011 用卫生行政数据与准实验匹配对照，报告参与者相对对照住院率下降 8.5%，尤其事故伤害与精神健康。
- 原文:it uses routinely collected health administration data and a quasi-experimental design to document an 8.5 percent reduction in the hospitalization rate for participants relative to controls, particularly for accidents and injuries and mental health.
- URL:https://ideas.repec.org/a/cpp/issued/v37y2011i3p283-305.html
- 来源:Evelyn L. Forget, 2011, The Town with No Poverty: The Health Effects of a Canadian Guaranteed Annual Income Field Experiment, Canadian Public Policy 37(3):283–305 · 2011-09 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:Dauphin 为饱和站点（全镇可申领），无随机对照；对照为马尼托巴其他地区匹配样本。Forget 本人 2012 简报另一表述称 8.5% 是起始差距收敛（secondhand，未核原文）。

## U25 (US)
- 论断:Green（2022）重估 Forget 2011：住院数据在 Dauphin 相对对照存在显著负向前趋势，考虑前趋势后标准设定下数据若有任何指向，反而是 Mincome 增加了住院使用；结论是 Mincome 数据不支持保障收入降低医疗成本。
- 原文:I reassess the hospital use data from Forget (2011), arguing that it contains statistically significant negative pre-trends in the relative hospital usage in the treated community of Dauphin compared to comparison communities. The pre-trend combined with short prior and treatment periods raises serious issues about the usefulness of the data, which, if anything shows positive effects of MINCOME on hospital usage in standard specifications.
- URL:https://ideas.repec.org/a/cpp/issued/v48y2022i4p539-548.html
- 来源:David A. Green, 2022, A Reanalysis of "The Town with No Poverty", Canadian Public Policy 48(4):539–548 · 2022-12 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:工作论文版（https://economics.ubc.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/38/2021/03/forget-cpp-reanalysis.pdf）措辞更强：'the data point to an increase in hospital use during MINCOME. This is the opposite of the conclusion reached in Forget(2011).' Forget 在同期 549–556 页有回应（未读，付费墙）。『-8.5% 住院率』是被广泛转引但已被同行重估质疑的数字。

## U26 (US)
- 论断:Calnitsky & Latner（2017）首次分析 Dauphin 档案调查数据（DiD）：劳动市场参与下降 11.3pp，其中近 30%（约 3.1pp）归因于『社区情境』效应；退出主要集中于年轻人和单亲户，自述原因为照护、残疾疾病、就业机会不均、教育投资。
- 原文:Using hitherto unanalyzed data we find an 11.3 percentage point reduction in labor market participation, and nearly 30 percent of that fall can be attributed to "community context" effects. Additionally, we show that withdrawals were driven disproportionately by young and single-headed households.
- URL:https://users.ssc.wisc.edu/~dcalnits/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/Calnitsky_Latner_Social_Problems_BI_in_a_small_town.pdf
- 来源:David Calnitsky & Jonathan P. Latner, 2017, Basic Income in a Small Town: Understanding the Elusive Effects on Work, Social Problems 64(3):373–397 · 2017-08 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:纠正『Mincome 无劳动数据』的说法：有调查档案，Calnitsky 等用了。设计为 DiD（Dauphin 前后 vs 马尼托巴对照），自报调查数据。11.3pp 的分母是参加 Mincome 调查的样本而非全镇居民（secondhand 提示，需核表）。饱和站点揭示社区溢出效应，这是分散抽样 RCT（ORUS 等）结构上看不到的。

## U27 (US)
- 论断:东部切罗基赌场分红（永久、无条件、全体成年部落成员、约 $4,000/年、每半年发放）：Akee 等用 Great Smoky Mountains 儿童纵向调查做 DiD，发现最贫困家庭额外 $4,000/年使子女 21 岁时教育多 1 年、16–17 岁轻罪率下降 22%；父母未减少工作时间。
- 原文:The additional income may allow the poorer households to substitute away from full-time employment towards part-time employment thus allowing for more child care. This does not appear to happen in our data; parents do not reduce their working time.
- URL:https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2891175/
- 来源:Akee, Copeland, Keeler, Angold, Costello, 2010, Parents' Incomes and Children's Outcomes: A Quasi-experiment Using Transfer Payments from Casino Profits, AEJ: Applied Economics 2(1):86–115 · 2010-01 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:是本线唯一『永久』转移，但局部（北卡西部 11 县，GSMS 样本 1,420 名儿童，其中美洲原住民子样本）。教育效应有混杂：原文 'American Indian children had an incentive to finish high school by age 18 as they became eligible for payment ... otherwise they would have to wait until age 21'，即带有条件现金转移成分。父母劳动供给结论来自『各波次是否就业』，不是精细工时数据。资助：NIDA/NIMH 等（GSMS）。
