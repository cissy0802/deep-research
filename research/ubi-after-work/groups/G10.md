# G10 低中收入国家劳动供给综述与 UBI 健康综述

## I23 (INTL)
- 论断:Banerjee 等再分析 7 项政府现金转移 RCT（洪都拉斯、印尼、摩洛哥、墨西哥×2、尼加拉瓜、菲律宾）：未发现系统性减少工作；合并估计就业系数 −0.003（95% CI −1.4pp 到 +0.8pp）。
- 原文:We re-analyze the data from 7 randomized controlled trials of government-run cash transfer programs in six developing countries throughout the world, and find no systematic evidence that cash transfer programs discourage work.
- URL:https://static.poder360.com.br/2024/09/artigo-transferencia-de-renda-desincentivo-trabalho.pdf
- 来源:Banerjee, Hanna, Kreindler, Olken, 2017, Debunking the Stereotype of the Lazy Welfare Recipient: Evidence from Cash Transfer Programs Worldwide, World Bank Research Observer 32(2):155–84 · 2017-08 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这些是定向贫困户、多为有条件现金转移（CCT），金额小、低收入国家，不是 UBI。来源 PDF 为第三方托管的工作论文版副本（MIT DSpace 需 JS），正文与 WBRO 版可能有细微差别。

## M01 (META)
- 论断:ODI 2016 综述(165 项研究进入抽取,其中就业 74 项;约 54% 为拉美项目):过半研究对成年人劳动无显著影响;显著者多数为增加;减少集中在老人、照护者与零工。
- 原文:For just over half of studies reporting on adult work, the cash transfer does not have a statistically significant impact on adult work. Among those studies reporting a significant effect among adults of working age, the majority find an increase in work participation and intensity. In the cases where a reduction in work participation or work intensity is reported, these reflect a reduction in participation among the elderly, those caring for dependents or are linked to reductions in casual work.
- URL:https://media.odi.org/documents/11316.pdf
- 来源:Bastagli, Hagen-Zanker, Harman, Barca, Sturge, Schmidt, Pellerano, 2016, Cash transfers: what does the evidence say?, ODI · 2016-07 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:系统性综述(rigorous review)非 meta,方法是计数「显著/不显著/方向」(vote counting),不给合并效应量;LMIC 定向、多为有期限项目;资助方:'This research has been funded by UK aid from the UK government'(DFID)。

## M02 (META)
- 论断:ODI 细分:14 项报告成年人总体劳动参与,其中 8 项针对劳动年龄成人,4 项显著(3 增 1 减);增加劳动时间的两例是乌干达 YOP、WINGS 这类专门为就业设计的创业补助。
- 原文:14 studies report on the effect on overall adult labour force participation: among the eight that report on adults of working age, four found statistically significant impacts, three being increases and one a decrease.
- URL:https://media.odi.org/documents/11316.pdf
- 来源:Bastagli et al., 2016, Cash transfers: what does the evidence say?, ODI · 2016-07 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:分母很小(8 项),「多数为增加」实际是 3 vs 1;且增劳动的例子是带就业目标的创业补助,不能外推到无条件全民 UBI。另见同报告:'The two interventions resulting in increases in time spent working resulted from large enterprise grants in Uganda – YOP and WINGS'。

## M03 (META)
- 论断:Baird–McKenzie–Özler 2018 综述:在 LMIC,无明确就业目标的 CCT/UCT 与侨汇对成年人劳动「几乎无变化」;例外是给老人和部分难民的转移会减少工作;创业/求职类转移反而增加劳动与收入。
- 原文:Overall, cash transfers that are made without an explicit employment focus (such as conditional and unconditional cash transfers, and remittances) tend to result in little to no change in adult labor. The main exceptions are transfers to the elderly and to some refugees, who reduce work.
- URL:https://izajodm.springeropen.com/track/pdf/10.1186/s40176-018-0131-9
- 来源:Baird, McKenzie, Özler, 2018, The effects of cash transfers on adult labor market outcomes, IZA Journal of Development and Migration 8:22 · 2018-10 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:叙述性综述,无 meta 合并值;作者自己指出 LMIC 有缺失市场、条件价格效应、动态与一般均衡等渠道,使简单劳动-闲暇模型失效——这正是不能直接外推到高收入国家的理由。引语取自 OpenAlex 摘要(与期刊摘要一致);Springer 全文页有 bot 挑战未读正文。McKenzie、Özler 为世界银行研究员。

## M04 (META)
- 论断:Baird 等指出基础劳动供给模型预测意外之财会让人「少工作少挣钱」,并以此作为检验框架——即「懒惰」担忧有理论依据,LMIC 证据未支持它主要是因为额外渠道。
- 原文:The basic economic model of labor supply has a very clear prediction of what we should expect when an adult receives an unexpected cash windfall: they should work less and earn less.
- URL:https://izajodm.springeropen.com/track/pdf/10.1186/s40176-018-0131-9
- 来源:Baird, McKenzie, Özler, 2018, IZA J Dev & Migration · 2018-10 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:反方锚点:高收入国家无缺失市场时,标准收入效应更可能显现(对照 M15 美国试点大样本 -3.2pp)。

## M05 (META)
- 论断:Evans & Popova meta:19 项定量研究,现金转移使烟酒等「诱惑品」总支出平均下降 0.18 SD;拉美/非洲/亚洲、CCT 与 UCT 皆如此。
- 原文:Results show that on average cash transfers have a significant negative effect on total expenditures on temptation goods, equal to −0.18 standard deviations. This negative result is supported by data from Latin America, Africa, and Asia, for both conditional and unconditional cash transfer programs.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1086/689575
- 来源:Evans & Popova, 2017, Cash Transfers and Temptation Goods, Economic Development and Cultural Change 65(2) · 2017-01 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:meta-analysis;单位是 SD 不是百分点;支出多为自报,可能受「社会期望」与项目宣传(social messaging)影响,作者自己在摘要中把 social messaging 列为渠道之一;另有 11 项为受访者自述用途调查。期刊页 403,引语取 OpenAlex 摘要。作者为世界银行研究员。

## M18 (META)
- 论断:Gibson–Hearty–Craig 2020 Lancet Public Health:尚无真正符合定义的 UBI 研究;对 27 项「类 UBI」研究(9 种干预,中上/高收入国家)的 scoping review 发现健康效应混合(出生体重、精神健康有强正效应),就业效应不一致,男性多很小,幼儿母亲较大。
- 原文:Evidence on health effects was mixed, with strong positive effects on some outcomes, such as birthweight and mental health, but no effect on others. Employment effects were inconsistent, although mostly small for men and larger for women with young children.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1016/s2468-2667(20)30005-0
- 来源:Gibson, Hearty, Craig, 2020, The public health effects of interventions similar to basic income: a scoping review, Lancet Public Health 5(3):e165–e176 · 2020-03 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:scoping review 不做质量加权和 meta;纳入 Alaska PFD、Mincome、美国 NIT 等,混合 RCT 与准实验;「幼儿母亲就业下降较大」可理解为转向照护,是否为福利损失取决于价值判断。作者为格拉斯哥大学 MRC/CSO 社会与公共卫生科学单位。

## M19 (META)
- 论断:Gibson 等结论:几乎没有证据显示就业大幅下降,宏观层面证据稀缺。
- 原文:In conclusion, little evidence exists of large reductions in employment, and some evidence suggests positive effects on some other outcomes, including health outcomes. Evidence for macro-level effects is scarce.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1016/s2468-2667(20)30005-0
- 来源:Gibson, Hearty, Craig, 2020, Lancet Public Health · 2020-03 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:「无大幅下降」≠「无下降」;2020 年前 OpenResearch 等大样本美国 RCT 尚未发布,需与 M15 对照。
