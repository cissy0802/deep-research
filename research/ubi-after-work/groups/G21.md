# G21 科技领袖与倡导者的 UBI 口径

## V01 (VOICES)
- 论断:【倡议+预测】Altman 2021 提出「American Equity Fund」:对超过某估值的公司每年征市值 2.5%(以股份缴纳)、对全部私有土地征价值 2.5%(以美元缴纳),18 岁以上公民每年领现金+股份;他预测十年后 2.5 亿成年人每人约 $13,500/年。
- 原文:The American Equity Fund would be capitalized by taxing companies above a certain valuation 2.5% of their market value each year, payable in shares transferred to the fund, and by taxing 2.5% of the value of all privately-held land, payable in dollars. All citizens over 18 would get an annual distribution, in dollars and company shares, into their accounts. ... a decade from now each of the 250 million adults in America would get about $13,500 every year.
- URL:https://moores.samaltman.com/
- 来源:Sam Altman, 2021, Moore's Law for Everything, 个人网站 · 2021-03 · 【厂商】 · 表态 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:说话人:OpenAI CEO(时任),本人自称「in the spirit of a conversation starter」——是倡议不是承诺。$13,500 是基于其自设的估值增长假设的预测,且他另断言技术会让 $13,500 的购买力大增,这一步无实证。机制上是财富税+土地税→全民股息,而非 Yang 式 VAT;按公司股份缴税意味着 OpenAI 这类高估值公司自身也被稀释——但当时 OpenAI 为 capped-profit 非上市结构,适用性不明。全民、永久、无条件;与本期实测(局部、有期限、定向低收入)不是同一种东西。

## V02 (VOICES)
- 论断:【表态】Altman 2024-05 在 All-In 播客把「universal basic income」改口为「universal basic compute」:每人分一份 GPT-7 的算力,可自用、转卖或捐赠;同段承认「钱不会解决所有问题,肯定不会让人快乐」。时点在 OpenResearch 结果(2024-07)公布前两个月。
- 原文:money is not going to go solve all problems it's certainly not going to make people happy but it might it might solve some problems ... I wonder if the future looks something like more like Universal basic compute than Universal basic income and everybody gets like a slice of gpt7 compute and they can use it they can resell it they can donate it to somebody to use for cancer research but but what you get is not dollars but this like productivity slice
- URL:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSM0xd8xHUM
- 来源:All-In Podcast, 2024-05-10, Sam Altman: Getting Fired (and Re-Hired) by OpenAI, Agents, AI Copyright issues(YouTube 原视频) · 2024-05 · 【厂商】 · 表态 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:逐字依据为 YouTube 自动字幕(非人工转写),媒体版本(Business Insider/TechSpot)措辞基本一致。谁受益:分发物是 OpenAI 自家产品的算力,等于把再分配载体换成发起人公司的产品——利益冲突最直接的一条。只是「I wonder」式设想,无机制、无规模、无资金来源。同段他说「2016 was a very long time ago」,即公开从现金 UBI 立场后撤。他也称自己「not a super fan of how the government has handled most policies designed to help poor people」。

## V03 (VOICES)
- 论断:【预测+倡议】Altman 2025 两篇博客:承认 AGI 可能打乱「资本与劳动的力量平衡」需「早期干预」,提出给每人「compute budget」,但同时认为单纯把智能成本压到最低可能也能达到目的;并预测「整类工作会消失」,但「社会契约」会渐进改变。
- 原文:it does seem like the balance of power between capital and labor could easily get messed up, and this may require early intervention. We are open to strange-sounding ideas like giving some “compute budget” to enable everyone on Earth to use a lot of AI, but we can also see a lot of ways where just relentlessly driving the cost of intelligence as low as possible has the desired effect.
- URL:https://blog.samaltman.com/three-observations
- 来源:Sam Altman, 2025, Three Observations;另见 The Gentle Singularity(2025-06, https://blog.samaltman.com/the-gentle-singularity) · 2025-02 · 【厂商】 · 表态 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:Gentle Singularity 原文:「There will be very hard parts like whole classes of jobs going away ... We probably won’t adopt a new social contract all at once」。口径演变:2021 现金+股份 → 2024 算力切片 → 2025 「compute budget」或干脆「降低智能成本」即可——后者等于把再分配问题还原为 OpenAI 的产品定价问题,无需任何转移支付。全部是表态,无承诺、无金额。

## V12 (VOICES)
- 论断:【倡议】Yang 2020「Freedom Dividend」:18 岁以上公民每人每月 $1,000、无条件;资金来自 10% VAT 与合并部分福利项目,且现有福利领取者须在原福利与 $1,000 之间二选一,「不会两者都拿」。
- 原文:Current welfare and social program beneficiaries would be given a choice between their current benefits or $1,000 cash unconditionally – most would prefer cash with no restriction. ... people already receiving benefits would have a choice between keeping their current benefits and the $1,000, and would not receive both.
- URL:https://web.archive.org/web/2020/https://www.yang2020.com/what-is-freedom-dividend-faq/
- 来源:Yang2020 竞选网站, The Freedom Dividend, Defined(Wayback 存档) · 2020-01 · 【厂商】 · 表态 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:竞选承诺(campaign promise),非政策。二选一设计意味着对最穷的福利领取者净增量可能接近 0,增量主要流向不领福利的人——与 Hoynes & Rothstein 的「替代 vs 叠加」分析(V21/V22)正好对上。网页称「1 out of 3 American workers are at risk of losing their jobs」为未注明来源的预测。Tax Foundation 估算 10% VAT 只够约一半成本(二手)。
