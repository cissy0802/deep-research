# G18 食利国家理论与石油诅咒跨国实证

## R01 (RENTIER)
- 论断:食利国家的原始定义（Mahdavy 1970）是财政口径：国家「定期获得大量外部租金」，与靠向本国公民征税的「税收国家」相对；定义本身不含政治后果。
- 原文:Rentier States are defined here as those countries that receive on a regular basis substantial mounts [sic: amounts] of external rent
- URL:VRÜ 4 (1971) S.105 书评 PDF（verfassungsvergleich.de VRÜ 存档；具体 URL 未保留）
- 来源:Herbert, 1971, Ein neuer Staatstyp – der 'Rentierstaat'（评 Mahdavy 1970 'The Patterns and Problems of Economic Development in Rentier States: the Case of Iran', in Cook ed.）, Verfassung und Recht in Übersee 4 · 1971-01 · 【研究】 · 论证 · secondhand
- 备注:引语是 1971 年德文书评逐字转引 Mahdavy 原书 p.428（OCR 把 amounts 识别成 mounts）；未见 Mahdavy 原章节扫描。Mahdavy 关注的是经济发展（消费挤占投资），政治议价权推论是后来 Beblawi/Luciani 加上去的。

## R02 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Beblawi 1987 把食利国家定义为：只有少数人生产租金、多数人只参与分配，且政府是外部租金的主要接收者；他把它与 Luciani 的「生产型 vs 分配型国家」挂钩。
- 原文:Fourth, a corollary of the role of the few, in a rentier state the government is the principal recipient of the external rent in the economy.
- URL:https://www.jstor.org/stable/41857943
- 来源:Hazem Beblawi, 1987, The Rentier State in the Arab World, Arab Studies Quarterly 9(4):383-398（同名文亦为 Beblawi & Luciani 1987 编著第 3 章底本） · 1987-10 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:p.385 页面图像逐字核对。同页：「No more than 2 to 3 per cent of the labor force is engaged in the production and distribution of the oil wealth」(p.386)——口径是海湾产油国，非全体食利国。

## R03 (RENTIER)
- 论断:「无代表不纳税」反向口号的原始措辞：Beblawi 1987 原文只写「几乎不收税，公民对政治参与的要求就低得多」，括号里引的是正向口号 no taxation without representation；广为流传的「no representation without taxation」不在此文中。
- 原文:With virtually no taxes, citizens are far less demanding in terms of political participation. The history of democracy owes its beginnings, it is well known, to some fiscal association (no taxation without representation).
- URL:https://www.jstor.org/stable/41857943
- 来源:Hazem Beblawi, 1987, The Rentier State in the Arab World, Arab Studies Quarterly 9(4), p.387 · 1987-10 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:页面图像逐字核对（p.387）。反向口号「no representation without taxation」常被归给 Huntington 1991《The Third Wave》（据称 p.65），本次未拿到该页原文，标 unverified；Herb 2005 Comparative Politics 论文以此为标题加问号。写作时不要把反向口号当作 Beblawi 原话。Beblawi 后任埃及总理（2013-14），属学者-官员身份。

## R04 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Ross 2001：113 国 1971–1997 合并时序截面数据，石油依赖（燃料出口/GDP）对民主有稳健负效应，在穷国更强，且不限于中东；非燃料矿产也有同样效应。
- 原文:First, the oil-impedes-democracy claim is both valid and statistically robust; in other words, oil does hurt democracy. Moreover, oil does greater damage to democracy in poor states than in rich ones, and a given rise in oil exports will do more harm in oil-poor states than in oil-rich ones.
- URL:https://pages.ucsd.edu/~mnaoi/page4/POLI227/files/page1_28.pdf
- 来源:Michael L. Ross, 2001, Does Oil Hinder Democracy?, World Politics 53(3):325-61 · 2001-04 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:跨国面板（FGLS、年份虚拟变量、5 年滞后，无国家固定效应——这正是 Haber & Menaldo 攻击点）。石油测量 = 燃料出口值/GDP（分母含 GDP，会把穷国机械地标为高依赖）。作者写作时为世界银行访问学者。

## R05 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Ross 2001 对「税收效应」的直接检验：加入税收变量（个人+公司所得税）后，石油系数下降 17%，税收系数显著为正——税收渠道只解释了部分反民主效应，且数据覆盖 104/113 国。
- 原文:the inclusion of Taxes produces a 17 percent drop in the Oil coefficient, which implies that the taxation effect may account for part of the antidemocratic influence of Oil.
- URL:https://pages.ucsd.edu/~mnaoi/page4/POLI227/files/page1_28.pdf
- 来源:Michael L. Ross, 2001, Does Oil Hinder Democracy?, World Politics 53, Table 5 讨论 · 2001-04 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:作者自己说因果检验数据「incomplete and potentially biased, so the results should be treated as suggestive, not conclusive」。同文引 Waterbury：中东历史上几乎看不到税负转化为民主化压力——反例在源头就存在。机制三分：rentier（税收+支出）/repression/modernization。

## R06 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Ross 2012 年本人口述：石油诅咒在约 1980 年前不存在；1970 年代前产油国并不更不民主。他把转折归因于 1970 年代国有化让政府直接攫取租金。
- 原文:Interestingly enough, we didn't observe these problems before about 1980. Up until the 1970s the oil-producing countries were no less democratic, no more conflict-ridden, than the rest of the world
- URL:https://media-1.carnegiecouncil.org/import/studio/The_Oil_Curse.pdf
- 来源:Michael L. Ross, 2012-03-01, The Oil Curse: How Petroleum Wealth Shapes the Development of Nations（Carnegie Council 演讲转写） · 2012-03 · 【研究】 · 表态 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这是书《The Oil Curse》(2012, Princeton UP) 的作者口述版，书正文未取得。同场：「Much of the oil curse began with these waves of nationalization」。书中改用「人均油气收入」而非出口/GDP。

## R07 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Ross 2012 同场把税收-问责机制讲成「Petrolia vs Industria」：石油国政府规模约为同收入非石油国的 2–3 倍，且收入不来自向人民征税，因此躲开审视；他另把「保密性」列为关键渠道。
- 原文:the revenues that Petrolia gets don't come from taxing the people. In Industria, the government has to fund itself. It has to go regularly to the people and ask for some sort of tax contribution, and people may object, they may despise it, but when they pay their taxes they demand some accountability.
- URL:https://media-1.carnegiecouncil.org/import/studio/The_Oil_Curse.pdf
- 来源:Michael L. Ross, 2012, The Oil Curse（Carnegie Council 演讲转写） · 2012-03 · 【研究】 · 表态 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:「2–3 倍」是口头估计，无表格口径。同场：「no country with as much oil wealth as Libya or Iraq, no country with half as much oil wealth as those, has ever made a successful transition to democracy」。对 AI 类比重要：Ross 把「收入规模+不靠征税+波动+保密」四者并列，税收只是其一。

## R08 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Haber & Menaldo 2011：用 1800 年起的历史数据、国家内时序方法，资源依赖上升与威权化不相关，许多设定甚至呈「资源祝福」。
- 原文:We find that increases in resource reliance are not associated with authoritarianism. In fact, in many specifications we generate results that suggest a resource blessing.
- URL:https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/abs/do-natural-resources-fuel-authoritarianism-a-reappraisal-of-the-resource-curse/0DC2DEF9747F89CA6894A6245E80B6F7
- 来源:Stephen Haber & Victor Menaldo, 2011, Do Natural Resources Fuel Authoritarianism? A Reappraisal of the Resource Curse, APSR 105(1):1-26 · 2011-01 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:关键口径差异：国家内（within）长期关系 + 国家固定效应 vs Ross 的跨国截面变异。批评对象是「random effects + short time dimensions」。2009 年 Stanford CID WP 386 版摘要措辞不同（无「resource blessing」）。资金：Stanford 内部基金。

## R09 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Andersen & Ross 2014 对 Haber–Menaldo 的回应：H-M 结论只在 1970 年代前成立；允许约 1980 年结构断点后，石油显著抑制民主转型——因为 1970 年代合同改写/国有化让东道国政府夺取租金（「big oil change」）。
- 原文:contract revisions that enabled the governments of host countries to seize control of these rents. We refer to this transformation as "the big oil change."
- URL:https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0010414013488557
- 来源:Jørgen J. Andersen & Michael L. Ross, 2014, The Big Oil Change: A Closer Look at the Haber–Menaldo Analysis, Comparative Political Studies 47(7):993-1021 · 2014-06 · 【研究】 · 观察 · paraphrase
- 备注:SAGE 403、CORE 下载被拦，引语来自检索摘要片段，未逐字核对正文；另一摘要片段为「since about 1980, there has been a pronounced resource curse」。注意 Ross 是合著者，等于原作者回应批评，不是独立复核。对 AI 类比的含义：诅咒取决于『谁控制租金』，而非资源本身。

## R10 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Ahmadov 2014 meta 回归：全球平均方向为「不可忽视的负相关」，但随地区与制度背景显著异质；调节作用主要来自更广泛的经济政治制度，不是先前政体或英国殖民遗产。
- 原文:While the results suggest a nontrivial negative association between oil and democracy across the globe, they also indicate a notable variation in this relationship across world regions and institutional contexts.
- URL:https://www.geg.ox.ac.uk/publication/oil-democracy-and-context-meta-analysis-comparative-political-studies-xxx-2013-pp-1-30
- 来源:Anar Ahmadov, 2014 (online 2013-07), Oil, Democracy, and Context: A Meta-Analysis, Comparative Political Studies 47(9) · 2013-07 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:meta 分析（纳入 29 项研究，据后续引用）。Lall 批评：29 项中 27 项用 listwise deletion 处理缺失值——meta 继承了原研究的缺失数据偏差。摘要另说石油不通过收入/城市化抑制民主，但可能经教育间接作用。

## R11 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Ahmadov 博士论文版 meta 的量级与测量敏感性：30 项研究 262 个估计，偏相关中位数约 −0.07（加权均值 −0.05 至 −0.09，属「小」效应）；用人均油气租金测量时负效应更一致，加入国家固定效应则估计更偏正。
- 原文:Ross (2008) suggests a better measure – oil and gas rents per capita. The MRA shows that, across various studies, this variable has a consistently negative and statistically significant coefficient. ... Introducing country-fixed effects, on the other hand, can result in more positive estimates, although the record is mixed.
- URL:https://researchonline.lse.ac.uk/id/eprint/131659/
- 来源:Anar Ahmadov, 2011, A conditional theory of the 'political resource curse': oil, autocrats, and strategic contexts, LSE PhD thesis, ch.2 & conclusion · 2011-01 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:数字取自论文表（Median -0.07；Weighted Average (N) -0.05/-0.06；Q1 加权 -0.08/-0.09），是偏相关系数而非民主分数变化。论文版 30 项研究，期刊版 29 项。另：「in Latin America oil actually helped sustain democracy for several decades」(Dunning 2008 支持)。测量口径决定结论方向，这是本线最大口径陷阱。

## R12 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Wiens, Poast & Clark 2014：166 国 1816–2006 动态 logit——资源依赖上升降低威权国家民主化概率（短期与长期），但对既有民主的存续无明显影响。即诅咒是『阻止进入民主』而非『摧毁已有民主』。
- 原文:We find that an increase in resource dependence decreases an autocracy's likelihood of being democratic over both the short-term and long-term, but has no appreciable effect on democracies' likelihood of persisting.
- URL:https://escholarship.org/uc/item/6hm3q66n
- 来源:David Wiens, Paul Poast & William Roberts Clark, 2014, The Political Resource Curse: An Empirical Re-evaluation, Political Research Quarterly 67(4) · 2014-12 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:关键调节变量 = 资源到来时的政体。对 AI 版：若把石油文献当类比，其最强证据只支持『AI 租金会让威权更难民主化』，对已成熟民主的预测力弱——这与 Intelligence Curse 自己「democracy is likely a necessary precondition」的判断一致。

## R13 (RENTIER)
- 论断:Mehlum, Moene & Torvik 2006：资源对收入的效应取决于制度——「掠夺者友好」制度下资源压低收入，「生产者友好」制度下资源提高收入；博茨瓦纳（钻石约占 GDP 40%）与挪威是正例。
- 原文:More natural resources push aggregate income down, when institutions are grabber friendly, while more resources raise income, when institutions are producer friendly.
- URL:https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/faculty/jones-ben/ftp/ExtraReadings/Mehlum%20et%20al%20Institutions%20and%20Nat%20Resource%20EJ%202006.pdf
- 来源:Halvor Mehlum, Karl Moene & Ragnar Torvik, 2006, Institutions and the Resource Curse, Economic Journal 116:1-20 · 2006-01 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:因变量是经济增长不是民主/议价权，别混用。正文：「Botswana, with 40% of GDP stemming from diamonds, has had the world's highest growth rate since 1965」；「the resource curse only appears in countries with inferior institutions」。制度测量本身可能受资源影响（内生），作者承认未解决。
