# G07 Haushofer-Shapiro 及长期效应衰减

## I12 (INTL)
- 论断:Haushofer & Shapiro 2016：九个月后受助户月消费从 $158 PPP 升至 $193 PPP；心理福祉指数 +0.26 SD，幸福感 +0.16 SD、生活满意度 +0.17 SD、压力 −0.26 SD；酒烟支出负且不显著。
- 原文:Transfers have a sizable effect on psychological well-being; in particular, we document a 0.16 std. dev. increase in happiness, a 0.17 std. dev. increase in life satisfaction, a 0.26 std. dev. reduction in stress, and a significant reduction in depression (all measured by psychological questionnaires).
- URL:https://haushofer.ne.su.se/publications/Haushofer_Shapiro_UCT_QJE_2016.pdf
- 来源:Haushofer & Shapiro, 2016, The Short-term Impact of Unconditional Cash Transfers to the Poor: Experimental Evidence from Kenya, QJE 131(4):1973–2042 · 2016-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:一次性/分期大额（$404 或 $1,525 PPP），定向（茅草屋顶），有期限。心理量表为自报。有预分析计划（偏离项在附录 A.3 列出）。QJE 2017 年 132(4) 刊有勘误（secondhand，未读原文）；2018 随访稿复述的数字为 0.18/0.13/0.23，与 2016 正文不同，可能来自勘误或不同对照组口径——引用时需标版本。Shapiro 为 GiveDirectly 联合创始人。

## I13 (INTL)
- 论断:Haushofer & Shapiro 2016 预注册与多重检验处理：分析写入预分析计划，并用指数变量与 FWER 校正。
- 原文:we address issues of multiple inference by prespecifying the analyses and by using index variables and family-wise error rate correction (FWER).
- URL:https://haushofer.ne.su.se/publications/Haushofer_Shapiro_UCT_QJE_2016.pdf
- 来源:Haushofer & Shapiro, 2016, The Short-term Impact of Unconditional Cash Transfers to the Poor: Experimental Evidence from Kenya, QJE 131(4):1973–2042 · 2016-11 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:方法透明度高，但不改变「自报 + 短期（9 个月）」的口径限制。

## I14 (INTL)
- 论断:Haushofer & Shapiro 三年随访：跨村比较只有资产显著（+40%，$422 PPP，约为初始转移的 60%），消费、心理福祉等其他结果跨村比较不显著。
- 原文:Comparing recipient households to non-recipients in distant villages, we find that transfer recipients have 40% more assets (USD 422 PPP) than control households three years after the transfer, equivalent to 60% of the initial transfer (USD 709 PPP). In contrast, other outcomes do not show significant treatment effects in the across-village analysis
- URL:https://www.povertyactionlab.org/sites/default/files/research-paper/The-long-term-impact-of-conditional-cash-tranfer_Kenya_Haushofer_Shapiro_January2018.pdf
- 来源:Haushofer & Shapiro, 2018-01, The Long-Term Impact of Unconditional Cash Transfers: Experimental Evidence from Kenya, 工作论文 · 2018-01 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:「心理福祉效应衰减」的严格说法：村内比较仍有效应，但跨村（干净对照）不显著——可能是功效不足，也可能是溢出。作者自己标注 remain to be substantiated。工作论文，未见正式发表。

## I15 (INTL)
- 论断:三年随访溢出：同村未受助户比纯对照户消费少约 $30 PPP（约 16%）、食物安全低约 0.25 SD、心理福祉指数低约 0.18 SD；经 Lee bounds 后仅消费与食物安全溢出稳健。
- 原文:Spillover households also score ˜0.18 SD less on an index of psychological well-being than pure control households. On the positive side, they score 0.16 SD higher on an index of female empowerment. When applying Lee Bounds to assess whether attrition may drive these spillover results, the consumption and food security spillovers are robust, but we do not see conclusive evidence of spillovers on other dimensions.
- URL:https://www.povertyactionlab.org/sites/default/files/research-paper/The-long-term-impact-of-conditional-cash-tranfer_Kenya_Haushofer_Shapiro_January2018.pdf
- 来源:Haushofer & Shapiro, 2018-01, The Long-Term Impact of Unconditional Cash Transfers: Experimental Evidence from Kenya, 工作论文 · 2018-01 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:brief 所说「溢出伤害非受助者生活满意度」严格来自另一篇 Haushofer, Reisinger & Shapiro「Your Gain is My Pain」（未取到一手 PDF，作者站 404）；本文的心理溢出在 Lee bounds 下不稳健。且与 Egger et al. 2022 的正溢出（消费）方向相反——差异在于定向 vs 全村饱和、样本与时点。

## M12 (META)
- 论断:Ridley 等引 Haushofer & Shapiro:肯尼亚 400–1,500 美元(PPP,约 3–12 个月家庭收入)的转移,抑郁量表在发完 4 个月后低 0.12 SD、近三年后低 0.16 SD。
- 原文:Scores on a depression scale were 0.12 standard deviations (SD, closely related to Cohen’s d) lower 4 months after completion and 0.16 SD lower after almost three years, with larger transfers causing substantially larger effects.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w27157.pdf
- 来源:Ridley et al., 2020, NBER WP 27157 · 2020-05 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:三年值是「村内」比较(受助者 vs 同村未受助者),而 H&S 自己指出跨村比较(对纯对照村)除资产外不显著——Ridley 的转述未交代这一点,见 M14。

## M13 (META)
- 论断:Blattman–Fiala–Martinez 乌干达 YOP:每人约 400 美元创业补助,4 年时劳动 +17%、收入 +38%;9 年时这些增益消失,两组在就业、收入、消费上趋同,但耐用资产与技能工作仍有持久影响。
- 原文:Four years on, an experimental evaluation found grants raised work by 17 percent and earnings by 38 percent (Blattman, Fiala, Martinez 2014). After nine years, we find these gains have dissipated.
- URL:https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/aeri.20190224
- 来源:Blattman, Fiala, Martinez, 2020, The Long-Term Impacts of Grants on Poverty: Nine-Year Evidence from Uganda's Youth Opportunities Program, AER: Insights 2(3):287–304 · 2020-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:「消失」机制是对照组追上('controls eventually increased their incomes through business and casual labor'),不是受助者倒退——是「加速」而非「抬升长期水平」。一次性、团体申请的创业补助,对象为青年,非 UBI。

## M14 (META)
- 论断:Haushofer & Shapiro 肯尼亚 GiveDirectly 三年随访:村内比较,受助者资产、消费、粮食安全、心理福祉均高于同村未受助者;跨村比较只有资产显著(+40%,422 美元 PPP),其他结果不显著。
- 原文:Comparing recipient households to non-recipients in distant villages, we find that transfer recipients have 40% more assets (USD 422 PPP) than control households three years after the transfer, equivalent to 60% of the initial transfer (USD 709 PPP). In contrast, other outcomes do not show significant treatment effects in the across-village analysis, possibly owing to lower power and within-village spillovers.
- URL:https://www.povertyactionlab.org/sites/default/files/research-paper/The-long-term-impact-of-conditional-cash-tranfer_Kenya_Haushofer_Shapiro_January2018.pdf
- 来源:Haushofer & Shapiro, 2018-01, The Long-Term Impact of Unconditional Cash Transfers: Experimental Evidence from Kenya (working paper, J-PAL hosted) · 2018-01 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:J-PAL 文件名误写为 'conditional',正文是无条件。溢出组消费与粮食安全低于纯对照('Households impacted by spillovers have lower consumption and food security than pure control households')——负外部性。资助:NIH R01AG039297 + 匿名捐赠者;与 GiveDirectly 合作,Shapiro 为 GiveDirectly 联合创始人(本文未声明,来自公开资料,secondhand)。迄今仍是工作论文,未见正式发表版。

## M15 (META)
- 论断:Baird–McIntosh–Özler 马拉维:针对少女与年轻女性的两年期现金转移,UCT 在项目期内降低 HIV、少女怀孕、早婚的效果在停发两年后迅速消失;CCT 带来持续的教育与生育下降,但无健康、劳动力市场或赋权收益。
- 原文:Significant declines in HIV prevalence, teen pregnancy, and early marriage among recipients of unconditional cash transfers (UCTs) during the program evaporated quickly two years after the cessation of transfers.
- URL:https://escholarship.org/uc/item/2rd3f9jv
- 来源:Baird, McIntosh, Özler, 2019, When the money runs out: Do cash transfers have sustained effects on human capital accumulation?, Journal of Development Economics 140 (CEGA WP-068 版) · 2019-05 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:引语取 CEGA 工作论文版(2016-11),JDE 正式版 doi 10.1016/j.jdeveco.2019.04.004 摘要未取到;另一持久收益:UCT 期间所生子女身高别年龄 z 分数更高。'When the money runs out' 是对「有期限转移」最直接的检验。

## M16 (META)
- 论断:Araujo–Bosch–Schady(厄瓜多尔 BDH,非 Progresa):10 年后,早发组虽多拿一倍转移,儿童各项测验无差异;断点设计下中学完成率仅提高 1–2 个百分点(基线 75%),代际减贫效应「可能有限」。
- 原文:Transfers increased secondary school completion, but the effects are small, between 1 and 2 percentage points from a counterfactual school completion rate of 75 percent. We conclude that any effect of cash transfers on the inter-generational transmission of poverty in Ecuador is likely to be modest.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.18235/0000618
- 来源:Araujo, Bosch, Schady, 2016, Can Cash Transfers Help Households Escape an Inter-Generational Poverty Trap?, IDB Working Paper(后收入 NBER/UChicago Press 2018 文集) · 2016-09 · 【研究】 · RCT · verbatim-checked
- 备注:brief 写的「Araujo 等 Progresa 长期」应更正为厄瓜多尔 Bono de Desarrollo Humano;一半是 RCT(早 vs 晚处理),一半是断点(准实验)。作者为美洲开发银行/世界银行经济学家。

## M17 (META)
- 论断:Molina-Millán 等 2019 综述 CCT 长期效应:多数研究发现长期正面的受教育年限,较少发现认知/学习/社会情感技能提升;就业与收入影响「好坏参半」,很多零效应无法区分真无效与方法局限。
- 原文:Most studies find positive long-term effects on schooling, but fewer find positive impacts on cognitive skills, learning, or socio-emotional skills.Impacts on employment and earnings are mixed, possibly because former beneficiaries were often still too young.
- URL:https://doi.org/10.1093/wbro/lky005
- 来源:Molina-Millán, Barham, Macours, Maluccio, Stampini, 2019, Long-Term Impacts of Conditional Cash Transfers: Review of the Evidence, World Bank Research Observer 34(1) · 2019-02 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:OpenAlex 摘要句间缺空格为原数据格式,文字未改。只管 CCT 与儿童/青年人力资本,不是成年人劳动供给。
