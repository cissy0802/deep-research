# G08 全民/永久项目:伊朗、阿拉斯加(劳动侧)与 PFD 法定设计

## I16 (INTL)
- 论断:伊朗 2011 全民现金转移：2011 年转移额约为 GDP 6.5%、家庭收入中位数 29%；除青年外未发现劳动供给下降，服务业工人工时反而增加。
- 原文:With the exception of youth, who have weak ties to the labor market, we find no evidence that cash transfers reduced labor supply, while service sector workers appear to have increased their hours of work, perhaps because some used transfers to expand their business.
- URL:https://erf.org.eg/app/uploads/2017/05/1090.pdf
- 来源:Salehi-Isfahani & Mostafavi-Dehzooei, 2017, Cash Transfers and Labor Supply: Evidence from a Large-Scale Program in Iran, ERF WP 1090（正式版 J Dev Econ 135:349–67, 2018） · 2017-05 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:全国全民、无条件、名义上永久——但它是取消能源补贴的补偿（物价同时上涨），并非纯额外收入；且三年通胀后降至人均 GDP 3% 以下。识别靠处理强度（转移/上年收入）与领取时点差异的 DiD + 固定效应，非随机。

## I17 (INTL)
- 论断:伊朗：20–29 岁青年工时有负效应；作者无法观察时间使用，不知道青年是否用钱读书。
- 原文:We do find a negative labor supply effect for workers 20-29 years old for their hours worked. This is not surprising since the attachment of Iranian youth to the labor market is weak and many have the option to enroll in tertiary and graduate education
- URL:https://erf.org.eg/app/uploads/2017/05/1090.pdf
- 来源:Salehi-Isfahani & Mostafavi-Dehzooei, 2017, Cash Transfers and Labor Supply: Evidence from a Large-Scale Program in Iran, ERF WP 1090（正式版 J Dev Econ 135:349–67, 2018） · 2017-05 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:「劳动未降」是平均口径，青年是反例。女性劳动样本小（16%），作者提醒谨慎解读。

## I18 (INTL)
- 论断:阿拉斯加永久基金分红（1982 起，全民永久）：合成控制法下就业率无影响，兼职比例 +1.8pp（+17%）。
- 原文:Using data from the Current Population Survey and a synthetic control method, we show that the dividend had no effect on employment, and increased part-time work by 1.8 percentage points (17%).
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24312/w24312.pdf
- 来源:Jones & Marinescu, 2018, The Labor Market Impacts of Universal and Permanent Cash Transfers: Evidence from the Alaska Permanent Fund, NBER w24312（正式版 AEJ: Economic Policy 2022） · 2018-02 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:全民、永久，但金额小：名义 1984 年低至 $331，1996 年后一般>$1,000，2015 年峰值 $2,072/人/年。n=1 个州的合成控制，推断靠置换检验；只有就业率与兼职率两个结果找到了好的合成对照（优于≥68% 其他州）。

## I19 (INTL)
- 论断:阿拉斯加：兼职增加可解读为集约边际劳动减少，但作者承认也可能是新进入者以兼职形式进入劳动力市场。
- 原文:With respect to our findings on the rate of part-time employment, the results suggest that there is a reduction in labor supply on the intensive margin. However, our confidence intervals for the extensive margin of labor supply do not rule out positive employment effects
- URL:https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24312/w24312.pdf
- 来源:Jones & Marinescu, 2018, The Labor Market Impacts of Universal and Permanent Cash Transfers: Evidence from the Alaska Permanent Fund, NBER w24312（正式版 AEJ: Economic Policy 2022） · 2018-02 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:作者偏好的解释是一般均衡（本地需求刺激非贸易部门就业）抵消了个人收入效应——即「全民」与「局部」的效应不可互推。

## V23 (VOICES)
- 论断:【官方/制度设计】阿拉斯加 PFD 的「分红」并不在宪法里:宪法第 9 条第 15 节只规定至少 25% 矿产收入进入永久基金、本金只能投资、收益「除法律另有规定外存入普通基金」;州最高法院 2017 年裁定基金收益「受正常拨款与否决程序约束」,支持州长 2016 年把分红砍到 $1,022;2025 年分红由立法机关 HB 53 定为 $1,000。
- 原文:The legislature's use of Permanent Fund income is subject to normal appropriation and veto budgetary processes. ... Absent another constitutional amendment, the Permanent Fund dividend program must compete for annual legislative funding just as other state programs.
- URL:https://caselaw.findlaw.com/ak-supreme-court/1872118.html ; https://dor.alaska.gov/department-of-revenue/news-detail/2025/09/22/department-of-revenue-announces-2025-permanent-fund-dividend-amount
- 来源:Wielechowski v. State, 403 P.3d 1141 (Alaska 2017);Alaska Department of Revenue, 2025-09-22 新闻稿 · 2025-09 · 【官方】 · 官方数据 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:宪法原文(经判决引用):「All income from the permanent fund shall be deposited in the general fund unless otherwise provided by law.」2016:立法拨款 $13.62 亿,州长否决到 $6.9565 亿,分红约为预期一半。DOR:「The amount of this year’s PFD was determined by the Alaska Legislature in House Bill 53」,发放对象 60 余万人。二手:法定公式下 2025 应约 $3,892。启示:全民、永久、无条件的唯一美国先例,其金额每年由政治决定——「永久」只是法律惯性,不是宪法保障;这对 Altman 式「全民股息」的可持续性是直接反例。AS 37.13.145 法条原文与 2018 POMV(5% 提取)法条未抓。
