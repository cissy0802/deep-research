# G13 彩票中奖者的福祉、Brickman 1978 与「中奖后还工作吗」

## W10 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:瑞典购彩者调查(中奖后 5–22 年,N=3,362,奖池 $2.77 亿,按预注册分析计划 OSF t3qb5):税后每 $10 万奖金使总体生活满意度提高 0.037 SD,且随中奖后年数不衰减——拒绝'完全享乐适应'。
- 原文:According to our estimate, an after-tax prize of $100,000 improves life satisfaction by 0.037 standard-deviation (SD) units. We find no evidence that the effect varies by years-since-win, suggesting a limited role for hedonic adaptation over the time horizon we analyze.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w24667
- 来源:Lindqvist, Östling, Cesarini, 2020, Long-run Effects of Lottery Wealth on Psychological Well-being, Review of Economic Studies 87(6)(引自 NBER WP 24667, 2018) · 2020-11 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:预注册:Östling, Lindqvist & Cesarini 2016, https://osf.io/t3qb5/ 。未调查中奖 5 年内的人,不能检验短期适应。效应量小:0.037 SD/$10万,作者称与年收入梯度相当。伦理审批 2016/524-31/5。

## W11 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:测量口径分化:同一样本中评价型指标(总体生活满意度 0.037 SD、财务满意度 0.067 SD/$10 万)显著;情感型指标(幸福感 0.016、GHQ-12 精神健康 0.013)不显著,且可拒绝与生活满意度等效(p<0.001),尽管两者相关 0.86;财务满意度是主要中介。
- 原文:The estimated effects on Overall LS and Financial LS are, respectively, 0.037 SD units and 0.067 SD units per $100,000 won, and remain significant after our multiple-hypothesis adjustment. For Happiness and Mental Health, the corresponding point estimates are 0.016 and 0.013, respectively.
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w24667
- 来源:Lindqvist, Östling, Cesarini, 2020, Long-run Effects of Lottery Wealth on Psychological Well-being, Review of Economic Studies 87(6)(引自 NBER WP 24667, 2018) · 2020-11 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:这是本线最关键的口径陷阱:'钱让人更满意'≠'钱让人更快乐/精神更健康'。Happiness 在本文是混合型单题,Mental Health 用 GHQ-12。写作中'幸福'一词必须标注是 life satisfaction 还是 affect。

## W12 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Lindqvist 等重估既往彩票-幸福研究:在其精度下,报告精神健康显著改善的三项早期研究(Lindahl 2005、Gardner & Oswald 2007、Apouey & Clark 2015)统计功效仅 5.02%–6.55%,显著结果有 15%–45% 概率符号错误、效应至少被高估 5 倍。
- 原文:Then the statistical power of the three previous studies reporting statistically significant effects on mental health (Lindahl 2005, Gardner & Oswald 2007, Apouey & Clark 2015) ranged from 5.02% to 6.55% (at α = 0.05).
- URL:https://www.nber.org/papers/w24667
- 来源:Lindqvist, Östling, Cesarini, 2020, Long-run Effects of Lottery Wealth on Psychological Well-being, Review of Economic Studies 87(6)(引自 NBER WP 24667, 2018)(footnote 16) · 2020-11 · 【研究】 · 论证 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:该功效计算假设早期研究的真实效应等于本文 95% CI 上限(0.044 SD),作者自承可能不适用(短期效应可能更大、小奖非线性)。但它是对'彩票改善精神健康'早期证据的直接方法批评。

## W13 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Brickman 等 1978(伊利诺伊):22 名大奖得主 vs 22 名同区对照 vs 29 名此前访谈的瘫痪事故受害者;得主不比对照更快乐,且从日常琐事中得到的乐趣更少。WDH 收录的原表数值:当前幸福(0–5)得主 4.00、对照 3.82、事故受害者 2.96,受害者显著低于两组——'截瘫者恢复到和中奖者一样快乐'是流行误读。
- 原文:Study 1 compared a sample of 22 major lottery winners with 22 controls and also with a group of 29 paralyzed accident victims who had been interviewed previously. As predicted, lottery winners were not happier than controls and took significantly less pleasure from a series of mundane events.
- URL:https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/690806/
- 来源:Brickman, Coates, Janoff-Bulman, 1978, Lottery winners and accident victims: Is happiness relative?, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology 36(8):917-927 · 1978-08 · 【研究】 · 观察 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:摘要逐字核对(PubMed)。数值来自 World Database of Happiness 条目(https://worlddatabaseofhappiness-archive.eur.nl/correlational-findings/413/:'Accident victims: M=2.96 ... Controls: M=3.82 ... Both difference with lottery winners and controls significant'),属二手整理,原文 PDF 未取得。受害者为面访、得主/对照为电话访问(模式差异);横截面、非随机、得主奖金 ≥$5 万、应答率低(WDH 记 ~48% 无应答)。Lindqvist 等将其重标后得 0.014(SE 0.025),与他们的 Happiness 估计接近——即'无效应'在情感型指标上大体被现代研究支持,但对生活满意度不成立。

## W14 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Gardner & Oswald(英国 BHPS,中奖 £1,000–£120,000):与无中奖、小额中奖两组对照相比,中奖者'最终'心理健康显著更好,中奖两年后 GHQ 平均改善 1.4 分。
- 原文:When compared to two control groups – one with no wins and the other with small wins – these individuals go on eventually to exhibit significantly better psychological health. Two years after a lottery win, the average measured improvement in mental wellbeing is 1.4 GHQ points.
- URL:https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:iza:izadps:dp2233
- 来源:Gardner & Oswald, 2007, Money and mental wellbeing: A longitudinal study of medium-sized lottery wins, Journal of Health Economics 26(1):49-60(IZA DP 2233 摘要) · 2007-01 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:中奖者样本很小(约百余人)、奖金'中等'、对照非同彩种内随机;Lindqvist 等认为功效极低(见 W12)。测量是 GHQ-12(情感/精神健康),与生活满意度不同口径。

## W15 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Apouey & Clark(英国 BHPS 面板,个体固定效应):中奖带来的正收入冲击对自评总体健康无显著影响,但显著改善精神健康;同时中奖与更多吸烟和社交饮酒相关,作者以此解释两者背离。
- 原文:Positive income shocks have no significant effect on self-assessed overall health, but a significant positive effect on mental health.
- URL:https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:hal:pseptp:halshs-01155641
- 来源:Apouey & Clark, 2015, Winning Big but Feeling no Better? The Effect of Lottery Prizes on Physical and Mental Health, Health Economics 24(5):516-538 · 2015-05 · 【研究】 · 准实验 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:Lindqvist 等引述:效应出现在中奖两年后的测量,而非中奖次年。同样面临小样本功效问题。'钱→更健康行为'不成立:摘要原文 'lottery winnings are also associated with more smoking and social drinking'。

## W16 (WINDFALL)
- 论断:Morse & Weiss 1955(美国就业男性全国样本)提出'若继承足够财富是否仍会工作'问题,结论是工作对大多数人不只是谋生手段;Vecchio 1980 用 1970 年代 GSS 重做,多数人仍会继续工作,但'纯为经济目的'工作的比例上升。常被引的'约 80% 会继续工作'未能回到原文核对。
- 原文:While the new study finds that the majority of workers still work for the aforementioned reasons, the proportion of people who work solely for economic purposes is greater.
- URL:https://onwork.edu.au/bibitem/1980-Vecchio,Robert+P-The+Function+and+Meaning+of+Work+and+the+Job+Morse+and+Weiss+(1955)+Revisited
- 来源:Vecchio, 1980, The Function and Meaning of Work and the Job: Morse and Weiss (1955) Revisited, Academy of Management Journal 23(2):361-367(经 On Work 书目库摘要);原始:Morse & Weiss 1955, ASR 20(2):191-198 · 1980-06 · 【研究】 · 调查 · secondhand
- 备注:引语来自书目库对 Vecchio 的摘要转述,非原文;Morse & Weiss 原文在 JSTOR(2088325)付费墙后,'80%'数字未核实。这是假设性问题(说的),与 Cesarini/Golosov 的行为数据(做的)互补:真中奖者也大多没有辞职。Arvey 等 2004 已由 JOBLESS 线收录(85.5% 中奖后继续工作,应答率约 16%)。

## J30 (JOBLESS)
- 论断:Arvey、Harpaz、Liao(美国彩票中奖者邮寄调查,有效 117 人,应答率约 16%):85.5% 中奖后继续工作,仅 14.5% 辞职;工作中心度、中奖金额及二者交互显著预测是否继续工作。
- 原文:The majority of lottery winners in our sample contin- ued to work after winning (n = 100 or 85.5%); 17 individuals opted to quit work- ing (14.5%).
- URL:https://web2-bschool.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/media_rp/publications/W2SWe1422938751.pdf
- 来源:Arvey, Harpaz & Liao, 2004, Work Centrality and Post-Award Work Behavior of Lottery Winners, The Journal of Psychology 138(5):404-420 · 2004-09 · 【研究】 · 调查 · verbatim-checked
- 备注:作者主页版 PDF 页眉误印 1996,正式版为 2004。样本小、应答率 16%,中奖额多数不足以终身无忧;文中转引 Kaplan(1978)『nearly 80% of $1 million dollar winners quit working』,说明金额足够大时多数人辞职——『继续工作』高度依赖金额。MOW(1987)八国研究原书未取得,工作中心度构念出自该书(本文转述)。
