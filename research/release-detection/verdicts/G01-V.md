## 逐条

**C01** 两句均逐字命中 sre.google/sre-book/introduction/。70% 句确在「Change Management」小标题下,后接 progressive rollouts / 快速检测 / 安全回滚三件套。原文无分母、无时间窗——论断描述准确。HOLDS。

**C02** 附录 C 全文逐字命中:"based on a sample of thousands of postmortems over the last seven years";Table C-1 标题 "Top eight outage triggers, 2010–2017",八项百分比全部一致(37/31/9/6/5/5/5/2)。Table C-2 原标题为 "Top five root-cause categories for outages"(引导句 "top five contributing root-cause categories"),五项数字一致。小修:写作时须说明 C-2 只是 top five、加总不到 100%。HOLDS。

**C08** "We analyzed 1247 headline news ... 597 unplanned outages ... 2009 to 2015" 逐字命中。"Table 3 shows that UPGRADE is one of the largest root causes (16%)" 命中(PDF 小型大写字母拆成 "U PGRADE",归一化后成立)。"355 outages (out of the total 597) have UNKNOWN root causes" 命中。Table 3 百分比 16/15/15/10/9/8/6/5/4/4/3/3/1 与论断完全一致,表注写明 "% the count percentage among known root causes"。多标签已证实:"an outage can be tagged with more than one root causes"。「开发者测试过升级」的出处:"The developers had tested the software upgrades, but when the updates were pushed to the full ecosystem, they caused new failures/anomalies that were undetected in the offline testing."。HOLDS。

**C09** "we manually analyzed the high-impact incidents during a three-month period. We found that 16% ..." 逐字命中;Type I 42% / II 36% / III 22% 一致;论文为 "Holistic Configuration Management at Facebook",作者署 Facebook Inc.。HOLDS。

**C19** 四句均逐字命中 how.complexsystems.fail。HOLDS(注意:这是论证性短文,不是数据)。

**C26** 三句引语均逐字命中 Uptime 新闻稿。上下文补充:23% 句紧接 "Power remains the leading cause of impactful outages."。三分之二原文为 "Over the nine years that Uptime has been tracking publicly reported outages, third-party IT and data center service providers ... have accounted for about two-thirds of those reported"——口径是「公开报道的 outage」,与 23%(调查口径)不同源。**新闻稿中搜不到 "54%"**,这一数字只能标「未验证(媒体转述)」,不得与逐字引语并列。CORRECTED。

**C28** 摘要逐字命中:"about 50.4% of incidents are caused by bad changes, mainly because of code defect, configuration error, resource contention, and software version";"achieving 0.95 F1-score on average and reducing MTTD (mean time to detect) by 20.4%∼60.7%"。摘要只写 "a large commercial bank";作者单位含 Tsinghua、Tianjin Univ、BizSeer、China Guangfa Bank。HOLDS。

**C29** 两句引语逐字命中 arXiv 2604.13462(ICSE-SEIP '26, Rio de Janeiro, April 12–18, 2026;数据为 ING 一年的变更)。修正:56% 是基线规则的**加权召回 wR**,不是普通召回;原文 "a moderate wR of 56%, and an AUC of 0.56, indicating poor discriminative performance",AUC 0.56 接近随机。CORRECTED(措辞)。

**CANARY:C11** 三句均命中(第一句只差括号前后的空格,属排版问题)。HOLDS。

## 成文时必须使用的锁定口径
1. 「约 70%」= SRE 书 Change Management 小节的经验断言,无分母、无窗口;能追溯到数据的最近数字是 Workbook 附录 C:binary push 37% + config push 31% = 68%,口径为「2010–2017 数千份 postmortem 抽样中的 top 8 trigger」,不是 root cause。
2. 「change」在 SRE 书中包括 "a new type of user traffic"。
3. COS(SoCC'16):16% UPGRADE 的分母是**已知根因**的标签计数(可多标签),597 起中 355 起根因未知;样本来自公开新闻/复盘。
4. Facebook 16% 是「配置管理相关」,不是「变更触发」;窗口为 3 个月,未给总数。
5. Uptime:23% 为 IT/网络类,电力仍是第一大原因;「三分之二归第三方」是九年公开报道口径;54% 电力**未验证**,不得引用。
6. 广发银行研究 50.4%:单一银行;MTTD 降 20.4–60.7% 是离线数据集上相对基线的作者自评。
7. ING:基线 wR 56%、AUC 0.56;所有模型精度都低(告警疲劳)。
