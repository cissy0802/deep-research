- **H23 HOLDS(带口径补充)**:原文「1. No UML (35/50); 2. Retrofit (1/50)… 3. Automated code generation (3/50)…」,另两类 selective 11/50、wholehearted 0/50;复算 35+1+3+11+0 = 50 ✓。方法节:「a series of interviews conducted over 2 years with more than 50 practicing professional software developers… Only one informant per company was included in the reported data, reducing the sample size to 50」——「50 家公司各 1 人」成立。口径补充:(1) 招募为「identified opportunistically, via networks of collaborators… LinkedIn」——机会抽样;(2) 方法节还记录一位审稿人认为 retrofit 应并入 no UML(即「不用」可读作 36/50);(3)「wholehearted 0」是**当前**使用的计数,文中另有两名受访者所在公司**过去**推行过全面采用、均未持续,另有若干二手报告——写「受访者中无人当前全面使用」,不要写「没有公司全面采用过」。
- **H24 HOLDS**:「A number of informants identified issues of synchronization or consistency as a barrier for wholesale adoption of UML.」逐字在;「A number of」无计数——禁写比例。文中另引 Lange 等调查「A further 64% reported that it happened ‘sometimes’」并称「systematic approaches to maintaining correspondence are rarely used in practice」——这是**他人调查的转引**,若要用须回 Lange 原文,不能算 Petre 的数据。
- **H25 HOLDS**:「productivity increases ranging anywhere from 20 to 800% [18, 5]」「quantified at 27% in [18]」「Of the comparative studies, results vary between a 35% gain to a 27% loss. Two of the studies showed no net impact.」逐字在;样本「questionnaire… over 250 responses; in-depth interviews with 22 MDE professionals from 17 different companies」逐字在。作者自限「Using largely qualitative questionnaire and interview methods」「these lessons should be interpreted with care」。口径:+20~800% 是**文献宣称**区间,+35%~−27% 是**比较研究**区间,两者不得合并成「MDE 生产率 −27% 到 +800%」后当作测量。
- **H26 HOLDS(带口径补充)**:摘要「we surveyed 450 MDE practitioners and carried out in-depth interviews with 22 more… developers rarely use it to generate whole systems; rather, they apply it to develop key parts of a system often using domain-specific modeling languages」;正文「from a 27% loss to an 800% gain [6]… most companies seem to experience productivity increases of between 20-30%」「code generation is a red herring when it comes to describing MDE」「code certification costs rose by a factor of eight」均逐字在。口径:(1) 2011 年 250+ 份与 2014 年 450 份是**同一研究项目的不同时点**,不是两个独立样本,不得相加或当两源互证;(2) 「20–30%」是「seem to」的归纳,非均值;(3)「8 倍」是单一政府信息系统案例;(4) 分母只有 MDE 实践者(幸存者偏差)。对 SDD 主张 3 最有用的一手判断是「red herring」句:在 MDE 中,真正的收益来自强迫写清架构,而非代码生成本身。
- **H27 修正(原引语按伪引用处理)+ 升级为 verbatim**:OMG omg/2003-06-01 全文检索「enough information」「drive one or more」0 命中;§2.2.12 原文定义为「A platform independent model is a view of a system from the platform independent viewpoint. A PIM exhibits a specified degree of platform independence so as to be suitable for use with a number of different platforms of similar type.」§2.2.13「A PSM combines the specifications in the PIM with the details that specify how that system uses a particular type of platform.」原件前言中最接近「spec 可再生代码」的承诺是:「we have the ability to automate at least some of the construction… when a new, more fire-resistant type of steel is invented, we can regenerate for the new infrastructure. This is the promise of Model Driven Architecture: to allow definition of machine-readable application and data models which allow long-term flexibility of…」以及「since the developed models can be used to generate code」。§2.1.2:「The three primary goals of MDA are portability, interoperability and reusability」。修正前「PIM 包含足以驱动一个或多个 PSM 的信息…即『模型为唯一事实源、代码可再生』的前代版本」→ 修正后「MDA 的官方承诺是**机器可读模型 + 至少部分自动构建 + 换平台时再生成**,目标是可移植/互操作/复用;原件并未宣称模型是唯一事实源」。与 Tessl spec-as-source 的类比只能落在「换实现时从模型再生成」这一点上。利益相关:贡献者名单含 IBM、Rational、Oracle、HP 及多家建模工具厂商——厂商口径。

## 成文提示
- 必须带的限定:Petre = 50 人定性访谈、机会抽样;Hutchinson/Whittle = MDE 实践者自选样本、定性为主;两篇 MDE 文是同一项目。
- 禁止:「MDA 承诺模型是唯一事实源」(原件无此句);「MDE 使生产率提高 20–800%」;「70% 的 CASE 工具一年后无人使用」;把 Petre 的 0 写成「从未有公司全面采用」。
- 最强写法:「Petre 2013 访谈 50 家公司各一名专业开发者:35 人完全不用 UML,11 人选择性地把它当草图用完即弃,3 人用于代码生成,当前全面采用者为 0;同步与一致性被多人列为障碍。MDE 产业研究的结论则是:代码生成是『转移注意力的话题』,真正的收益在于被迫把架构写清楚——这正是 SDD 支持者最该认领、也最该警惕的历史经验。」
