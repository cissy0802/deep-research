# G16 — Lens K(口径)

**组判定:修正**(Binamungu、Nagappan、Fucci 的数字在一手 PDF 中定位、复算无误;Hellesøy 与 Zampetti 两条 secondhand 补到一手(后者为官方摘要),Zampetti 的样本「矛盾」实为三阶段不同样本,已解;但 Zampetti 的「27% 采用」是**框架存在**口径,须修正表述;Rafique/Bissi 仍未取得)

核验底稿:作者站 PDF https://lepebina.github.io/maintaining.pdf(pdftotext);tpn/pdfs 中 Nagappan 等 2008 PDF(pdftotext -layout);arXiv 1611.05994(Fucci 等,直抓 PDF);cucumber.io 原帖 https://cucumber.io/blog/collaboration/the-worlds-most-misunderstood-collaboration-tool/(直抓 HTTP 200,日期 March 3, 2014);Zampetti 2020 摘要取自 Semantic Scholar API(DOI 10.1016/j.infsof.2020.106311,全文未取得)。

- **H29 HOLDS(带口径补充)**:摘要「We surveyed 75 BDD practitioners from 26 countries」;§III「We received a total of 82 responses. We removed 7 responses… leaving 75 valid responses」;§V「organizations (20% of respondents) that have made BDD a mandatory tool, a significant proportion (61% of respondents) use it as an optional tool」;§IV.C「61% of the respondents held the view that the presence of duplication in BDD specifications can cause them to become difficult to extend and change (leading potentially to frozen functionality)」;「Anecdotal evidence… are leading some teams to drop the technique」均逐字在。**口径警告**:两个「61%」是不同题项(一个是「可选使用」占比,一个是「认为重复**可能**导致难以修改」的观点占比),成文不得混为一谈;后者是**假设性观点**(can cause),不是「61% 的团队遇到了功能冻结」。抽样:便利 + 可能的滚雪球,通过 BDD/Cucumber Google groups 招募(偏向在用者)。
- **H30 修正 + 升级为 verbatim**:原帖逐字「If you think Cucumber is a testing tool, please read on, because you are wrong.」「This improper usage of Cucumber caused many Rails teams to reject Cucumber.」「Cucumber is first a formost a collaboration tool that aims to bring a common understanding to software teams - across roles.」修正前日期「c.2014」→ 修正后「2014-03-03」。**口径**:帖首「Cucumber reached a million downloads in the first three years and 5 million downloads 3 years later」——下载量 ≠ 采用 ≠ 正确使用;「many Rails teams」是作者印象(链接一个讨论串),无数。**利益相关已在原文自证**:同帖「This is part of the reason why I co-founded Cucumber Ltd and our flagship product, Cucumber Pro」——帖子兼有推销商业协作工具的目的,厂商口径成立。
- **H31 修正 + 升级(摘要级 verbatim)**:摘要原文——「a large-scale analysis to understand the extent to which BDD frameworks are used in 50,000 popular open-source projects written in five programming languages; (ii) a study on the co-evolution of scenarios, fixtures and production code in a sample of 20 Ruby projects, through the Granger’s causality test, and (iii) a survey with 31 developers」「≃ 27% of the sampled projects use BDD frameworks, with a prevalence in Ruby projects (68%). In about 37% of the cases, we found a co-evolution between scenarios/fixtures and production code. Specifically, changes to scenarios and fixtures often happen together or after changes to source code.」「most of them write tests while/after coding rather than strictly applying BDD」「developers felt BDD remains quite effort-prone」。**样本矛盾已解**:50,000 / 20 / 31 是三个阶段各自的样本,不矛盾。修正前「BDD 框架采用不少」→ 修正后「约 27% 的抽样项目**引入了 BDD 框架**(Ruby 68%),但作者明确指出常被用于单元测试等非 BDD 用途——框架存在 ≠ BDD 实践」。**对 SDD 最有用的口径**:在 20 个 Ruby 项目中只有约 37% 出现规约与代码共同演化,且规约变更多与代码**同时或之后**发生——这是 T4 可执行规约「spec 跟随代码而非先行」的观测证据(主张 2 相关),但样本仅 20 个 Ruby 项目、2020 年以前,全文未读,「27%」的分母(5 万还是其筛选子集)未核。
- **H32 HOLDS(带复算)**:摘要「the pre-release defect density of the four products decreased between 40% and 90%」「Subjectively, the teams experienced a 15–35% increase in initial development time」逐字在。Table 3 复算:0.61/0.38/0.24/0.09 → 降幅 39% / 62% / 76% / 91%(「40–90%」为取整);开发时间增幅表列「15–20% / 25–35% / 15% / 25–20%」(VS 一栏原表即如此,疑为排印错),标注「[Management estimates]」。**可比性口径**(Table 2):TDD 项目与对照项目规模差距大——MSN 26.0 vs 149.0 KLOC(约 5.7 倍)、VS 155.2 vs 222.0、IBM 41.0 vs 56.6;对照组缺陷密度绝对值以 W/X/Y/Z 隐去,只能看比值。作者自承(第 424–430 行)IBM 对照是遗留系统、TDD 是新代码。
- **H33 部分升级**:Fucci 等 arXiv 版摘要逐字「We analyzed 82 data points collected from 39 professionals… Quality was measured by functional correctness… Sequencing, the order in which test and production code are written, had no important influence… The claimed benefits of TDD may not be due to its distinctive test-first dynamic, but rather due to the fact that TDD-like processes encourage fine-grained, steady steps」——**升级为 verbatim**。口径:数据来自单元测试/TDD **工作坊**中的编程任务(TDD vs 迭代测试后写 ITL),属观察性回归,任务规模小;质量=功能正确性(验收测试通过),不是缺陷密度。Rafique & Mišić 2013、Bissi 等 2016 本镜头未取得(仍 secondhand);Bissi 的 76%/88% 是「报告改进的研究占比」(计票),不得与 Rafique 的效应量、Nagappan 的缺陷密度降幅放在同一句里比较。
- **外推缺口(全组)**:TDD/BDD 是 T4;把 Fucci「顺序不是有效成分」迁移到 SDD「先写 spec」是**类比**,须明说;同理 Nagappan 的缺陷降幅不能当作 SDD 主张 1 的先验。

## 成文提示
- 必须带的限定:Binamungu = 75 名自选 BDD 实践者;Nagappan = 4 对非随机比较、时间为管理层估计;Fucci = 39 名专业人员的工作坊任务;Zampetti = 框架存在口径 + 20 个 Ruby 项目的共演化。
- 禁止:「61% 的团队因重复导致功能冻结」;「27% 的开源项目在做 BDD」;「TDD 降低缺陷 40–90%」不带「4 个团队、非随机」;把三种 TDD 证据(效应量 / 计票 / 缺陷密度比)写成一致的数字。
- 最强写法:「BDD 是『可执行规约』的前一代实验:框架进了约四分之一的抽样开源项目,但多被当单元测试用;在被追踪的 20 个 Ruby 项目里,只有约 37% 的规约与代码共同演化,且规约更多是跟着代码改,而不是先于代码。TDD 研究里最尖锐的一项结果是:测试先写还是后写本身无关紧要,要紧的是小步、匀速——『先写 spec』里真正起作用的,可能也不是『先』。」
