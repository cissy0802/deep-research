# G12 — Lens K(口径)

**组判定:修正**(Royce 原文各数字与引语在 PDF 文本层定位,复算无误;H10 的引语用的是 c2 wiki 草稿措辞,与期刊终稿不同,须改用终稿;H09 仍未取得一手。核心考古结论——「waterfall 一词不在 Royce 原文」「Royce 反对的是不加五步的单趟实施,而不是阶段化,且他主张更多前置文档」——成立)

核验底稿:`git clone --filter=blob:none github.com/tpn/pdfs` 取出「Managing the Development of Large Software Systems - 1970 (waterfall).pdf」(pdfinfo:11 页),`pdftotext -layout` 检索;Larman & Basili 2003 作者站 PDF https://www.craiglarman.com/wiki/downloads/misc/history-of-iterative-larman-and-basili-ieee-computer.pdf(10 页,页脚「June 2003」,为期刊排版版本)。Bell & Thayer 1976 的 dl.acm.org PDF 仍返回 403。

- **H01 HOLDS**:文本层第 85–95 行「I believe in this concept, but the implementation described above is risky and invites failure. The problem is illustrated in Figure 4… one can expect up to a lO0-percent overrun in schedule and/or costs」(OCR 把 100 读作 lO0)。口径:「up to 100%」是**上限式经验判断**,无数据、无样本;语境是航天任务软件,失败机制是「时序/存储/IO 只能在测试时被经验到」——对 LLM 时代的外推须说明这个机制前提(今天可以很早运行代码)。
- **H02 HOLDS**:第 104–105 行「fundamentally sound… five additional features that must be added to this basic approach」;第 381–384 行「the simpler method has never worked on large software development efforts and the costs to recover far exceeded those required to finance the five-step process listed」。口径:「never worked」限定在「large software development efforts」;成文不得去掉「大型」。
- **H03 HOLDS**:第 126、146 行图注(Fig.3「Hopefully… confined to successive steps」/ Fig.4「Unfortunately… never confined to the successive steps」)。
- **H04 HOLDS**:第 189、193、206 行逐字在(「ruthless enforcement of documentation requirements」「Replace project management. Stop all activities not related to documentation.」「these three nouns (documentation, specification, design) denote a single thing」)。这是 Royce↔SDD 同构最强的一手证据;但口径是 1970 年**政府合同**语境下的文档要求(Larman & Basili 终稿也强调「within the constraints of government contracting at that time」),外推时须带语境。
- **H05 HOLDS(带残损限定)**:第 196–198 行「a 30 page specification… I would estimate ~ 1[,00 pa~e specification is about right」——文本层第二位字符为乱码「[,」,与 Round 1 的「残损、不可定 1000/1500」一致。复算:软件/硬件规约页数比 = 1000/30 ≈ 33× 或 1500/30 = 50×;只能写「三四十倍以上」「上千页」。纯估计,无数据。
- **H06 HOLDS**:第 301–303 行「If the effort runs 30 months then this early development of a pilot model might be scheduled for 10 months… reduced to 12 months, then the pilot effort could be compressed to three months perhaps」。复算:试点占总工期 1/3(30 月)到 1/4(12 月)。口径:试点是「entire process done in miniature」(第 298 行)——即整个流程的缩微版,不是「扔掉代码重写」,与「spec 可再生代码」只有弱可比性。
- **H07 HOLDS(带语境)**:第 96–98 行「these phases are managed with relative ease and have little impact on requirements, design, and testing」。紧接着是「whole departments consumed with the analysis of orbit mechanics…」——「分析」指数学分析,不等于今天的「编码」全部;「代码便宜」的历史类比只能弱写。
- **H08 HOLDS**:pdftotext 全文 `grep -i waterfall` 0 命中(文件名中的「(waterfall)」是上传者所加)。否定性结论仍受 OCR 质量限制,但与 Round 1 的独立转录一致。
- **H09 未能核**:ACM 一手仍 403;只有两份转录。口径:写「目前已知最早的印刷用例通常归于 Bell & Thayer 1976」,不写「首创」。「其 Fig.1 对应 Royce Fig.2」未能核,**不得写**。注意利益相关性弱:两人与 Royce 同在 TRW。
- **H10 修正 + 升级为 verbatim(终稿)**:期刊版第 2 页原文为——「Many—incorrectly—view Royce’s paper as the paragon of single-pass waterfall.」「Thus, we see hints of iterative development, feedback, and adaptation in Royce’s article. This iterative feedback-based step has been lost in most descriptions of this model, although it is clearly not classic IID.」Walker Royce 引语终稿为「He was always a proponent of iterative, incremental, evolutionary development. His paper described the waterfall as the simplest description, but that it would not work for all but the most straightforward projects. The rest of his paper describes [iterative practices] within the context of the 60s/70s government-contracting models (a serious set of constraints).」修正前(c2 草稿)「This iterative feedback-based step **in a classic "waterfall paper"** has been lost…」→ 修正后使用终稿措辞(无该插入语)。私人通信、亲属事后转述、Larman 为 IID 推广者——仍属弱证据。

## 成文提示
- 必须带的限定:Royce 的论断全部是「大型、政府合同、航天任务软件」语境下的经验判断,无数据;「100% 超支」「上千页规约」都是作者估计。
- 禁止:「Royce 发明了瀑布」「Royce 反对瀑布」(两头都错);单点写「1,500 页」;「Bell & Thayer 首创 waterfall 一词」。
- 最强写法:「Royce 1970 从未使用『waterfall』一词;他说单趟顺序实施『有风险、招致失败』,但认为阶段化本身『根本上健全』,开出的药方是更多前置文档(『文档、规约、设计』在编码前是同一件事)、先做一个试点版本、让客户参与——这比今天的 SDD 叙事更重文档,而不是更轻。」
- 与 SDD 对位时只取机制(前置规约 + 试点 + 文档纪律),不取数字。
