- **H23 HOLDS**:Table I 题注逐字:“DECLARED CURRENT UML USE AMONG 50 PROFESSIONAL SOFTWARE DEVELOPERS FROM 50 COMPANIES.”;计数为 no UML 35 / retrofit 1 / automated code generation 3 / selective 11 / wholehearted 0。“Selective (11/50): UML is used in design in a personal, selective, and informal way, for as long as it is considered useful, after which it is discarded”;“Wholehearted (0/50 – but described in secondary reports)”。方法部分逐字:“a series of interviews conducted over 2 years with more than 50 practicing professional software developers”;作者自限:“any counts in the paper are offered for the purpose of description and are not held to be statistically characteristic of the whole population of software engineers.” 应把这句自限一并写入成文。
- **H24 HOLDS**:逐字:“A number of informants identified issues of synchronization or consistency as a barrier for wholesale adoption of UML.”;“Informants criticized UML for its complexity, lack of formal semantics, inconsistency, and issues of synchronization between different diagrams (and between models and code).”(“lack of formal semantics” 另一处出现在 Petre 转引 Lange et al. 的文字中,R1 引用的是受访者批评这一句,无误。)
- **H25 HOLDS**:逐字:“These benefits have reportedly led to productivity increases ranging anywhere from 20 to 800% [18, 5]. On the other hand, there is anecdotal evidence that cites reasons for decreased productivity – quantified at 27% in [18] – such as poor tool support, a tendency to over-model (“model paralysis”), increased complexity, and the need to keep models/code in sync.”;“Of the comparative studies, results vary between a 35% gain to a 27% loss. Two of the studies showed no net impact.” 样本逐字:“a questionnaire widely disseminated to MDE practitioners, which received over 250 responses; (ii) in-depth interviews with 22 MDE professionals from 17 different companies; (iii) on-site observational studies”。补充:“+35% / −27%” 是 Hutchinson 对 Mohagheghi & Dehlen 文献综述的转述(“Few of the papers surveyed provided strong empirical evidence”),不是本研究自己的测量。成文应写“文献综述中的比较研究”。
- **H26 HOLDS**:摘要逐字:“we surveyed 450 MDE practitioners and carried out in-depth interviews with 22 more. Findings suggest that MDE may be more widespread than commonly believed, but developers rarely use it to generate whole systems”;“although reports of productivity gains vary widely (from a 27% loss to an 800% gain [6]), most companies seem to experience productivity increases of between 20-30%.”;“code generation is a red herring when it comes to describing MDE”;“code certification costs rose by a factor of eight.”(语境:“one case study … certifying code for use in government information systems”);“adoption largely depends on social and organizational factors”。注意 250+(2011)与 450(2014)是同一项目先后的问卷数,不构成混口径;但两者不能相加。
- **H27 推翻(伪引用)→ 以原件替换**:原件 https://www.omg.org/cgi-bin/doc?omg/03-06-01.pdf(“Document Number: omg/2003-06-01 / Date: 12th June 2003 / MDA Guide Version 1.0.1”,62 页)。全文检索 “enough information”“to drive”“abstract model which contains”,均为 0 次。R1 quote “A Platform Independent Model (PIM) is an abstract model which contains enough information to drive one or more Platform Specific Models (PSM).” **不在该文件中**,判为伪引用(可能出自他人转述或其他 OMG 文档,未定位)。原件可用的逐字措辞:§2.2.12 “A platform independent model is a view of a system from the platform independent viewpoint. A PIM exhibits a specified degree of platform independence so as to be suitable for use with a number of different platforms of similar type.”;§2.2.15 “Figure 2-2 illustrates the MDA pattern, by which a PIM is transformed to a PSM.”;§3.7 “A tool might transform a PIM directly to deployable code, without producing a PSM.”;§1 的收益表述:“since the developed models can be used to generate code, they can equally be validated against requirements”。用这些原句仍能支撑“模型→变换→代码”的前代叙事,但原件**没有**“唯一事实源”的措辞,成文不得把这一说法当作 OMG 原话。

## 成文提示
- 必须带的限定:Petre 为 50 人定性访谈,作者明言计数不代表总体;Whittle/Hutchinson 的分母是 MDE 实践者(幸存者偏差),“20–30%” 是 “seem to” 的归纳。
- 禁止的表述:任何以 “OMG 称 PIM ‘contains enough information to drive…’” 为引号的写法;“OMG 宣称模型是唯一事实源”;Kemerer 的 “70% 一年后从未使用”。
- 可用的最强写法:“一项 50 名专业开发者的访谈中,35 人完全不用 UML,没有一人所在组织‘全面采用’;受访者把图与图、模型与代码之间的同步列为障碍——这正是今天 spec 漂移问题的前身。”
