# G13 — Lens K(口径)

**组判定:HOLDS**(Brooks 四条在 UNC TR86-020 原 PDF 文本层逐字定位,升级为 verbatim;Parnas & Clements、Dijkstra、Knuth 一手本镜头未能取得,口径层面无可复算数字,维持原分级。全组均为专家论述,不是测量——这是成文时唯一但关键的口径限定)

核验底稿:直抓 https://www.cs.unc.edu/techreports/86-020.pdf(21 页,带文本层),`pdftotext` 检索。www.cs.utexas.edu(EWD 档案、Parnas fakeit.pdf 镜像)在本会话 DNS 不可解析/证书链失败,literateprogramming.com 不可解析——**须在会话网络设置中放行 www.cs.utexas.edu 方可补核 H17**。

- **H11 HOLDS + 升级为 verbatim**:§5.2「Requirements Refinement and Rapid Prototyping」:「The hardest single part of building a software system is deciding precisely what to build.」「Therefore the most important function that the software builder does for his client is the iterative extraction and refinement of the product requirements. For the truth is, the client does not know what he wants.」「Much of present-day software acquisition procedures rests upon the assumption that one can specify a satisfactory system in advance, get bids for its construction, have it built, and install it. I think this assumption is fundamentally wrong」。**口径补充**:同节 Brooks 给的药方是「rapid prototyping of systems as part of the iterative specification of requirements」并断言「it is really impossible for a client… to specify completely, precisely, and correctly the exact requirements… before having built and tried some versions」——即「规约须经原型迭代得到」。对 SDD 是双刃:反对一次性前置 spec(不利主张 1 的重型版),但若 LLM 让原型变便宜,则支持「spec 与原型交替」的轻型版。成文须两面都写,不得只取反方一半。
- **H12 HOLDS + 升级为 verbatim**:§4.5:「For almost 40 years, people have been anticipating and writing about "automatic programming", the generation of a program for solving a problem from a statement of the problem specifications.」「Parnas {8} implies that the term is used for glamor and not semantic content, asserting, In short, automatic programming always has been a euphemism for programming with a higher-level language than was presently available to the programmer. He argues, in essence, that in most cases it is the solution method, not the problem, whose specification has to be given.」口径:Parnas 句为 Brooks 转引(Parnas 原文未回);「solution method… not the problem」是 Brooks 对 Parnas 的**概括**(「He argues, in essence」),引用时须标「Brooks 概括 Parnas」。同节 Brooks 也列了例外(排序生成器、微分方程系统)——不是全盘否定。
- **H13 HOLDS + 升级为 verbatim**:§4.7 原文逐字吻合(「even perfect program verification can only establish that a program meets its specification. The hardest part of the software task is arriving at a complete and consistent specification, and much of the essence of building a program is in fact the debugging of the specification.」)。分型:语境是程序验证(T3),外推到 T1 自然语言 spec 时须写明是类比。
- **H14 HOLDS + 升级为 verbatim(带语境)**:§4.6「Nothing even convincing, much less exciting, has yet emerged from such efforts. I am persuaded that nothing will.」「it has proved to be essentially useless as a design tool- programmers draw flowcharts after, not before, writing the programs they describe.」口径:Brooks 的第二条理由是「the screens of today are too small, in pixels」——部分论据绑定 1986 年硬件;对象是**图形化编程/流程图**,不是文本规约。外推到 SDD「事后补 spec」只能作类比。
- **H15 未能核(一手)/ HOLDS(口径)**:一手未取得;论述性,无数字。口径:Parnas & Clements 的「fake it」是**文档组织方式**建议(按理性过程呈现),而非反对先写规约——成文不得写成「Parnas 认为规约无用」。
- **H16 未能核(一手)/ HOLDS(口径)**:同上。「in most cases, will not be maintained」是专家判断,无测量;是主张 2(spec 活性)的机制性前科,不是漂移率数据。其限定语是「按意识流组织的文档」——成文须保留该条件,否则误把一切文档都说成不可维护。
- **H17 未能核(一手)**:utexas 不可达;仍为转录。论述性,无数字。分型 T1 对。注意 EWD667 年份「c.1978」为档案推定,写「约 1978」。
- **H34 未能核(一手)/ HOLDS(口径)**:引语在多份转录中一致,但一手未读。口径:Round 1 已正确标注「未找到采用率数据」——成文只能写「未成为主流实践」,并且 Rule 等 2018 的「约 1/4 笔记本无解释性文本」仅二手摘要,**不得承重**。

## 成文提示
- 必须带的限定:全部是论述/观点,不是测量;时代背景(1978–1987)写清。
- 禁止:「Brooks 证明了 SDD 不可行」;把 Parnas 的「euphemism」写成 Parnas 原文直引而不注明经 Brooks 转引;把 H14 的流程图判断套到文本 spec 上而不说是类比。
- 最强写法:「Brooks 1986 认为软件最难的是『精确决定要构建什么』,而且客户在试用过几版原型之前不可能完整、精确地说清需求;他转引 Parnas:『自动编程』一直是『用比当时更高级的语言编程』的委婉说法。SDD 若把自然语言 spec 当作『新的高级语言』,正落在这条 40 年老判断上。」
