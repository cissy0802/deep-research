# T-theory-history: 理论与历史类比 (截至 2026-10)

状态: 完成 (2026-10-05); 结构化论断见调研线输出

## 理论原典

### T1 Bain 利润池 (Gadiesh & Gilbert, HBR May-Jun 1998)
- URL: https://hbr.org/1998/05/profit-pools-a-fresh-look-at-strategy ; Bain 摘要页 https://www.bain.com/insights/profit-pools-a-fresh-look-at-strategy/
- 来源: 官方(原刊摘要;全文付费)
- 摘要页原文(Bain): "U-Haul recognized that while the core rental business represented the vast majority of the industry's revenue pool, accessories provided a large share of the industry's profit pool"
- 要点: 利润池=价值链各环节利润总和;利润集中格局常与收入集中格局不同。全文(付费)含美国汽车业例子未能取得逐字,待验证。

### T2 Christensen 利润守恒定律 (HBR "Breakthrough Ideas for 2004", 2004-02;《The Innovator's Solution》2003 第6章)
- URL: https://hbr.org/2004/02/breakthrough-ideas-for-2004
- 原文(经搜索摘录, 非直接抓取全文): "When attractive profits disappear at one stage in the value chain because a product becomes commoditized, the opportunity to earn attractive profits with proprietary products usually emerges at an adjacent stage."

### T3 Ben Thompson 聚合理论 (Stratechery 2015-07-21)
- URL: https://stratechery.com/2015/aggregation-theory/
- "The value chain for any given consumer market is divided into three parts: suppliers, distributors, and consumers/users."
- "The Internet has made distribution (of digital goods) free, neutralizing the advantage that pre-Internet distributors leveraged to integrate with suppliers."

### T4 Joel Spolsky "Strategy Letter V" (2002-06-12)
- URL: https://www.joelonsoftware.com/2002/06/12/strategy-letter-v/
- "Smart companies try to commoditize their products' complements."
- "Microsoft's goal was to commoditize the PC market."

### T5 Thompson 对 AI (2025-12-01 "Google, Nvidia, and OpenAI")
- URL: https://stratechery.com/2025/google-nvidia-and-openai/
- "Aggregators, meanwhile, have totally commoditized their suppliers, but have done so in the service of maximizing attention, which they can monetize through advertising."
- "At one point it didn't seem possible to commoditize content more than Google or Facebook did, but that's exactly what LLMs do"
- "the long-term threat to Nvidia from TPUs is margin dilution"
- "the real decision maker may be TSMC, which makes both chips"
- "OpenAI's refusal to launch and iterate an ads product for ChatGPT — now three years old — is a dereliction of business duty, particularly as the company signs deals for over a trillion dollars of compute."

### T6 Thompson "Aggregator's AI Risk" (2024-03-04)
- URL: https://stratechery.com/2024/aggregators-ai-risk/
- "Generative AI flips this paradigm on its head: suddenly, there isn't an abundance of supply, at least from the perspective of the end users; there is simply one answer."

### H4 光纤/电信泡沫 一手 (Odlyzko 论文)
- Odlyzko "Data networks are lightly utilized, and will stay that way" (AT&T Labs, 1998-10-07) https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/network.utilization.pdf
  - "the Internet backbone links appear to have average utilizations closer to 10% to 15%, and corporate long-haul links (which is where the bulk of data transport capacity is) have utilizations in the 3% to 5% range."
- Odlyzko "Internet traffic growth: Sources and implications" (ITCom 2003) https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/itcom.internet.growth.pdf
  - "Backbone transport is likely to remain a commodity and be provided as a single high quality service. It is probable that backbone revenues will stay low, as the complexity, cost, and revenue and profit opportunities continue to migrate towards the edges of the network."
  - "Since there is a glut of this basic infrastructure, it sells for a small fraction of the construction cost."
- Thompson "The Benefits of Bubbles" (Stratechery 2025-11-05) https://stratechery.com/2025/the-benefits-of-bubbles/

  - 反向(对芯片层不利): "Chips break down and get superseded by better ones; most hyperscalers depreciate them over five years, and that may be generous." → GPU 不像光纤/铁路那样能以折旧完毕资产形式长期廉价存续; 他认为 fab 与电力是长寿命基建。
- Global Crossing 2002-01-28 申请 Chapter 11 (媒体/维基转述: 资产 $22.4B、债务 $12.4B, 时为美国第四大破产)。FCC 主席 Powell 2002-07-30 参议院证词: 行业欠债约一万亿美元(转述)。待补一手。

### H5 铁路狂热 (Odlyzko "Collective Hallucinations and Inefficient Markets: The British Railway Mania of the 1840s", 2010-01-15 预印本) https://www-users.cse.umn.edu/~odlyzko/doc/hallucinations.pdf
- "The British Railway Mania of the 1840s was by many measures the greatest technology mania in history, and its collapse was one of the greatest financial crashes."
- "By the end of 1850, British investors had put about £250 million into their country's railways, almost half of their GDP ... Their financial losses can be estimated ... at about a third of that, or about £80 million"

### H6 电气化 (Paul David, "The Dynamo and the Computer", AER P&P 80(2), 1990-05, 同行评审(会议论文集)) http://www.dklevine.com/archive/refs4115.pdf
- "factory electrification did not reach full fruition in its technical development nor have an impact on productivity growth in manufacturing before the early 1920s. At that time only slightly more than half of factory mechanical drive capacity had been electrified. ... This was four decades after the first central power station opened for business."
- "approximately half of the 5 percentage point acceleration recorded in the aggregate TFP growth rate of the U.S. manufacturing sector during 1919-29 ... is accounted for statistically simply by the growth in manufacturing secondary electric motor capacity"
- 含义: 通用技术的生产率收益滞后数十年, 由重组流程的使用方获得;注意此文讲生产率不讲利润分配。

## 追加 (第二轮)

### T2b Christensen 原书逐字 (《The Innovator's Solution》2003, 经 Thompson 2026-09-21 "Frontier Overhangs" 原样引用) https://stratechery.com/2026/frontier-overhangs/
- "when there is a performance gap — when product functionality and reliability are not yet good enough to address the needs of customers in a given tier of the market — companies must compete by making the best possible products. In the race to do this, firms that build their products around proprietary, interdependent architectures enjoy an important competitive advantage against competitors whose product architectures are modular"
- "Once their requirements for functionality and reliability have been met, customers begin to redefine what is not good enough. ... away from the interdependent, proprietary architectures that had the advantage in the not-good-enough era toward modular designs in the era of performance surplus."
- 另经 Matan Zinger (Substack 2026-06-02, https://94040.substack.com/p/could-anthropic-commoditize-the-cloud) 引书: "Whenever it [commoditization] is at work somewhere in a value chain, a reciprocal process of de-commoditization is at work somewhere else in the value chain." / "companies that position themselves at a spot in the value chain where performance is not yet good enough will capture the profit."

### T2c Christensen, Raynor & Verlinden "Skate to Where the Money Will Be" (HBR 2001-11, 注意是 2001 不是 2004) https://hbr.org/2001/11/skate-to-where-the-money-will-be
- "When IBM decided to outsource its operating system and processor chips in the early 1980s, it was, or appeared to be, at the top of its game. It owned 70% of the entire mainframe market, controlled 95% of its profits, and had long dominated the industry."

### T7 Thompson 2026 的转向
- "Agents Over Bubbles" (2026-03-16) https://stratechery.com/2026/agents-over-bubbles/ : "profits flow away from modular parts of the value chain — which are commoditized — and flow towards integrated parts of the value chain, which are differentiated." / "if agents require integration between model and harness, that the companies building that integration — specifically Anthropic and OpenAI — are actually poised to be significantly more profitable than it might have seemed as recently as late last year."
- "Anthropic's Safety Superpower" (2026-06-15) https://stratechery.com/2026/anthropics-safety-superpower/ : "the biggest beneficiaries have been Nvidia, TSMC, and the memory makers (SK hynix, Samsung, and Micron). Anthropic and OpenAI, meanwhile, have collectively lost tens of billions of dollars building leading-edge models that, once released, are distilled and commoditized by open source models, primarily from China." / "Right now that's compute, but in the fullness of time, whenever we have enough compute, the most valuable place to be in the value chain will be the place that has always been the most valuable: owning the user touchpoint."
- "Frontier Overhangs" (2026-09-21): 对 harness 整合论"wavered"; Microsoft Copilot Cowork 已可换模型 → "clearly the harness and the model can be different things"; "current model capabilities are 'good enough' for customers to not do whatever is necessary to get access to the cutting edge, which reduces the value of the cutting edge to its proprietors" / "Pure capability no longer translates directly into a moat."
- "Copilot Cowork, Anthropic's Integration, Microsoft's New Bundle" (2026-03-10): "Microsoft is seeking to commoditize its complements, but Anthropic has a point of integration of their own"
- "Google, Nvidia, and OpenAI" 摘要外: "Blackwell margins are doing just fine ... in a world where everyone is starved for compute."

## 口径与类比警示
- 聚合理论前提是 "the Internet has made transaction costs zero" 与分发免费 (Thompson 2015) ; AI 推理边际成本非零 (Evans 2026: 推理毛利 40-50%, 二手), 聚合者利润结构可能不同于搜索/社交。
- Bain 利润池原文汽车业例子数据未取到(付费墙)。
- Apple 85% 为 Counterpoint 2022 年 "operating profit share" 估算, 分析机构口径, 经 AppleInsider 转述; Thompson 2026 "capture nearly the entirety of the PC and smartphone sector profits" 为从业者断言。
- 光纤 "2.7% 点亮" 为 WSJ 二手口径, 未找到一手; Odlyzko 1998 的 3-5%/10-15% 为"平均利用率"而非"点亮比例", 不可混用。
- PC 利润率: Intel/Compaq 日历年, MSFT 6 月财年, Dell 1-2 月财年; 全部 GAAP 经营利润。
- 铁路损失 £80M 为 Odlyzko 估算 (预印本, 非同行评审终稿)。
- 前沿实验室"每个模型盈利"为 Amodei 自称的卡通示例, 非财报数字。
