**Group verdict: HOLDS**(全部数字经独立下载 1980a PDF 文本层复核;另有 Science 1981 独立印证 O01)

## O01 — HOLDS
- 1980a 文本层 l.256-257:"The fourteen submitted entries came from three countries and five disciplines ... the sixteen people"。
- 独立印证:Axelrod & Hamilton 1981 Science(umich 扫描 PDF,pdftotext):"The 14 entries and a totally random strategy were paired with each other in a round robin tournament." 15 = 14+RANDOM 成立。
- 反向:Rapoport, Seale & Colman 2015 (PLoS ONE) 确实写 "he invited 14 scientists"——二手误述,备注正确。
- 搜索角度:Science 1981 原文;axelrod.readthedocs "Overview of past tournaments"(写 "14 strategies, plus a random entry",一致);Hoffmann 2000 JASSS(不给数)。
