## 关联反证(影响本组成文)
- Adami & Hintze 2014 勘误 (Nat Commun 5:3764, 2014-06-16):"the instability found here for zero determinant (ZD) strategies only applies to the set of coercive strategies we considered" (https://ideas.repec.org/a/nat/natcom/v5y2014i1d10.1038_ncomms4764.html)。所以写"ZD 被证明演化不稳定"时必须限定为"勒索/强制型 ZD"。慷慨型 ZD 是稳健的(Stewart–Plotkin 2013)。
