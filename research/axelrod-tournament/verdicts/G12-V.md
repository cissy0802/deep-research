## E01 HOLDS
- aeaweb 逐字："We show that cooperation may prevail in infinitely repeated games, but the conditions under which this occurs are more stringent than the subgame perfect conditions usually considered or even a condition based on risk dominance."

## E02 HOLDS
- WP(封面日期 "April 2007")逐字："each subject participated in between 23 and 77 infinitely repeated games"。
- Table 3 "Risk-Dominant Cooperation" 的取值：δ=1/2 时为 NO NO YES，δ=3/4 时为 NO YES YES。共 3/6 格成立，与论断一致。
- 脚注 5 的原文为 "0.72 under R=23, 0.4 under R=40, and 0.8 under R=48"，其中有两处笔误：
  - "R=23" 应为 R=32；
  - "0.8" 应为 0.08，(50−48)/(50−25)=0.08。
- 由此推出：除 δ=1/2 & R=32 外，其余格子都满足 SPE，论断成立。

## E03 HOLDS
- 逐字："While in the first rounds of the early repeated games in the risk-dominant treatments cooperation was 46.5%, in later repeated games (after 110 interactions) it reached 70.6%"。
- 逐字："Cooperation was 29% in the first round of the repeated games that begin within the first 10 interactions, dropping to 5.5% in the repeated games that begin within interactions 111 to 120"。
