# G10-V 逐字核验(2004/2005 二十周年赛与南安普顿合谋)

**Group verdict: 修正**(M05 推翻、M12 修正，另有若干口径注释)

核验方式：
- Rogers 2007 的 eprints 直链返回 403(反爬页面)，改从 Wayback 取到 PDF(web.archive.org/web/2015id_/…/journal_paper.pdf，13 页)，经 pdftotext 检索。
- 另检索了 Slany & Kienreich 的 arXiv PDF、Wired 转载页(thereitis.org)、Southampton 新闻页和 bactra 原页。

## M01 HOLDS
- Rogers 原文逐字："To stimulate novel research, the rules of Axelrod's original tournaments were extended in two key ways. Firstly, noise was introduced, whereby the moves of each player would be mis-executed with some small probability. Secondly, researchers were explicitly invited to enter more than one player..."
- 书目："Theoretical Computer Science 377 (2007) 243–259"，doi 10.1016/j.tcs.2007.03.015。
- 注意：noise 的类型各方说法不一，见 M09。

## M02 HOLDS
- Southampton 新闻页："Published: 7 October 2004"；"The members of the Southampton team were Professor Nick Jennings, Dr Alex Rogers, Gopal Ramchurn, Raj Dash and Krishnen Vytelingum."
- 新闻稿描述的环境是 "a noisy environment where moves could be misinterpreted"(误读，而不是误执行)。
- Jennings 原话："Our solution beats the standard tit-for-tat strategy."

## M03 HOLDS(作为媒体口径)
- 转载页标注 "Oct. 13, 2004 | Wired by Wendy M. Grossman"。逐字："The 2004 competition had 223 entries" / "the Southampton team submitted 60 programs" / "Southampton had the top three performers"。
- 60 这个数字与 Rogers 的 4×28=112、Slany 的 112 冲突，应以 112 为准，60 只能标为媒体口径。

## M04 HOLDS
- 逐字："These, Jennings explained, were all slight variations on a theme and were designed to execute a known series of five to 10 moves" / "a load of utter failures at the bottom of the table who sacrificed themselves for the good of the team"。
- master/slave 的说法出自 Jennings 本人。
- 码长：Rogers 原文为 "single block Hamming code that takes a 4 bit code word and generates a 7 bit message"(2004 年用这个，7 步)，以及 "[15, 5] BCH ... encodes a 5 bit code word into a 15 bit message"(2005 年用这个，15 步)。

## M05 推翻(引语归属)
- 原文段落顺序是：Kendall 的话之后另起一段 "'Ultimately,' he said, 'what's more important is the research.' 'What's interesting from our point of view,' he said, 'was to test some ideas we had about teamwork in general agent systems... It turns out we had far too many — we would have won with around 20.'"，紧接着是 "Jennings is also interested in testing..."。
- "ideas we had about teamwork in general agent systems" 是 Southampton 智能体组的研究议程；"we had far too many" 指的是自家投放的 colluders 数量。语境指向的是 **Jennings**，不是组织者 Kendall。
- 原文只写了 "he said"，没有点名。
- 修正后：成文不得写"Kendall 说"。要么写"Southampton 团队(Jennings)称"，要么不引用这句。M10 备注里"与 Kendall 的话一致"也要同步删掉。

## M06 HOLDS
- 逐字："we entered four different teams. Each team consisted of 28 players, and used the single block Hamming code ... the team leader that employed tit-for-tat as a default strategy won the competition by a significant margin (it scored 13% more than the second placed competing player)."
- 原文前一句是 "the policy was not widely adopted"。13% 对应哪个 league，原文没说，这一点备注判断正确。

## M07 HOLDS
- Slany 原文逐字："after the STAR group strategy of Gopal Ramchurn (with 112 members, though we are not sure that all strategies colluded as one group)" / "112 is exactly the smallest integer larger than 223 divided by 2 ... However, we now believe that this might have been just a coincidence."
- 成文时应保留"可能只是巧合"这层自我保留。

## M08 HOLDS

## M09 HOLDS(要补噪声类型)
- 逐字："the second league of the 2004 competition, which was the league with a small probability of erroneous interpretation of the other player's last move, OTFT was placed as the second best non-group, individual strategy, placed third after three members of Ramchurn's STAR group and an individual strategy sent in by Colm O'Riordan"。
- 1.2.2 节的另一处写的是 "OTFT was a very close 2nd"，Slany 自己前后口径不一。
- 注意：Slany 和 Southampton 新闻稿都把 league 2 描述为**误读**(misperception)，Rogers 写的是**误执行**(mis-execution)。成文要么只写"噪声"，要么并列两种说法。

## M11 HOLDS

## M12 修正
- 逐字："with just two team members and no noise, a team player will win the tournament just 3.4% of the time. However, as the noise level increases, the performance of the other players within the tournament degrades at a faster rate than that at which the effectiveness of the signalling between team members diminishes. At a noise level of 1/5 the same team members win 70.2% of the time."
- 修正前(1)："with 10% noise, two team members win only 3.4% without noise"，自相矛盾。
- 修正后(1)：无噪声时 3.4%；噪声 1/5 时 70.2%；竞争者是 18 个基础策略。
- 修正前(2)："In ecological, remove-worst, three or more still wins every competition"。
- 修正后(2)：Table 7(3 名队员)中 round-robin 86%、Ecological 77%、Remove Worst Overall 100%、Remove Worst 49%。"wins every competition" **只适用于 Remove Worst Overall**，原句为 "In the case of the Remove Worst Overall format ... three or more team players ... the team leader wins every competition"。
- 逐字："tit-for-tat performs relatively poorly and is easily beaten ... Adaptive and Gradual clearly outperform"。

In fact, a mutant which used the coding scheme to recognize supposed masters and always defected against them, but played TFT with everyone else, should do better than a slave"。

## 成文提示
- "本可以只用 20 个"这句话属于 Southampton(Jennings)，不是组织者 Kendall。
- 生态赛制下合谋并非全胜：3 名队员时 Ecological 只有 77%，只有 Remove Worst Overall 是 100%。
- 2004 年的噪声到底是误读还是误执行，各方说法冲突，成文不要定性。
