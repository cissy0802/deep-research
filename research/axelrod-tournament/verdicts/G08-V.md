# G08-V 逐字核验(零行列式原文与媒体放大)

**Group verdict: 修正**(全部引文逐字存在；只有一处书目细节错误和几处措辞需要收紧)

核验方式：curl 下载 PMC 全文(PMC3387070、PMC3387035)、edge.org/node/21471、MIT TR、Quanta 原页，转纯文本后做字符串检索，没有依赖摘要模型。

## Z01 修正
- 引文 "One specialization of ZD strategies allows X to unilaterally set Y's score." 逐字存在(PMC3387070，小节标题 "X Unilaterally Sets Y's Score")。"X cannot unilaterally set her own score in IPD." 也逐字存在。
- 书目：PMC 页头为 "2012 May 21;109(26):10409–10413. doi: 10.1073/pnas.1206569109"。Edge 写的是 "(May 22, 2012)"，所以 5-21/22 都可以接受。
- **错误**：备注说"Contributed track, Dyson NAS member"。原文是 "Contributed by William H. Press, April 19, 2012 (sent for review March 14, 2012)"。
- 改为:**由 Press 本人以 NAS 院士身份 Contributed**，而不是 Dyson。

## Z04 HOLDS(要补限定)
- "X's score is always greater than the mutual cooperation value of 3, and Y's is always less. X's limiting score as [χ→∞] is 13/3." 逐字存在。"enforces an extortion factor 3" 也逐字存在。
- 限定：原句针对的是"Y 的最佳回应 / 完全合作"这一点上的一般 χ>1 情形，不只是 χ=3 一例。χ 的取值是图片，无法从文本确认。
- 自算值 3.73/1.91 与 13/3 的极限一致，但原文里找不到这两个数，**不要当作引文**。

## Z09 HOLDS(注意语境)
- 原文："I am still very skeptical about its use as a model for the evolution of cooperation." 以及 "...So the strategy that is best for outcome is worst for evolution, and vice versa. Cooperation loses and defection wins."
- 语境：这是 Dyson 对 Stewart–Plotkin Fig.1 的解读，他把"赢局数"等同于演化优势。这个读法恰好与 S&P 2013 的结论相反，引用时要点明这一点。

## Z10 HOLDS(引文作者顺序要改)
- 逐字："biological or cultural evolution is not a phenomenon on the level of the individual. It requires a population." / "We had known about strategies that allow to nail down the opponent's payoff" / "The only norm which is not self-defeating through population dynamics requires players to guarantee each other as much as themselves. We are then back to Tit For Tat."
- 1997 年那篇的作者顺序：Edge 脚注写作 "M.A. Nowak, M.C. Boerlijst, K.Sigmund, Equal pay for all prisoners, AMS Monthly 104 (1997) 303-307"。Hilbe-Nowak-Sigmund 预印本 ref 12 写作 "Boerlijst MC, Nowak MA, Sigmund K (1997) ... 104: 303-307"。
- 成文统一用 Boerlijst, Nowak & Sigmund。

## 成文提示

- Dyson 那句 "best for outcome is worst for evolution" 是他对 S&P 图的个人误读，应该和 S&P 2013 的结论放在一起对照写。
- χ=3 的具体分数是自算的，不是原文数字。
