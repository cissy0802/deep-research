## 反向/更新证据(本组缺失,建议补入)
- Li & Shirado,"Spontaneous Giving and Calculated Greed in Language Models",EMNLP 2025 main(oral),arXiv 2502.17720(v4 2025-10-29):"reasoning models consistently reduce cooperation and norm enforcement, favoring individual rationality";推理模型越多的群体,重复互动中的收益越低。比较对象是 o1 vs GPT-4o、R1 vs V3。
