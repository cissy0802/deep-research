## O10 — HOLDS
- RSC 2015(PLoS ONE 10.1371/journal.pone.0134128)原文:"TFT did not score even a single win—by its nature, it can never outscore its co-player—and was ranked last." 计胜规则 "a win whenever the winning margin ... was positive"——平局不算胜,与'可以平不能赢'一致。
- 独立同向:Hilbe, Chatterjee & Nowak 2018 复述 TFT "did not win any pairwise encounter"。

## M18 — HOLDS
- Harper et al. 2017 PLoS ONE:"these tournaments were repeated 50000 times";176 个策略(53 个随机);"(We note that it never wins a tournament)";"Tit For Tat does not win any match"。
- 文中确无 TFT 中位名次数字(仅 Fig 9 "Ranks for Tit for Tat over 50000 tournaments")。
- 提示:结论绑定于 Axelrod-Python 当时版本的策略池,不是一般性结论。

## M29 — 修正(措辞)
- 之前:"points ranking 与 wins ranking 不相关 (ρ = −.103)"
- 之后:ρ = −.103 且 "cannot be rejected (p > .70)"——即第一届**无显著相关**;2004(n=223)ρ = −.45, p<0.001 显著**负相关**。两者性质不同,写作时不要并列成"都负相关"。
- 2004 联赛口径:RSC 只说 "incorporated random noise",未指明哪个 league;冲突未能解决(无法核验)。
