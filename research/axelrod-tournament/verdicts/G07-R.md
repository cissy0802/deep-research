## N23 — HOLDS

## O28 — HOLDS(措辞微修)
- JASSS 1(1) review1 原文确认三句:"TIT-FOR-TAT was not so very successful in Axelrod's simulation"、"well known for more than a quarter of a century"、"All that is then left on Axelrod's list is the requirement that a successful strategy be retaliatory"。
- 语境:'not so very successful' 是 Binmore 本人判断,其依据是 Linster 复现(TFT 约 1/6,且扫描初始条件时只约 1/4 情形最多)、GRIM 在全部二状态自动机中表现很好、Probst 的 mean machines。
- 'retaliatory' 也被 Binmore 限定为"仅在两人互动中必要"(多人情形下受害者不必亲自惩罚)。→ 写作时不要写成"Binmore 认为 retaliatory 普遍必要"。
