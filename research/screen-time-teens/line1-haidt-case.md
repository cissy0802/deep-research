## A. 核心命题与定义

### 论断 1:核心因果命题——"大重构"是青少年心理疾病浪潮的最大单一原因

- 逐字(书的宣传/论述口径):"Between 2010 and 2015, the social lives of American teens moved largely onto smartphones... This Great Rewiring of Childhood is the single largest reason for the tidal wave of adolescent mental illness that began in the early 2010s."(此句为 Haidt 书中/宣传材料反复使用的表述,经由多家转载确认;逐字以书为准)【书籍;转述层为媒体转载,待对书页码核】
  - 来源:Jonathan Haidt, *The Anxious Generation: How the Great Rewiring of Childhood Is Causing an Epidemic of Mental Illness*, Penguin Press, 2024;转载见 The Conversation 书评 https://theconversation.com/have-smartphones-created-an-anxious-generation-jonathan-haidt-sounds-the-alarm-227344 【媒体】
- Haidt 在 Senate 证词(2022-05-04)中的因果表述(一手 PDF,逐字):"Correlational, experimental, and eye-witness testimony points to social media as a major cause of the crisis. I do not believe that social media is the *only* cause of the crisis, but there is no alternative hypothesis that can explain the suddenness, enormity, and international similarity that I laid out in part 1 of this document."
  - 来源:Haidt, "Teen Mental Health Is Plummeting, and Social Media is a Major Contributing Cause," Testimony before Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on Technology, Privacy, and the Law, 2022-05-04, p.10. https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/Haidt%20Testimony.pdf 【官方】(证词文本本身;其立场为 Haidt 个人)
- After Babel 主张句(2023-02 首发、2024-04-09 更新版标题即主张):"Yes, Social Media Really Is a Cause of the Epidemic of Teenage Mental Illness";Haidt 强调是 "a substantial cause, not just a tiny correlate"。
  - 来源:https://www.afterbabel.com/p/social-media-mental-illness-epidemic 及 https://www.afterbabel.com/p/phone-based-childhood-cause-epidemic 【博客/Substack】

### 论断 2:"play-based childhood → phone-based childhood" 的替换

- 官方运动网站逐字:"In 2012, childhood shifted from play and independence to screens and social media." 使命:"roll back the phone-based childhood and restore real play, connection, and independence for kids."
  - 来源:https://www.anxiousgeneration.com/ 【官方(运动方)】
- 书的框架:play-based childhood 自 1980s 开始衰退,在 2010s 初被 phone-based childhood "很突然地" 终结;Haidt 称 2010-2015 为拐点窗口,"when smartphones and social media became common sights in a high school cafeteria"【转述,待核页码】。
  - 来源:NYU 官方报道 https://www.nyu.edu/about/news-publications/news/2024/june/-the-great-rewiring-of-childhood-.html 【官方(机构新闻)】;anxiousgeneration.com/book 【官方(运动方)】

### 论断 3:大重构时间窗定义 = 约 2010 至 2015

- 书副标题即主张:"How the Great Rewiring of Childhood Is Causing an Epidemic of Mental Illness"。时间窗:2010(iPhone 4 前置摄像头面世)至 2015(智能手机+社交媒体在美国青少年中普及完成)。
  - 前置摄像头 2010:多个书评/访谈转述书中论点,"The first iPhone with a front-facing camera came out in 2010"【转述,待核】。来源:The Conversation(同上)【媒体】
  - "By 2015, about 80% of those kids had a smartphone with a front-facing camera and a bunch of social media apps"【转述,待核——书中引 Common Sense Media / Pew 口径,Pew 2015 为 73% 的美国 13-17 岁拥有或可用智能手机;80% 与 73% 两个口径并存,见交叉口径问题 #7】。来源:The Conversation(同上)【媒体】

### 论断 4:危机的四个特征(specific, gigantic, sudden, international)

- Senate 证词 Part 1 标题逐字:"THE SPECIFIC, GIGANTIC, SUDDEN, AND INTERNATIONAL MENTAL HEALTH CRISIS"。四个子论断(均逐字自证词):
  - 1.1 "The crisis is specific to mood disorders — those related to depression and anxiety. It is not a general across-the-board increase in other illnesses."
  - 1.2 "The crisis is not a result of changes in the willingness of young people to self-diagnose... We know this because the same trends occurred, at the same time, and in roughly the same magnitudes, in behavioral manifestations of depression and anxiety, including hospital admissions for self-harm, and completed suicides."
  - 1.3 "The crisis came on suddenly, in the early 2010s... They are more like 'hockey sticks,' with a long relatively flat period before the early 2010s, and then a sharp upturn or elbow. This is rare in mental health data."
  - 1.4 "When you compare rates in 2009 –before most teens were daily users of social media—to 2019—the last full year before Covid made things even worse—the increases are generally between 50% and 150%, depending on the disorder, gender, and subgroup."
  - 来源:Senate 证词 PDF pp.2-3(同上)【官方】

## B. 四大基础伤害(four foundational harms)

### 论断 5:phone-based childhood 通过四种机制伤害发育

  - 来源:https://www.anxiousgeneration.com/book 【官方(运动方)】;四大伤害的章节结构见二手摘要(LitCharts/SuperSummary 等)【转述,待核页码】 https://www.litcharts.com/lit/the-anxious-generation/chapter-6
- 佐证数据点(书中引):美国青少年到 2016 年 "more than 83% of adolescents in the U.S. used social media every day"——此数字出现在 Twenge, Martin, & Spitzberg (2019) 并被 Twenge/Haidt 2021 PISA 论文引用(见论断 13 来源 PDF 正文)。【研究】

- Haidt 证词逐字:"The crisis emerged in the exact years when American teens were getting smart phones and becoming daily users of social media platforms such as Instagram."(Senate 证词 p.10)【官方】

## F. 国际同步主张

### 论断 13:PISA 学校孤独感——36/37 国 2012-2018 同步上升(Twenge/Haidt 2021 一手)

一手论文全文已核(PDF)。关键结果:
- 样本:PISA 2000/2003/2012/2015/2018,37 国,n = 1,049,784(51% 女),15-16 岁;6 题 school loneliness 量表(Cronbach α = .82),高孤独 cutpoint = 1-4 量表上 2.22。
- 逐字(摘要):"School loneliness increased 2012–2018 in 36 out of 37 countries. Worldwide, nearly twice as many adolescents in 2018 (vs. 2012) had elevated levels of school loneliness."
- 全球高孤独比例:2012 = 17.12% → 2018 = 30.86%(相对 +80.26%);2000-2018 相对 +91.05%。
- 性别:2012 后女孩增幅更大(相对 +93.41%,d = 0.38)vs 男孩(+66.45%,d = 0.31),Z = 17.93, p < .001。
- 分区域 d(2012-2018):Orthodox 0.53 > Baltic 0.50 > English-speaking 0.45 > Latin America 0.40 > ... > Confucian 0.13(最小;韩国是 37 国中唯一未上升的国家)。
- 组间相关(multi-level,群体层面):smartphone access Std. b = .29***、internet use Std. b = .38**,均在控制年份趋势后仍显著;失业率方向相反(高失业年份孤独更低,Std. b = -.18*);GDP、GINI(收入不平等)、总和生育率(家庭规模)均不显著。综合模型中仅 smartphone access(Std. b = .26)或 internet use(Std. b = .40)+年份显著。
- 结论逐字(含保留):"The psychological well-being of adolescents around the world began to decline after 2012, in conjunction with the rise of smartphone access and increased internet use, though causation cannot be proven and more years of data will provide a more complete picture."
- 讨论中逐字:"Although these analyses cannot prove causation, they demonstrate that loneliness grew among adolescents around the world in tandem with widespread smartphone and internet use."
- 交互发现:初始智能手机渗透率低的国家孤独上升更大;作者解读为群体动力(social norm shift)而非纯个体剂量效应——"solutions to curbing the concerning rise in loneliness and depression among adolescents should focus on group-level solutions, not just individual ones."
- 测量不变性:12 国全不变性(增幅最大,d = 0.43),12 国 configural+metric,13 国未过 configural(含日、韩、法、巴西等)。
  - 来源:Twenge, J. M., Haidt, J., Blake, A. B., McAllister, C., Lemon, H., & Le Roy, A. (2021). "Worldwide increases in adolescent loneliness." *Journal of Adolescence*, 93, 257-269. doi:10.1016/j.adolescence.2021.06.006. PDF: https://district8sonpm.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Adolescent-Loneliness-Twenge-201.pdf 【研究】

## G. 剂量-反应主张

### 论断 16:YRBS 电子设备使用与自杀相关结果(Twenge et al. 2018)

- 一手:美国 9-12 年级 YRBS 2009-2015,"adolescents using devices 5 or more hours a day (vs. 1 hour) were 66% more likely to have at least one suicide-related outcome";5+ 小时组 48% 至少有一项自杀相关结果 vs 1 小时组 29%。证词 Figure 4 同图:横轴 none→5+ hours,纵轴 % yes(depressive symptoms 从 ~25% 升至 ~41%;1+ suicide risk factor 从 ~30% 升至 ~48%,none 组略高于 1/2-1 小时组,曲线呈 J 形)。
  - 构念注意:此处暴露变量是 **electronic device use(广义屏幕时间)**,非社交媒体。
  - 来源:Twenge, Joiner, Rogers, & Martin (2018), *Clinical Psychological Science*, doi:10.1177/2167702617723376 【研究】;Senate 证词 p.6 Figure 4【官方】

### 论断 17:Millennium Cohort(英国)社交媒体小时数与抑郁(Kelly et al. 2019)

- Haidt/Twenge 依 Kelly, Zilanawala, Booker, & Sacker (2019, EClinicalMedicine) Table 2 自制图(证词 Figure 5):"Percent of UK adolescents with 'clinically relevant depressive symptoms' by hours per weekday of social media use, including controls."自图读数:女孩 none ~11% → 5+ hrs ~38%(约 3 倍);男孩 ~7% → ~14.5%(约 2 倍)。
- Haidt 2023-02 表述:女孩从 0 到 5 小时/日,抑郁率 "tripling";男孩 "doubling"。2024-04 版:"Girls using 5+ hours daily: three times as likely to be depressed... [boys] closer to two-to-one."
  - 构念注意:此处是 **social media hours(自报)**+自报抑郁症状量表(短版 Moods and Feelings Questionnaire),非临床诊断。
  - 来源:Kelly et al. (2019), *EClinicalMedicine* 6:59-68【研究】;Senate 证词 p.6【官方】;After Babel(论断 1 两个 URL)【博客/Substack】
- 另一被引数字:"risk of depression increased by 13% for each hour increase on social media"(2024-04 文中引 meta-analysis)【转述,待核出处——疑为 Vidal et al. 或类似 meta 分析】。

### 论断 18:曲线形态主张——阈值/曲线性而非线性

- 证词 2.1 逐字:"Nearly all studies find a correlation, and it is usually curvilinear. That is, moving from no social media use to one or two hours a day is often not associated with an increase in poor mental health, but as usage rises to 3 or 4 hours a day, the increases in mental illness often become quite sharp."(证词 p.5)【官方】——这是 Haidt 反驳"平均线性效应小"的核心口径:他主张看 heavy users,不看全样本线性 β。

## H. 性别差异主张

### 论断 19:女孩受社交媒体伤害显著大于男孩,机制有四

- 书第 6 章("Why Social Media Harms Girls More Than Boys")四个理由【转述自书摘,待核页码】:① visual social comparison and perfectionism(视觉社会比较与完美主义);② relational aggression(关系性攻击,线上化后"persistent and inescapable");③ emotional sharing and contagion(情绪分享与传染,女孩网络中更易扩散);④ predation and harassment(被捕猎与骚扰)。
  - 来源:LitCharts Chapter 6 摘要 https://www.litcharts.com/lit/the-anxious-generation/chapter-6 【媒体/书摘】;After Babel "Social Media is Disproportionately Hurting Girls" https://www.afterbabel.com/p/social-media-conversations-with-daughters 【博客/Substack】
- 相关强度主张(证词 2.4 逐字):"What would the correlation be if we could just look at girls? Several studies have found that it is substantially larger than for boys... I know of no study that has found a larger relationship for boys. A ballpark figure for the correlation just for girls is roughly r = .15 to r = .22."(证词 p.8)【官方】
- 青春期窗口(证词 2.5,引 Orben, Przybylski, Blakemore, & Kievit 2022 Nature Communications):女孩敏感窗口 11-13 岁,男孩 14-15 岁;"For them, the size of the correlation with poor mental health could be well above r = .20."(证词 p.8)【官方;底层为研究】
- 证词 1.5 逐字(限定):"The collapse of mental health has hit both sexes, and on many measures, boys and girls are up by roughly similar percentages. However... A) the base rate for mood disorders is always higher for girls... B) there are some disorders and age groups for which girls are up far more, especially for self-harm."(证词 p.3)【官方】
- 配套:协作文档 "What is Happening to Boys?" 单独处理男孩问题(Haidt 主张男孩路径不同:游戏/色情/退缩,而非社交媒体直接伤害)。【博客/Substack】

## I. 对方法学批评的公开回应

### 论断 20:对 Orben & Przybylski 2019(specification curve,"土豆效应")的回应

- 被回应对象:Orben & Przybylski (2019), "The association between adolescent well-being and digital technology use," *Nature Human Behaviour* 3:173-182——数字技术使用与幸福感的关联"负但极小"(解释方差中位数约 0.4%,β 约 -0.03 至 -0.05 水平),与"吃土豆""戴眼镜"同级;"these effects are too small to warrant policy change"。【研究(批评方)】
- Haidt/Twenge 回应(证词 2.2 逐字):"Orben and Przybylski made 6 analytical choices, each one defensible, that collectively ended up reducing the statistical relationship and obscuring an association that is actually equivalent to a correlation coefficient of around r=.20. The first issue to note is that the 'potatoes' comparison was what they reported for all 'digital media use,' not for social media use specifically... In their own published report, when you zoom in on 'social media' only, the relationship is between 2 and 6 times larger than for 'digital media.' Also crucial is that Orben and Przybylski lumped together all teens (boys and girls), while many studies have found that the correlations with harm are larger for girls."(证词 p.7)【官方】
- 已发表回应论文:Twenge, Haidt, Joiner, & Campbell (2020), "Underestimating digital media harm," *Nature Human Behaviour* 4:346-348。【研究】
- 证词 2.3 逐字(引批评者自己的综述为据):"Amy Orben herself conducted a 'narrative review'... (Orben, 2020). Her own conclusion is that 'The associations between social media use and well-being therefore range from about r = − 0.15 to r = − 0.10.' I agree with this assessment, for both sexes combined."(证词 p.7)【官方;底层 Orben 2020, *Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology* 55:407-414【研究】】

### 论断 21:"r=.15-.20 不算小"的效应量辩护

- 证词 2.6 逐字:"Correlations between .15 and .20 are not 'small.'... The key paper here is Gotz, Gosling, and Rentfrow (2020)... in the domains of public health and education, many of the things that warrant public expenditure are correlated with outcomes in the ballpark of r = .05 to r = .15. For example... the correlation of calcium intake and bone mass in pre-menopausal women is r = .08... The correlation of childhood lead exposure and adult IQ is r = .11, which is enough to justify a national campaign to remove lead from water supplies. These correlations are *smaller* than the links between mood disorders and social media use for girls."并强调 "effects that accumulate over time and at scale"。(证词 p.8)【官方】
- 群体涌现论(2023-04-17 文):"the map is not the territory, and the dataset is not reality";测量误差压低相关;个体剂量-反应只是多条因果通路之一(还有 sensitive period、群体规范改变挤占线下社交)。引 Chris Said 分析:"a correlation of r = .17 could account for a 50% increase in the number of depressed girls in a population."【博客/Substack;Said 分析为博客层级,待核】
  - 来源:Haidt, "Why Some Researchers Think I'm Wrong About Social Media and Mental Illness," After Babel, 2023-04-17. https://www.afterbabel.com/p/why-some-researchers-think-im-wrong 【博客/Substack】

### 论断 22:对 Odgers(Nature 书评,2024-03)的回应

- Odgers 原评论断(被回应,逐字见 Haidt 转引):Haidt "mistaken correlation for causation";"there is no evidence that using these platforms is rewiring children's brains or driving an epidemic of mental illness"。(Odgers, *Nature* 628:29-30, 2024)【研究期刊书评(批评方)】
- Haidt 回应(X + After Babel 2024-04-09):"Both of these assertions are untrue";"Odgers is wrong to say that I have no evidence of causation";列出实验性证据:"22 experimental studies, 16 of which found significant evidence of harm" 与 "nine quasi-experiments or natural experiments... eight of which found evidence of harm",并指 Odgers 只引了其中第 9 个 quasi-experiment。
  - 来源:https://x.com/JonHaidt/status/1774571680511508601 【博客/社媒】;https://www.afterbabel.com/p/phone-based-childhood-cause-epidemic 【博客/Substack】
- 注意实验计数随时间演变:2022-05 证词为 "ten of the studies show a statistically significant effect... just four studies failed to find an effect"(10/14),且 "nearly all of these experiments used college students or older samples; none used middle school students"(证词 p.9,逐字,含自认局限);2023-02 版为 12/18;2024-04 版为 16/22。同一论证的证据库是滚动更新的 Google Doc。

## 交叉口径问题清单(供 L2/L3 与验证轮)

1. **构念混引(本期最大口径险点)**:Haidt 论证链在三种暴露变量间切换——YRBS 图(论断 16)是 *electronic device use*(广义屏幕);Kelly/MCS 图(论断 17)是 *social media hours*;PISA 组间分析(论断 13)是 *smartphone access(有无)+ internet hours*。三者被统一叙述为"social media/phone-based childhood 的剂量-反应",但没有一张图是"社交媒体×临床诊断"。
2. **测量工具口径**:NSDUH 为结构化访谈近似 MDE 诊断;YRBS/MCS 为自报症状量表;自伤为 ED/住院行政记录;PISA 为学校孤独感(非抑郁,论文自己写明 "does not directly assess depressive symptoms",仅与负性情感相关)。Haidt 叙事把孤独感数据当作"心理健康崩塌国际化"的证据,需在引用时保留构念差。
3. **实验研究计数随时间漂移**:10/14(2022-05)→ 12/18(2023-02)→ 16/22 + quasi 8/9(2024-04)。滚动 Google Doc 无版本锁,引用必须带日期;且 2022 证词自认"没有一项实验用初中生样本"。
4. **国际主张的强度演变**:2022 证词:"We do not yet see signs of similar epidemics in continental Europe or in East Asia";2023-24 After Babel/书:北欧、西欧甚至"全球"。同一作者两个时点口径不同;PISA 一手数据里 Confucian 区 d=0.13(最小)、韩国不升反降、13 国未过 configural invariance——"36/37 国"与"全区域同步"的表述强度差需要保留。
5. **"tripling/doubling" 的底数**:Kelly et al. 图为 Haidt/Twenge 自 Table 2 重绘;Kelly 原文的完全调整模型会衰减该梯度;"三倍"是对未完全调整的百分比读数的概括。待与 Kelly 原表核对(L2 线)。
6. **"66% more likely" vs "48% vs 29%"**:同一结果的相对风险表述与绝对百分比表述并存;且该曲线 none 组高于 1 小时组(J 形),"non-users 略差"这一点 Haidt 方少提。
7. **智能手机渗透率数字打架**:"by 2015 ~80% of teens had a smartphone"(书的转述)vs Pew 2015 的 73%(拥有或可及)vs Twenge/Haidt 论文引 "by 2016, more than 83% used social media daily"(构念又不同:拥有手机 ≠ 每日用社交媒体)。三个数字常被混引,需逐一回源。
8. **"+139% anxiety" 的年龄段**:该数是 18-25 岁(年轻成人),常被二手引为"青少年焦虑 +139%"。【已标转述,待核】
9. **自伤 +188%/+48% 的年龄段与年份窗**:二手转述未给清年龄段(疑为 10-14 岁女孩,2010-2020,CDC ED 数据);"10-14 岁女孩自杀 +167%"本轮未取到一手。两者都待 CDC WONDER/NEISS 核对。
10. **PISA 论文的自我设限 vs 传播口径**:论文两处逐字声明 "causation cannot be proven",且发现失业率与孤独负相关(与经济解释相反)被 Haidt 用作排除性论据——但同一逻辑下 GDP/GINI 不显著也约束了"富裕个人主义国家更重"叙事的数据基础(那是 After Babel 层面的观察,不在该论文统计中)。
11. **效应量单位混战**:β(specification curve,全屏幕、双性别合并)vs r(社交媒体×女孩,.15-.22)vs OR/RR(66% more likely)vs 人群归因推算(r=.17 → 抑郁女孩 +50%,Chris Said 博客层级)。正反双方经常拿不同单位对轰;引用任何一方数字必须带口径。
12. **"13% per hour" meta 分析出处未核**;"Instagram 10M→90M" 用户数未回源;书内"four norms"与官网"four norms"措辞有微差("phone-free schools" vs "phone-free schools, from bell to bell")。
