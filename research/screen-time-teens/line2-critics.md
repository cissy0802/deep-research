## A. Candice Odgers 的 Nature 书评及其与 Haidt 的交锋

### 论断 A1:Odgers 书评的核心判词——「不被科学支持」
逐字原文(书评开篇):
> "Two things need to be said after reading The Anxious Generation. First, this book is going to sell a lot of copies, because Jonathan Haidt is telling a scary story about children's development that many parents are primed to believe. Second, the book's repeated suggestion that digital technologies are rewiring our children's brains and causing an epidemic of mental illness is not supported by science."

- 作者/出处:Candice L. Odgers, "The great rewiring: is social media really behind an epidemic of teenage mental illness?", Nature 628, 29–30, 2024-03-29(书评)【媒体】(Nature 的 Books & Arts 栏目,非同行评审)
- URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-024-00902-2
- 注:另一次抓取返回了变体 "Two things can be independently true about social media...",判断为抽取模型混淆;上面版本有多个二手来源逐字佐证(含 Haidt 方引用),采信上面版本。

### 论断 A2:因果方向——是心理问题预测使用,不是使用预测心理问题
逐字原文:
> "When associations over time are found, they suggest not that social-media use predicts or causes depression, but that young people who already have mental-health problems use such platforms more often or in different ways from their healthy peers."

另:
> "Hundreds of researchers, myself included, have searched for the kind of large effects suggested by Haidt. Our efforts have produced a mix of no, small and mixed associations. Most data are correlative."

- 出处同 A1。URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-024-00902-2

### 论断 A3:「没有证据表明平台在重塑大脑」+ 对趋势图方法的讥评
逐字原文:
> "…there is no evidence that using these platforms is rewiring children's brains or driving an epidemic of mental illness."(此句经 Haidt 本人在 X 上逐字引用并反驳,可视为已核)
> "Plots presented throughout this book will be useful in teaching my students the fundamentals of causal inference, and how to avoid making up stories by simply looking at trend lines."
- 出处同 A1;Haidt 引用见 https://x.com/JonHaidt/status/1774571680511508601

### 论断 A4:Odgers 的替代解释(共同原因假说)

## B. Orben & Przybylski 2019 NHB(specification curve)及后续争论

### 论断 B1:数字技术使用最多解释青少年幸福感方差的 0.4%
摘要逐字:
> "The association we find between digital technology use and adolescent well-being is negative but small, explaining at most 0.4% of the variation in well-being. Taking the broader context of the data into account suggests that these effects are too small to warrant policy change."
- 三个数据集(方法节逐字):"three nationally-representative datasets from the US and the UK…encompassing a total of 355,358, predominately 12 to 18-year-old, adolescents surveyed between the years of 2007 and 2016" —— 即 YRBS(美)、Monitoring the Future(美)、Millennium Cohort Study(英)。
- 每数据集中位效应(AAM 原文数字):YRBS median β = −.034 (partial η² = .001, median n = 61,525);MTF median β 约 −.005~−.03 区间随规格变化;MCS(含自报+母报测量的技术使用)median β = −.032 (median partial η² = .004);median standard error 多为 .004。总规格数:MCS 侧达 20,004 个规格;Odgers & Jensen 2020 转述其总分析数为 3,221,225,472 组合。
- 作者/出处:Amy Orben & Andrew K. Przybylski, "The association between adolescent well-being and digital technology use", Nature Human Behaviour 3, 173–182, 2019【研究】
- URL(期刊): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-018-0506-1 ;AAM 全文: https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:5d844350-a359-47d3-b10c-4bfb93ce613b

### 论断 B2:「吃土豆 / 戴眼镜」对比的原始表述
讨论节逐字(AAM):
> "…in all three datasets the effects of both smoking marijuana and bullying have much larger negative associations with adolescent well-being (2.7x and 4.3x respectively for YRBS) than technology use does. Positive antecedents of well-being are equally illustrative; simple actions like getting enough sleep and regularly eating breakfast have much more positive associations with well-being than the average impact of technology use (ranging from 1.7x to 44.2x more positive in all datasets). Neutral factors provide perhaps the most useful context to judge technology engagement effects: the association of well-being with regularly eating potatoes was nearly as negative as the association with technology use (0.9x, YRBS) and wearing glasses was more negatively associated with well-being (1.5x, MCS)."
- 出处同 B1。注意:土豆对比出自 YRBS、眼镜对比出自 MCS,并非同一数据集;媒体常并列转述而不注明。

### 论断 B3:Twenge/Haidt 方的 reanalysis 反击(2020)
- Jean M. Twenge, Jonathan Haidt, Thomas E. Joiner & W. Keith Campbell, "Underestimating digital media harm", Nature Human Behaviour 4, 346–348, 2020【研究】(Matters Arising 评论)
- URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-020-0839-4
- 核心主张(转述+部分逐字):O&P 做出了"six analytical decisions that resulted in lower effect sizes"(六项拉低效应量的分析决策,如把看电视等混入"digital technology use"、把男女合并、用连续量而非高低使用组对比);其 Figure 1 展示 MCS 中"Percentage of the MCS cohort with clinically relevant depressive symptoms as a function of social media use";结论性表述:小效应结论"in stark contrast with the practically important differences identified in other analyses of the same datasets, especially for social media use among girls."
- 【转述,待核】Twenge/Haidt 方在多处主张:构念缩窄到「女孩 × social media(非泛屏幕)」时,MCS 中相关可达 r ≈ .17–.20;本次未能从该 NHB 评论一手页核到确切 r 值(页面仅部分可及)。引用时需回到 Kelly et al. 2018 EClinicalMedicine 或 Twenge & Martin 2020 原文核数。
- Orben & Przybylski 的答复:"Reply to: Underestimating digital media harm", Nature Human Behaviour 4, 349–351, 2020【研究】 https://www.nature.com/articles/s41562-020-0840-y 。答复称多数质疑已在原文补充材料处理,并用附加分析反驳其余质疑(一手页仅部分可及,具体反驳数字未核出,引用其具体数值前需补核)。

## C. Orben 的综述与 within-person 纵向证据

### 论断 C1:Orben 2020「综述的综述」——平均关联为负但很小
- Amy Orben, "Teenagers, screens and social media: a narrative review of reviews and key studies", Social Psychiatry and Psychiatric Epidemiology 55, 407–414, 2020【研究】
- URL: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00127-019-01825-4
- 逐字:"the association between digital technology use, or social media use in particular, and psychological well-being is—on average—negative but very small";"associations ranging from about r = − 0.15 to r = − 0.10";"the correlations fall to about r = − 0.10 to r = − 0.05 in some work lauded as being more transparent";"the research field is dominated by cross-sectional work that is generally of a low quality standard."
- 覆盖 80+ 篇系统综述/元分析。换算口径:r = −0.10~−0.15 ≈ 解释方差 1%–2.3%。

### 论断 C2:PNAS 2019 within-person 双向效应——两个方向都「trivial」
- Amy Orben, Tobias Dienlin & Andrew K. Przybylski, "Social media's enduring effect on adolescent life satisfaction", PNAS 116(21), 10226–10228, 2019【研究】
- URL: https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1902058116 (全文 PDF 镜像: https://www.teachertoolkit.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/1902058116.full_.pdf )
- 设计:Understanding Society(UK Household Longitudinal Study)2009–2016 八波,n = 12,672 名 10–15 岁青少年;random-intercept cross-lagged panel models + specification curve 框架。
- 摘要逐字:"We found that social media use is not, in and of itself, a strong predictor of life satisfaction across the adolescent population. Instead, social media effects are nuanced, small at best, reciprocal over time, gender specific, and contingent on analytic methods."
- 效应量逐字:"Both median longitudinal effects were trivial in size (social media predicting life satisfaction, β = −0.05; life satisfaction predicting social media use, β = −0.02)." 分性别:女孩 social media→生活满意度 "b = −0.13 to −0.05 or β = −0.09 to −0.04"(除对外貌满意度外各域);女孩满意度→使用 "b = −0.17 to −0.05 or β = −0.11 to −0.07";男孩仅 "tenuous decreases"(b = −0.08 to −0.04 / β = −0.07 to −0.05,少数规格显著)。
- 原文亦自我设限逐字:"the yearly interval between measurements in these data might not be optimal for understanding reciprocal social media effects over time."

## D. Przybylski & Weinstein 2017 digital Goldilocks 假说

### 论断 D1:适度使用与幸福感无可检出的负关联,曲线呈倒 U(二次函数)
- Andrew K. Przybylski & Netta Weinstein, "A Large-Scale Test of the Goldilocks Hypothesis: Quantifying the Relations Between Digital-Screen Use and the Mental Well-Being of Adolescents", Psychological Science 28(2), 204–215, 2017【研究】(预注册分析)
- URL: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0956797616678438
- 样本:n = 120,115 英格兰 15 岁青少年;幸福感用 Warwick-Edinburgh Mental Well-being Scale(14 题)。
- 摘要逐字:"data from a representative sample of English adolescents (n = 120,115) provided evidence that the links between digital screen time and mental well-being follow quadratic functions."

## E. Ferguson 的元分析

### 论断 E1:2021 屏幕媒体×心理健康 meta——合并 r+ = .052
- Christopher J. Ferguson, Linda K. Kaye, Dawn Branley-Bell, Patrick Markey, James D. Ivory, Dana Klisanin, Malte Elson 等, "Like This Meta-Analysis: Screen Media and Mental Health", Professional Psychology: Research and Practice, 2021 (© 2021 APA), doi:10.1037/pro0000426【研究】
- URL(全文 PDF): https://gwern.net/doc/psychiatry/depression/2021-ferguson-2.pdf
- 摘要逐字:"The current meta-analysis included 37 effect sizes from 33 separate studies. To consider the most recent research, all studies analyzed were published between 2015 and 2019. Across studies, evidence suggests that screen media plays little role in mental health concerns."
- Table 1 确切数字:全部研究 k=37, r+ = .052 [95% CI .036, .068], I²=88.4;分型:general screens r=.059 (k=20)、smartphones r=.041 (k=11)、social media/internet r=.043 (k=6);correlational r=.051 (k=25) vs longitudinal r=.055 (k=12)("Longitudinal studies did not provide any more evidence for effects than correlational studies");判定阈值 r=.10(低于则视为无假设支持价值);Tandem Procedure 未见强出版偏倚。

### 论断 E2:2024 社交媒体×青少年内化障碍 meta——β = .061,标题即结论
- Christopher J. Ferguson, Linda K. Kaye, Dawn Branley-Bell & Patrick Markey, "There Is No Evidence That Time Spent on Social Media Is Correlated With Adolescent Mental Health Problems: Findings From a Meta-analysis", Professional Psychology: Research and Practice, © 2024 APA, doi:10.1037/pro0000589【研究】
- URL(校样 PDF): https://christopherjferguson.com/Social%20Media%20Meta.pdf
- 确切数字(Table 1 与正文):46 项研究、79 个效应量,合并 β = .061 [95% CI .047, .075], I²=98.8;女性 β=.075 [.050,.101] vs 男性 β=.044 [.025,.062];correlational β=.072 vs longitudinal β=.044(moderator p=.067 不显著);bespoke 数据集 β=.041 < 全国性调查数据集 β=.067 (p=.043)。
- 结论逐字:"the observed effect size for the relationship between social media use and adolescent mental health is below the evidentiary threshold and is as likely due to methodological noise as any actual effect."
- 方法学盘点:预注册仅 5%、干扰项 0%、多源报告 19%;引用偏倚(citation bias)存在于 15% 效应量。

### 论断 E3:2024 社交媒体减用/戒断实验 meta——d = .088,与零无显著差异;随即被强烈质疑
- Christopher J. Ferguson, "Do social media experiments prove a link with mental health: A methodological and meta-analytic review", Psychology of Popular Media, 2024(在线)【研究】;纳入 27 项实验研究(25 发表 + 2 学位论文)。
- 关键数字【转述,待核——取自批评方 After Babel 与 PMC 综述的引用,本次未从一手 PDF 核出】:合并 d = .088, 95% CI [−0.018, +0.197],结论为效应与零无统计学差异。
- 争论:Haidt/Rausch 三连发批评 "The Fundamental Flaws of The Only Meta-Analysis of Social Media Reduction Experiments" Part 1–3, After Babel, 2025【博客/Substack】 https://www.afterbabel.com/p/the-case-for-causality-part-1 (指其把满意度/孤独感/抑郁等异质 DV 硬合并、效应量提取与样本量有错、错误均偏向"无效应");Johns Hopkins 团队 reanalysis "Social Media Reduction or Abstinence Interventions Are Providing Mental Health Benefits—Reanalysis of a Published Meta-Analysis", Psychology of Popular Media, 2025【研究】 https://pure.johnshopkins.edu/en/publications/social-media-reduction-or-abstinence-interventions-are-providing- (加入干预时长 moderator 后称减用干预有益)。另有独立 meta 得相反结论:"Reducing Social Media Use Decreases Depression Symptoms: A Meta-Analysis of Randomised Controlled Trials"【研究】 https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12651081/ 。此战线目前双方各有 meta,未定论。

## F. 其他重量级批评

### 论断 F1:Odgers & Jensen 2020 JCPP 年度研究综述——结论逐字
- Candice L. Odgers & Michaeline R. Jensen, "Annual Research Review: Adolescent mental health in the digital age: facts, fears, and future directions", Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry 61(3), 336–348, 2020【研究】
- URL: https://acamh.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jcpp.13190 (全文镜像: https://www.cs.virginia.edu/~rmw7my/Teaching/odgers.pdf )
- 摘要结论逐字:"The most recent and rigorous large-scale preregistered studies report small associations between the amount of daily digital technology usage and adolescents' well-being that do not offer a way of distinguishing cause from effect and, as estimated, are unlikely to be of clinical or practical significance."
- 正文逐字(证据基础小结):"…and meta-analytic work do not support causal claims, or even strong and consistent correlational patterns, linking adolescents' digital technology usage with mental health problems."

- 低收入外溢逐字:"youth from low-income families tend to report more negative spillover of negative experiences on social media to offline conflict, fights, and trouble at school."

### 论断 F2:Vuorre & Przybylski 2023——72 国 Facebook 渗透与幸福感,未见广泛伤害
- Matti Vuorre & Andrew K. Przybylski, "Estimating the association between Facebook adoption and well-being in 72 countries", Royal Society Open Science 10(8): 221451, 2023【研究】
- URL: https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rsos.221451 (PMC 全文: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10410217/ )
- 数据:Gallup World Poll 946,798 人 × 72 国 × 2008–2019,对接 Facebook 内部提供的分国家渗透/活跃用户数据。
- 摘要逐字:"We found no evidence suggesting that the global penetration of social media is associated with widespread psychological harm: Facebook adoption predicted life satisfaction and positive experiences positively, and negative experiences negatively, both between countries and within countries over time."
- 国家内(within-country)数字:DAU 每 +1%,生活满意度 +0.01 [−0.02, 0.04] 个百分点、积极体验 +0.03 [0.00, 0.06]、消极体验 −0.03 [−0.07, 0.00];年轻组(13–34)关联比 35+ 组更偏正;性别差异不显著。注意:关联小,且部分未过其 97.5% 单侧可信度阈值。
- 口径警告:这是国家层渗透率×国家层均值的生态学关联,不是个体使用量,不能与个体层相关(r)直接比较,也不能直接证伪个体层因果。

### 论断 F3:Laurence Steinberg——「没人真的知道」
- Laurence Steinberg, "Does Instagram Harm Girls? No One Actually Knows.", The New York Times (Opinion), 2021-10【媒体】
- URL: https://www.nytimes.com/2021/10/10/opinion/instagram-teen-girls-mental-health.html (核引自镜像/引用页: http://dangerouslyirrelevant.org/2021/10/instagram-adolescent-girls-and-the-importance-of-evidence.html )

## G. 对 Haidt 趋势论证的具体反驳(诊断/求助行为与共同原因)

### 论断 G1:美国青少年「自杀相关就诊暴涨」很大程度是报告与编码变化
- Adriana Corredor-Waldron & Janet Currie, "To What Extent Are Trends in Teen Mental Health Driven by Changes in Reporting? The Example of Suicide-Related Hospital Visits", Journal of Human Resources 59(S), S14–S40, 2024【研究】
- URL: https://jhr.uwpress.org/content/59/S/S14
- 关键发现(转述,含引语碎片):用新泽西 2008–2019 医院数据,自伤/自杀未遂/自杀完成率 "were essentially flat over this period";就诊数两个跳升点分别对应 2011 年抑郁筛查建议(叠加 ACA 保险覆盖)与 2016 年 10 月「要求把自杀意念记为次级诊断」的编码指令修订;涨幅 "mainly accounted for by a sharp increase in diagnoses of suicidal ideation" 而非未遂/完成。
- 对 Haidt 的含义:趋势线中相当一部分「恶化」可由识别/编码增强解释——这是批评方对「2012 后陡升」图形证据的最强反驳之一。

### 论断 G2:ICD-9→ICD-10 转换(2015-10)造成自伤编码断点
- "Changes in Coding of Suicide Attempts or Self-Harm With Transition From ICD-9 to ICD-10", Psychiatric Services 68(3), 2017【研究】
- URL: https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ps.201600450
- 要点(转述):2015 年秋编码意图(intent)记录方式剧变 "almost certainly represent artifacts of coding changes rather than true changes in suicidal behavior";ICD-10-CM 把自伤并入主诊断的必填外因码,"undetermined intent" 类就诊减少、编码为自伤的就诊增加(该文报告总体自伤就诊量在转换点前后大体不变,但构成剧变),提示解读 2015 前后趋势必须考虑该断点。

### 论断 G3:Odgers 的「低收入家庭青少年」论点与「新数字鸿沟」
- Candice L. Odgers, "Smartphones are bad for some teens, not all", Nature 554, 432–434, 2018-02(Comment)【媒体】(Nature 评论栏目)
- URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-018-02109-8
- 逐字:"Young people who are already struggling offline might experience greater negative effects of life online."
- 转述:平均效应掩盖异质性;新数字鸿沟不在「有没有设备」而在使用方式与线下资源;低收入/已有心理困难的青少年承受被放大的风险,而多数青少年无害甚至受益。此文是 Odgers 2024 书评中「别把注意力从真正的风险因素上引开」论点的前身。

### 论断 G4:共同原因假说的清单(批评方版本)
- 汇总自 A4(Odgers 2024)、F1(Odgers & Jensen 2020)、C1(Orben 2020):枪支暴露、结构性歧视与种族主义、性别歧视与性侵、阿片类流行、经济困难(2008 大衰退后效,尤其收入分布底部 20% 家庭)、社会隔离、学业压力与求助行为常态化。注意:批评方自己也未给出这些替代因子的因果证据,Haidt 方对此的反驳见 A6(时间、跨国、收入梯度三点)。

## 交叉口径问题(可疑数字 / 矛盾口径 / 待验证点)

1. 构念混用是本战场核心:Orben & Przybylski 2019 测的是宽口径 "digital technology use"(含看电视);Twenge/Haidt 方主张缩到 "social media × 女孩" 才是正确构念。0.4% 方差(η²,≈r .06)与 Haidt 方常引的 r≈.17(女孩×社交媒体)并不互斥——是同一批数据在不同构念/子群/统计量下的读数。引用任何一个数字必须带构念+子群+统计量三件套。
2. 「土豆/眼镜」对比的精确口径:土豆 0.9x 来自 YRBS,眼镜 1.5x 来自 MCS,不是同一数据集;且对比的是中位规格的 β,不是所有规格。媒体转述常删掉数据集标注。
3. 「the kids are not all right」归属:不在 Odgers 的 Nature 书评原文;是 Clare Morell(支持 Haidt 方)书评的标题。若上游把它当 Odgers 名言用,属于误归【伪引嫌疑】。
4. Odgers 书评开篇引语存在两个抓取版本("Two things need to be said…" vs "Two things can be independently true…"),已采信前者(多方逐字佐证);终稿引用前建议再对 Nature 原文核一次。
5. Twenge et al. 2020 NHB 评论中的具体 r 值(女孩×社交媒体 r≈.17–.20)与「重度 vs 不使用者抑郁比例翻倍」的具体百分数:本次仅二手可及,【转述,待核】,应回 Kelly et al. 2018 / Twenge & Martin 2020 原文核。
6. Ferguson 2024 实验 meta 的 d = .088 [−0.018, 0.197]:数字取自批评方转引【转述,待核】;且该 meta 本身被指效应量提取错误(Johns Hopkins reanalysis 得出相反结论)。RCT-meta 战线目前是「一正一负一中」三份 meta 并存,不能只引一份。
7. Ferguson 两份相关性 meta(r+=.052 与 β=.061)与 Orben 2020 的 r=−0.10~−0.15 表面矛盾:纳入年份(2015–2019 vs 更早)、纳入标准、outcome 定义(内化障碍 vs 广义 well-being)不同;方向符号也只是报告习惯差(正负号代表"更多使用×更差健康"同一方向)。不可直接横比。
8. Vuorre & Przybylski 2023 是国家层生态学设计(Facebook 渗透率),不是个体屏幕时间;它能反驳「社交媒体普及必然伴随人群幸福感下滑」的宏观叙事,但不能反驳个体层剂量-反应主张。引用时勿降级为「研究证明 Facebook 无害」。
9. Goldilocks 拐点(1h57m 手机等)常被误读为「建议使用时长」;原文仅是二次曲线顶点,且周末拐点更高;其「高使用负关联 < 1/3 早餐/睡眠正关联」与 O&P 2019 的「1.7x–44.2x」是不同数据集不同测量,不能拼成同一句话。
10. 趋势反驳的时间线要对齐:JHR 论文的两个跳点是 2011(筛查建议)与 2016-10(编码指令),ICD-10 转换是 2015-10;三者都不等于 2012——批评方用它解释「陡升」时,与 Haidt 主张的 2012 拐点存在 1–4 年错位,这本身是双方都该交代的口径问题。
11. Odgers & Jensen 2020 转述 O&P 分析总数为 "3,221,225,472 analyses",原文正文写 "N > 350,0000"(排版错误,应为 350,000+);转引其样本数时用 355,358。
12. Steinberg 的 NYT 逐字引语经第三方引用页核对,直接引用前建议过一遍 NYT 原文(付费墙)。
